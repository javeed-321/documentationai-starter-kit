## Navigating the workspace

The workspace sidebar lists every space and page you have permission to see. Click any item to open it, or use the search bar at the top to jump directly to a known page title.

Expand parent pages to reveal nested content. Drag pages in the sidebar to reorder them when you hold edit rights.

## Finding and editing pages

Use the global search field to locate pages by title or keyword. Once a page is open, click the edit button to modify its content or add new blocks.

Changes save automatically. Restore earlier versions from the page menu if you need to revert an edit.

## Requesting access to team spaces

Open the space settings from the sidebar menu and select the request access option. Provide a short reason so the space owner can approve your request.

Owners review requests within one business day and grant the minimum permission level required for the task.
