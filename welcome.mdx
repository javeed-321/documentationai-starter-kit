## Overview

This workspace serves as the central internal knowledge base for the company. Teams document processes, store reference materials, and share updates that remain accessible to coworkers. Content is organized in a hierarchy of pages and databases that supports both individual work and cross-team visibility.

<Callout kind="tip">
Access controls can restrict visibility of pages and databases to specific teams or individuals. Check sharing settings before adding sensitive information.
</Callout>

## Navigating the Workspace Hierarchy

Workspaces contain top-level pages that belong to teams or departments. Each page can hold nested subpages that create a tree structure. Use the sidebar to expand sections and locate content quickly. Search at the top of the workspace returns results across all pages you have permission to view.

## Creating and Organizing Pages

Create a new page from the sidebar or by typing the slash command inside an existing page. Give the page a clear title and add a parent page to keep related material together. Use inline headings, bullet lists, and toggles to break content into readable sections. Move pages by dragging them in the sidebar or by updating the parent property.

## Using Databases for Team Knowledge

Databases store structured records such as meeting notes, project trackers, or policy lists. Add properties like status, owner, or due date to each row. Filter and sort views so team members see only the entries that matter to them. Link database rows to other pages to connect related information without duplication.

## Permissions and Sharing Settings

Every page and database carries its own sharing settings. Set access to "Team" to limit visibility to a specific group or choose "Anyone at company" for broader reach. Invite individuals by email when finer control is required. Review the share menu regularly to remove former team members who no longer need access.
