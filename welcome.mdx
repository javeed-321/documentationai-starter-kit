## Welcome

This knowledge base helps your team use Notion for shared notes, project tracking, and automated workflows. You can navigate the workspace, configure AI agents, and maintain a central knowledge base without switching tools.

Access to pages and databases here follows your workspace permissions. Team members see only the sections they have rights to view or edit.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Workspace owners can restrict this knowledge base to specific team members or groups through page permissions and sharing settings.
</Callout>
