You want to extend Zendesk with tools your team already uses. This page shows how to browse the Marketplace, install apps, and keep data flowing across systems.

## Installing an App

Follow these steps to add an app from the Marketplace.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Marketplace">

  Log in to your Zendesk account.

  Go to the Admin Center and select Marketplace.

</Step>
<Step title="Find the app">

  Use the search bar or browse categories.

  Click the app you want to review its details and permissions.

</Step>
<Step title="Install the app">

  Click Install.

  Choose the account or instance where the app should run.

  Review the requested permissions, then confirm.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
After installation, test the app in a sandbox account before rolling it out to your live environment.
</Callout>

## Actions and Integrations

Apps can trigger actions inside Zendesk or pull data from external systems. Common actions include:

- Creating or updating tickets automatically
- Sending notifications to external tools
- Syncing customer data in real time

Check the app's documentation inside the Marketplace listing to see which actions it supports.

## Partner Ecosystem

Over 1,800 partners build apps for Zendesk. These partners range from large software vendors to specialized developers. Each app lists its partner name, support contact, and update history so you can evaluate reliability before installing.

## Unified Data Flows

When you connect apps, Zendesk keeps ticket, customer, and agent data consistent across channels. Data flows in both directions:

| Direction       | Example                                      |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Zendesk to app  | Ticket updates sent to a CRM                 |
| App to Zendesk  | New orders from an e-commerce platform create tickets |

Review the data-mapping settings in each app to control exactly what information moves.
