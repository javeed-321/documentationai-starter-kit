Connect your Shopify store to Mailchimp to sync products, orders, and customers automatically. This page shows you how to set up the connection and start using the synced data.

## Connect your store

Follow these steps to link your Shopify store.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the integration page">
    Log in to Mailchimp. Go to the Integrations section and search for Shopify.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the connection">
    Click Connect. Enter your Shopify store URL and approve the permissions Mailchimp requests.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the link">
    Return to Mailchimp. You should see a success message and your store listed under connected stores.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Make sure you have admin access in Shopify before you start. This prevents permission errors during setup.

</Callout>

## Sync products, orders, and customers

Once connected, Mailchimp pulls your store data every few hours. You can also trigger a manual sync.

- Products appear in your Mailchimp product library.
- Orders and customers show up in the audience associated with the store.
- New orders update customer purchase history automatically.

To force a sync, go to the store settings and click Sync now.

## Set up ecommerce automations

Use synced data to create targeted campaigns.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an automation">
    Go to Automations and choose an ecommerce template such as Abandoned Cart or Product Follow-Up.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your store">
    Pick the connected Shopify store as the data source.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize and turn on">
    Edit the email content, set timing rules, and activate the automation.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="How long does the first sync take?">
  The initial sync usually finishes within 30 minutes for stores with fewer than 10,000 products. Larger stores may take up to a few hours.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I sync only some products?">
  Yes. In the store settings, choose specific collections or use tags to limit what Mailchimp imports.

</Expandable>
