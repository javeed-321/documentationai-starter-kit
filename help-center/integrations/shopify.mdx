## Connect your Shopify store

The Shopify integration pulls products, orders, and customer data into Mailchimp so you can run targeted campaigns and track revenue. Follow the steps below to connect your store.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Log in to your Mailchimp account and go to the Integrations section. Search for Shopify and click Connect.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize access">
    Enter your Shopify store URL when prompted. Review the requested permissions and click Allow to grant Mailchimp access to your products and orders.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose sync settings">
    Select which data types to sync: products, customers, and orders. Choose whether to import historical data or start from today.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## What syncs automatically

Once connected, Mailchimp keeps the following data up to date:

| Data type   | Details                                      | Update frequency |
|-------------|----------------------------------------------|------------------|
| Products    | Titles, prices, images, and variants         | Every 30 minutes |
| Orders      | Purchase details and order status            | Real time        |
| Customers   | Email, name, and purchase history            | Every 30 minutes |

## Use product recommendations

After the first sync completes, add product recommendations to your emails. Open any campaign, click the product block, and choose items based on best sellers or abandoned carts.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Recommendations perform best when you have at least 50 orders in the last 30 days.
</Callout>

## Track revenue

Revenue tracking shows how much each campaign earns from Shopify orders. View these numbers on the Reports page for any email or automation.

## Common questions

<Expandable title="How long does the first sync take?" default-open="false">
  The initial import usually finishes in under 15 minutes for stores with fewer than 5,000 products. Larger catalogs may take up to an hour.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I disconnect the integration later?" default-open="false">
  Yes. Go to Integrations, find Shopify, and click Disconnect. Your Mailchimp audience data stays intact.

</Expandable>

Still stuck? Contact Mailchimp support through the in-app chat or help form.
