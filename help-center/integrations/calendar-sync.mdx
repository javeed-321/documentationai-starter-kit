Calendly cannot detect your availability until you connect at least one calendar. This guide shows how to link Google, Outlook, iCloud and other calendars, enable multi-calendar sync, and turn on conflict detection.

## Connect your first calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open calendar settings">
    In Calendly, go to Account Settings and select Calendar Connections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your provider">
    Click Connect next to Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, iCloud, or Exchange.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and grant access">
    Follow the prompts to sign in. Calendly requests read-only access to events and free/busy information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the calendars to sync">
    Choose up to six calendars. Calendly checks all selected calendars for conflicts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  You must grant Calendly permission to read events. Without this scope, conflict detection will not work.
</Callout>

## Permission scopes explained

Calendly asks for the smallest set of permissions needed:

| Provider       | Required scope                  | What it allows                              |
|----------------|---------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Google         | See and download calendar events| Read events to find free time               |
| Outlook        | Read calendars                  | View availability and event details         |
| iCloud         | Read calendars                  | Check busy times only                       |
| Exchange       | Read free/busy                  | Detect conflicts without full event access  |

## Turn on conflict detection

After you connect calendars, conflict detection runs automatically. To confirm it is active:

1. Go to your event type.
2. Open the Availability section.
3. Make sure "Check for conflicts" is toggled on.

Calendly now blocks times that appear busy on any connected calendar.

## Multi-calendar sync limits

- You can connect up to six calendars per user.
- All connected calendars are checked together for conflicts.
- Changes made in Calendly appear only on the primary calendar you choose during setup.

## Common questions

<Expandable title="Why does Calendly need read access to my calendar?">
  Calendly reads your events to know when you are busy. It never creates or edits events without your explicit approval.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What happens if two calendars show the same meeting?">
  Calendly treats overlapping events as a single conflict and blocks the time slot.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I sync more than six calendars?">
  No. The limit is six per user. Remove an unused calendar before adding another.
</Expandable>
