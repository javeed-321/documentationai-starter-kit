---
title: Training Fin
description: Teach Fin your brand voice, policies, and knowledge base so it resolves tickets accurately.
---

If Fin gives inaccurate or off-brand answers, connect your knowledge base, add custom instructions, and tune resolution settings to improve accuracy.

## Sync your knowledge base

Connect your existing help articles and documents so Fin can reference them when answering tickets.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fin settings">
    Go to Fin in your workspace and select Training.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect sources">
    Choose the sources you want to sync. Intercom pulls content automatically.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish">
    Check the imported articles for accuracy, then publish the sync.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Sync only the sources your team keeps up to date. Outdated content lowers resolution quality.
</Callout>

## Add custom instructions

Custom instructions tell Fin how to speak and what rules to follow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Custom instructions">
    In Training, select Custom instructions.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your guidelines">
    Add short, clear rules such as "Always use friendly language" or "Escalate refunds over $50."

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Save the instructions, then test Fin with a sample ticket.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Optimize resolution rate

Track how often Fin resolves tickets without human help and adjust settings.

| Setting | Effect | When to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confidence threshold | Raises the bar before Fin answers | High-volume teams that want fewer mistakes |
| Auto-escalate | Sends hard tickets to agents faster | Teams that prioritize quick handoff |
| Tone examples | Shows Fin sample replies | Brands with strict voice guidelines |

<Expandable title="How often should I retrain Fin?">
  Review training data monthly or after any major policy change. Frequent small updates keep resolution rates high.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What if Fin still gives wrong answers?">
  Check that your knowledge base articles are current and that custom instructions are specific. Test with recent tickets to spot gaps.
</Expandable>
