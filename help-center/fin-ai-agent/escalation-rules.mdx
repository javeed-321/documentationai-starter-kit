When Fin reaches the limit of what it can handle, it hands the conversation to a human agent. This page shows how to set the triggers, pass along full context, and measure satisfaction after the hand-off.

## Set hand-off triggers

Define the exact conditions that cause Fin to escalate.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the rules editor">
    In the Fin settings, go to Escalation rules and click New rule.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose trigger conditions">
    Select one or more conditions from the list below.

    | Condition              | What it means                              |
    |------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
    | Unresolved after N turns | Conversation reaches a set number of replies |
    | Specific intent detected | User asks about billing, refunds, or legal issues |
    | Sentiment drops         | User expresses frustration or anger        |
    | Custom keyword          | Message contains words you list            |

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Click Save, then use the test chat to confirm the rule fires.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Start with one or two triggers. Too many rules can cause unnecessary hand-offs.

</Callout>

## Share context with your team

Fin automatically includes the full conversation history and any data it collected.

To add extra details:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Attach custom attributes">
    In the rule editor, turn on Include custom attributes and choose the fields your team needs.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a summary note">
    Toggle Add AI summary. Fin writes a short note that appears at the top of the ticket.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track satisfaction after hand-off

Measure how well the hand-off worked.

1. Enable post-escalation surveys in Settings > Satisfaction.
2. Choose the survey that appears once a human takes over.
3. Review results in the Escalation reports dashboard.

## Common questions

<Expandable title="Can I stop Fin from escalating a certain topic?">
  Yes. Create a rule with the condition "Intent equals X" and set the action to "Stay with Fin."

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How long does context sharing take?">
  Context is sent instantly when the hand-off occurs.

</Expandable>
