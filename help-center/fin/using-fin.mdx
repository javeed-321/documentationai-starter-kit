Fin can miss context on tricky support requests. This page explains how Fin processes conversations, learns from outcomes, and surfaces insights so you can guide its improvement.

## Conversation flow

Fin follows a clear sequence on every incoming message.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive and classify">
    Fin reads the message, checks your knowledge base, and decides whether to answer or hand off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft and verify">
    Fin writes a reply, confirms facts against your content, and adds sources when available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or escalate">
    Fin sends the reply or routes the conversation to a human teammate with a summary.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Self-improving responses

Fin gets better each time a teammate edits or resolves a conversation.

- Review Fin's replies in the conversation timeline.
- Edit any answer that needs correction.
- Mark the conversation resolved once the issue is closed.

Fin records the change and applies the updated wording to similar questions in the future.

<Callout kind="tip">
  The more edits you make in the first week, the faster Fin aligns with your tone and policies.
</Callout>

## AI insights

Open the Insights tab to see where Fin needs help.

| Insight | What it shows | Action you can take |
|---------|---------------|---------------------|
| Resolution rate | Percentage of conversations Fin solved alone | Add missing articles for low-rate topics |
| Hand-off reasons | Common triggers for escalation | Update Fin's rules or knowledge base |
| Suggested improvements | Specific reply edits teammates made | Accept or reject each suggestion |

Review these numbers weekly and add or refine content based on the top suggestions.

<Expandable title="What if Fin keeps giving the wrong answer?">
  Check that the correct information exists in your knowledge base. If it does, add a direct edit to the conversation so Fin learns the preferred reply.
</Expandable>
