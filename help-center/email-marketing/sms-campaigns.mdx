## SMS campaigns in Mailchimp
You need to reach customers quickly via text while staying compliant and hitting the right people. This guide walks you through the exact steps to set up, target, and send SMS campaigns.

## Compliance rules
SMS marketing has strict rules. Follow these before you send anything.

<Callout kind="alert">
  You must get explicit opt-in from every recipient. Never import lists without permission.
</Callout>

Key requirements:

- Include your business name or brand in every message.
- Provide a clear way to opt out (for example, reply STOP).
- Follow local laws such as TCPA in the United States.
- Keep records of consent for at least four years.

## Message templates
Use templates to keep messages short and consistent.

| Template type | When to use | Example |
|---------------|-------------|---------|
| Welcome | New subscribers | Thanks for joining. Reply STOP to opt out. |
| Promotion | Sales or offers | 20% off ends today. Shop now at mailchimp.com. |
| Reminder | Abandoned carts or events | Your cart is waiting. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. |

## Targeting your audience
Send only to contacts who opted in for SMS.

1. Go to Audience and select the list you want to use.
2. Create a segment with the condition "SMS consent status equals opted in."
3. Save the segment and name it clearly, such as "SMS Active."

<Callout kind="info">
  Segments update automatically when new contacts opt in.
</Callout>

## Send your first SMS campaign
Follow these steps to build and launch.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the campaign">
    Click Campaigns, then Create Campaign, and choose SMS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your audience">
    Select the SMS consent segment you created earlier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Keep it under 160 characters. Add your brand name and opt-out instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    Preview on a test phone, then schedule or send immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions
<Expandable title="Can I send SMS to contacts who only gave email permission?">
  No. You need separate SMS opt-in for every recipient.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I handle replies like STOP?">
  Mailchimp automatically processes standard opt-out keywords. No extra setup is required.
</Expandable>
