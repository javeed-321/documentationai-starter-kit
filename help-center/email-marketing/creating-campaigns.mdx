If you need to send a targeted email but do not know where to begin, this guide shows you how to pick a template, insert personalization tags, and run an A/B test inside Mailchimp.

## Choose a template

Follow these steps to start with a ready-made design that matches your brand.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign builder">
    Log in to Mailchimp, click Campaigns, then Create Campaign and choose Email.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a template">
    Browse the template gallery and click a layout that fits your message. You can filter by industry or goal.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply your branding">
    Replace placeholder text and images with your own content. Use the style panel to match your brand colors and fonts.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add personalization tags

Personalization tags pull in each recipient's details so every message feels individual.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Always preview your campaign with test contacts to confirm the tags fill in correctly.
</Callout>

Common tags include:

| Tag              | What it shows          |
| ---------------- | ---------------------- |
| `*|FNAME|*`      | First name             |
| `*|LNAME|*`      | Last name              |
| `*|EMAIL|*`      | Email address          |
| `*|COMPANY|*`    | Company name           |

Type the tag exactly as shown, including the asterisks, or pick it from the personalization menu in the editor.

## Set up A/B testing

Test two versions of your subject line or content to see which performs better.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the test">
    In the campaign builder, scroll to A/B Testing and toggle it on.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to test">
    Select Subject Line, From Name, or Send Time. Enter the two variations.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your winner">
    Decide whether Mailchimp should send the winner automatically or let you choose after the test ends.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the campaign">
    Click Confirm and Send. Mailchimp splits your audience and tracks results.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="Can I reuse a template for future campaigns?">
  Yes. Save any edited template to your Saved Templates folder so you can load it again with one click.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What happens if a personalization tag is missing data?">
  Mailchimp leaves the tag blank or shows a fallback you set in the tag options.

</Expandable>

Still stuck? Contact Mailchimp support through the Help menu in your account.
