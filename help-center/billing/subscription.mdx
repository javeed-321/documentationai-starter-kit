You want to choose or change your Mailchimp plan. This page shows your options, how the 14-day trial works, and the steps to upgrade.

## Pricing Plans

Mailchimp offers four tiers. Pick the one that matches your audience size and features.

| Plan       | Monthly Price | Audience Limit | Key Features                     |
|------------|---------------|----------------|----------------------------------|
| Free       | $0            | 500            | Basic email, limited automations |
| Essentials | $13           | 500            | Email support, basic reporting   |
| Standard   | $20           | 500            | A/B testing, priority support    |
| Premium    | $350          | 10,000         | Advanced automation, onboarding  |

Prices scale with audience size. You pay only for what you use.

## Starting the 14-Day Trial

You can try any paid plan free for 14 days.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your account">
    Open Mailchimp and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Billing">
    Click your profile icon, then select Billing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select the tier you want and click Start trial.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your trial ends automatically if you do not upgrade. No charge applies during the trial.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Add a payment method before the trial ends to avoid service interruption.
</Callout>

## Upgrading Your Plan

You can move to a higher tier at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing settings">
    From the menu, go to Billing and Plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select new tier">
    Click Upgrade next to the plan you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm payment">
    Review the prorated charge and complete checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your new features activate right away.

## Common Questions

<Expandable title="Can I downgrade later?">
  Yes. You can switch to a lower plan at the end of your billing cycle.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What happens after the trial?">
  You move to the paid plan you selected. Cancel before the trial ends to stay on the free plan.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Do I get charged during the trial?">
  No. The first charge occurs only after the 14 days if you keep the plan.
</Expandable>
