## Upgrading your plan

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I upgrade my Calendly plan?" default-open="true">
  Open your account settings and select Billing. Choose the plan that fits your needs, then confirm the change. Your new features activate right away.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I switch from monthly to annual billing?">
  Yes. Select the annual option during upgrade. You keep access to all current features while the new rate applies from the next billing cycle.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Understanding prorated billing

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="What happens to my current subscription when I upgrade?" default-open="true">
  Calendly calculates the remaining time on your current plan and applies a credit. You pay only the difference for the upgraded plan.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Will I see a prorated charge on my next invoice?">
  Yes. The invoice shows the prorated amount for the upgrade plus the new plan rate. You can view the full breakdown in your billing history.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Canceling your subscription

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I cancel my Calendly subscription?" default-open="true">
  Go to Billing in account settings and choose Cancel subscription. Follow the prompts to confirm. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What happens to my data after cancellation?">
  Your event types and booking links remain viewable until the subscription ends. After that, Calendly keeps your data for 30 days so you can reactivate if needed.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
