You need to select the Calendly plan that matches your scheduling needs. This page compares the free, standard, teams, and enterprise plans so you can decide fast.

## Plan comparison

| Feature              | Free                  | Standard             | Teams                | Enterprise           |
|----------------------|-----------------------|----------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Event types          | 1                     | Unlimited            | Unlimited            | Unlimited            |
| Calendars synced     | 1                     | 2                    | 6                    | 6                    |
| User seats           | 1                     | 1                    | Starts at 3          | Custom               |
| Reminders            | Email only            | Email and SMS        | Email and SMS        | Email, SMS, and custom |
| Integrations         | Basic                 | Full                 | Full + team          | Full + admin controls |
| SSO and advanced security | No               | No                   | No                   | Yes                  |

## Upgrade your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in and open billing">
    Sign in to your Calendly account and go to Account Settings then Billing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the new plan">
    Select Standard, Teams, or Enterprise and review the seat count you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm payment">
    Enter payment details and click Upgrade. Your new features activate right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Start with Standard if you need more than one calendar. Add Teams later when you need shared event types.
</Callout>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="How many seats come with each plan?" default-open="false">
  Free and Standard give one seat. Teams starts with three seats. Enterprise lets you buy any number of seats.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I switch plans later?" default-open="false">
  Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from the Billing page. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What happens when I reach my seat limit?" default-open="false">
  You cannot add more users until you upgrade. Existing users keep their access.
</Expandable>
