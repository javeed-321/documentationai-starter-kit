## Common Billing Issues

Use the sections below to fix the most frequent payment problems. Each expandable shows the exact symptom, what usually causes it, and the quickest fix.

<Expandable title="Payment method declined at checkout" default-open="false">
Your card is rejected with the message "Your payment method was declined. Please try another card."

This happens when the card issuer blocks the charge or the details do not match your Discord account.

Update the card details in User Settings > Billing, then retry the purchase. If the error repeats, contact your bank to approve the transaction or add a different payment method.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Nitro renewal charge failed" default-open="false">
You receive an email stating "Your Nitro subscription renewal failed" and your perks disappear.

Discord tries the saved payment method three times. After the third failure the subscription pauses.

Go to User Settings > Billing, remove the failing card, and add a new one. Then select "Retry payment" on the same page. Your Nitro access returns once the charge succeeds.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Cancel Nitro subscription" default-open="false">
You want to stop future charges but keep access until the current period ends.

Subscriptions do not cancel automatically when you remove a card.

Open User Settings > Billing, click "Cancel Subscription," and confirm. You keep Nitro until the paid period expires, then it reverts to the free plan.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Request a refund after accidental purchase" default-open="false">
You bought Nitro by mistake and see the charge on your statement.

Discord offers refunds only within 14 days and only for the first purchase on an account.

Submit a ticket through the Help menu with the order number from your email. Support replies within two business days with the refund decision.

</Expandable>
