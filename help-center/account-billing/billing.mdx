## Manage your Calendly billing

Need to update payment details, switch plans, or cancel your subscription? This page walks you through the exact steps.

## Update your payment method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing settings">
    Log in to Calendly and go to Account Settings, then select Billing.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit payment details">
    Click the current payment method, then enter your new card or bank information.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Confirm the update. Calendly charges the new method on your next billing date.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Keep your payment method current to avoid any interruption in service.
</Callout>

## Compare annual and monthly billing

| Billing cycle | Best for | Savings |
|---------------|----------|---------|
| Monthly | Testing or short-term needs | None |
| Annual | Long-term use | Two months free on paid plans |

Switch cycles from the same Billing page by selecting Change plan and choosing the new cycle. Your new rate starts on the next renewal date.

## Cancel your subscription

<Expandable title="What happens when I cancel?">
  You keep full access until the end of your current paid period. After that, your account moves to the free plan.

</Expandable>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to billing">
    From Account Settings, open the Billing section.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Select cancel">
    Click Cancel subscription and follow the prompts to confirm.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Download invoices">
    Export any needed invoices before the plan ends.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
  Canceling stops future charges but does not issue refunds for the current period.
</Callout>
