## Overview

Mailchimp templates help you build consistent campaigns without starting from scratch. You can edit a ready-made layout in minutes or apply your brand kit for a polished look across email, SMS, and social. The editor keeps changes live so you see results instantly.

## Using the Template Editor

Open any campaign and select a template to begin. The editor shows a live preview on the right while you adjust blocks on the left.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Drag and drop" icon="move">
    Click a content block and drag it into place. Drop it between existing sections to rearrange your layout.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Style panel" icon="palette">
    Select any block to open the style panel. Change fonts, colors, and spacing without touching code.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Save your work often. The editor autosaves every few seconds, but a manual save gives you a restore point.
</Callout>

## Applying Your Brand Kit

Your brand kit stores logos, colors, and fonts in one place. Once saved, you can apply the entire set to any template with two clicks.

<Expandable title="How to add assets to the brand kit" default-open="false">
  Go to Audience and then Brand kit. Upload your logo, enter brand colors in hex, and choose your primary font. These assets appear automatically in every new template.

</Expandable>

## Optimizing for Mobile

Every template must look good on small screens. The editor includes a mobile preview toggle so you can check spacing and text size before sending.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Stack columns" icon="layout" href="#">
    Turn side-by-side columns into a single stack on phones so nothing gets cut off.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Font size check" icon="type" href="#">
    Keep body text at least 16 px. Smaller sizes become hard to read on mobile.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Quick Examples

- Welcome series: Start with the "Welcome" template, swap in your logo from the brand kit, and add a simple discount code block.
- Abandoned cart: Use the "Reminder" template, shorten the subject line, and test the mobile view before launch.

## Best Practices

- Keep the most important message in the first screen of the preview.
- Use your brand kit on every campaign so subscribers recognize you at a glance.
- Preview on both desktop and mobile before you schedule or send.
