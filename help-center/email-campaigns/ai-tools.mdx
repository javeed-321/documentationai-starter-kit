## Overview

Mailchimp's AI tools help you create effective email campaigns without starting from scratch. You can generate subject lines, draft content, and identify audience segments that are likely to engage. These features reduce guesswork so you can focus on your message.

## Content Generation

The AI content tool writes email body text based on your campaign goals and brand voice. Enter a short prompt describing your offer, and the system suggests complete sections you can edit.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Keep prompts specific. Mention your product name and target audience for more relevant suggestions.
</Callout>

## Subject Line Optimization

Subject line suggestions appear before you send. The tool scores each option for open-rate potential and shows why it may perform well.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Short subject lines" icon="zap">
    Brief lines under 40 characters often work best for mobile readers.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Question subject lines" icon="help-circle">
    Questions can increase curiosity when they match your audience's interests.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Predictive Segmentation

Predictive segmentation creates audience groups based on past behavior and engagement likelihood. You select a goal such as "likely to purchase" and the AI builds the list automatically.

<Card title="Start with a test segment" href="#" icon="users">
  Send to a small predicted group first to confirm the segment performs as expected.
</Card>

## Examples

Here is a simple workflow:

1. Open a new campaign and choose the AI content option.
2. Enter your offer details and generate a draft.
3. Review subject line scores and pick the highest-rated option.
4. Apply predictive segmentation to your audience list.

## Best Practices

- Review every AI suggestion before sending. The tool provides starting points, not final copy.
- Test one change at a time so you can measure results clearly.
- Update your brand voice settings regularly for better personalization.

<Expandable title="What data does the AI use?" default-open="false">
  The models analyze your past campaigns, audience activity, and publicly available industry patterns. No external data sources are added without your approval.
</Expandable>
