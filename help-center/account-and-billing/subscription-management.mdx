If you need to change your plan, adjust the number of seats, or review your current usage, this page shows you the exact steps.

## Plan tiers

Intercom offers three paid tiers. Choose the one that matches your team size and feature needs.

| Tier    | Best for                  | Key limits                     | Monthly price (billed annually) |
|---------|---------------------------|--------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Starter | Small teams               | 5 seats, basic Fin             | $29 per seat                    |
| Growth  | Growing support teams     | 20 seats, advanced automation  | $65 per seat                    |
| Scale   | Large or enterprise teams | Unlimited seats, custom Fin    | Contact sales                   |

## Upgrade or downgrade your plan

1. Open Intercom and go to **Settings > Subscription**.
2. Click **Change plan**.
3. Select the new tier and confirm the seat count.
4. Review the prorated charge and click **Confirm**.

Your new plan activates immediately. Any extra seats are billed on your next invoice.

<Callout kind="tip">
Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.
</Callout>

## Manage seats

Add or remove seats at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a seat">
    Go to **Settings > Team > Seats**, click **Invite teammate**, enter their email, and assign a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a seat">
    Find the teammate in the same list, click the three-dot menu, then select **Remove access**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View usage reports

You can see how many conversations, Fin resolutions, and seats you have used this month.

1. Go to **Settings > Subscription > Usage**.
2. Choose the date range you want to review.
3. Download the CSV if you need the data for your finance team.

## Common questions

<Expandable title="When will I be charged for added seats?">
  Added seats are prorated and charged on your next invoice.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I switch from monthly to annual billing?">
  Yes. Select **Annual** on the plan change screen to receive the discount.
</Expandable>
