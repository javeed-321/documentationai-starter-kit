Need help with your Mailchimp account? This page shows how to reach support, hire an expert, or book personalized onboarding.

## Start a chat with support

You can chat with a support agent directly from your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the chat">
    Log in to your Mailchimp account and click the chat icon in the lower right corner.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your issue">
    Type your question or problem in the chat window. Include any error messages or account details.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Get help">
    An agent responds within minutes during business hours. Save the chat transcript for your records.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Chat is fastest for quick questions. Have your account login ready.

</Callout>

## Hire an expert

Mailchimp experts help with campaign setup, list management, and automation.

<Expandable title="How do I find and hire an expert?" default-open="false">
  Go to the Experts directory in your account menu. Browse profiles, read reviews, and send a direct message to start a project.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What services do experts offer?" default-open="false">
  Experts handle email design, audience segmentation, integration setup, and growth strategy.

</Expandable>

## Schedule personalized onboarding

New users or teams can book a guided onboarding session.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a session">
    From the Help menu, select "Schedule onboarding" and choose a time that fits your calendar.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare your goals">
    List the campaigns or automations you want to build so the session stays focused.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the call">
    A Mailchimp specialist walks you through setup and answers questions live.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="Is chat support available 24/7?" default-open="false">
  Chat runs during standard business hours. Outside those hours, submit a ticket from the Help menu.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How much does an expert cost?" default-open="false">
  Pricing varies by expert and project scope. You see rates before you hire.

</Expandable>

Still stuck? Contact support through the chat icon in your account.
