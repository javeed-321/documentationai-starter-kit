If you need help with Discord or want to share feedback, this page shows exactly how to submit a request and what to expect next.

## Submitting a Support Request

Follow these steps to open a ticket.

<Steps>
<Step title="Visit the Help Center">
  Go to the Discord Help Center in your browser and sign in with your account.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose your topic">
  Select the category that matches your issue, such as account problems or server settings.

</Step>
<Step title="Fill out the form">
  Describe the problem in detail and attach any relevant screenshots or logs.

</Step>
<Step title="Submit and save the ticket number">
  Click submit. Keep the confirmation email for reference.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Expected Response Times

Support handles requests in this order:

| Priority | Typical Response | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | Within 24 hours | Account hacks or safety issues |
| Medium | 1–3 days | Billing or server outages |
| Low | 3–7 days | Feature suggestions or general questions |

## Sharing Community Feedback

You can suggest improvements without opening a ticket.

- Use the in-app feedback button under User Settings.
- Post ideas in official Discord feedback channels.
- Tag `@discord_support` in public posts for visibility.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Feedback sent this way does not receive a personal reply but helps shape future updates.
</Callout>

## Common Questions

<Expandable title="Can I check the status of my ticket?">
  Yes. Log back into the Help Center and view your open requests under the Tickets section.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What if my issue is urgent?">
  Mark the ticket as high priority and include clear evidence. Response times are faster for safety-related reports.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I follow up on a closed ticket?">
  Reply directly to the resolution email or open a new ticket and reference the original number.

</Expandable>

## Still stuck?

Reach out to `@discord_support` directly on Discord.
