Need help with your Mailchimp account or campaigns? This page shows you how to reach support, hire experts, access onboarding services, and connect with customer success resources.

## Hire an expert

<Expandable title="How do I hire a Mailchimp expert?">
  Mailchimp partners with certified experts who can build campaigns, set up automations, and optimize your audience growth.

  Follow these steps to get started:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Browse the expert directory">
      Log in to your Mailchimp account and visit the Experts page to view available professionals by skill and location.

    </Step>
    <Step title="Request a consultation">
      Select an expert and submit your project details, including campaign goals and timeline.

    </Step>
    <Step title="Review and book">
      Compare proposals, check reviews, then book the expert directly through the platform.

    </Step>
  </Steps>

</Expandable>

## Onboarding services

<Expandable title="What onboarding services does Mailchimp offer?">
  New users can get guided setup for email, social, and ad campaigns through Mailchimp's onboarding programs.

  - Free self-guided tutorials cover account basics and first campaign creation.
  - Paid onboarding includes live sessions with a specialist who configures your audience segments and automations.

  <Callout kind="tip">
    Schedule your onboarding call within the first 14 days for the fastest results.

  </Callout>

</Expandable>

## Customer success resources

<Expandable title="How do I access customer success help?">
  Mailchimp customer success managers provide ongoing strategy reviews for growing accounts.

  Check your account dashboard for eligibility. If available, request a success call to review performance metrics and plan next steps.

</Expandable>

## Still stuck?

Contact Mailchimp support directly through the Help menu in your account.
