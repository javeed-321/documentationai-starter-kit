## Get help when you need it

You need assistance with Calendly and want a fast way to reach the team. This page lists the available support channels and what to expect.

## Available channels

| Channel          | Best for                     | How to reach it                          |
|------------------|------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Chat             | Quick questions              | Sign in and click the chat icon in the lower right |
| Email            | Detailed issues              | Send a message from your account settings |
| Schedule a call  | Complex setup or billing     | Use the "Talk to Sales" link on the website |

## Start a chat

Follow these steps to open a chat:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Log in to your Calendly account.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the chat">
    Click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of any page.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your issue">
    Type your question or problem and send the message.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Chat is available during business hours and usually responds within a few minutes.
</Callout>

## Response times

<Expandable title="How long does support take to reply?" default-open="false">
  Chat replies arrive within minutes during business hours. Email replies arrive within one business day. Scheduled calls are confirmed within 24 hours.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What if I have an Enterprise plan?" default-open="false">
  Enterprise customers receive priority routing and dedicated support contacts. You can also request a call directly from your account dashboard.
</Expandable>

## Still stuck?

Contact support through any of the channels above.
