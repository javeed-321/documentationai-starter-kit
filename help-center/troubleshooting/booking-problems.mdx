---
title: Fix booking and reminder failures
description: Troubleshoot why invitees cannot book or why notifications are not sent.
---

## Booking link problems

<Expandable title="Invitees see 'This link has expired' when they try to book">
  Calendly marks a booking link as expired after the event type is deleted or its availability window ends.

  **Fix**

  - Open the event type in your Calendly account.
  - Extend the date range under Availability.
  - Copy the new link and send it again.

  Test the link in an incognito window before sharing.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Invitees receive 'Unable to book this event' after selecting a time">
  The most common cause is a calendar conflict that Calendly detects only after the time slot is chosen.

  **Fix**

  - Ask the invitee to refresh the page and try a different slot.
  - In your Calendly settings, increase the buffer time between meetings.
  - Confirm that all connected calendars are set to sync every five minutes.
</Expandable>

## Reminder and notification issues

<Expandable title="Confirmation or reminder emails land in spam or promotions folders">
  Calendly sends messages from `notifications@example.com`. Some inboxes treat these as marketing mail.

  **Fix**

  - Tell invitees to add `notifications@example.com` to their contacts.
  - In your Calendly account, go to Notifications and enable "Send from your own domain" if available on your plan.
  - Check the deliverability report in your account settings for any recent blocks.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="No reminder email arrives even though the booking succeeded">
  Reminders are skipped when the invitee's email address fails validation or when the event is booked less than one hour before it starts.

  **Fix**

  - Ask the invitee to rebook using a different email address.
  - Edit the event type and lower the minimum reminder time to fifteen minutes.
  - Manually send a test reminder from the event page to confirm delivery.
</Expandable>
