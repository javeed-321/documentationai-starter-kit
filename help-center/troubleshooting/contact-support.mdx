## Submit a support ticket

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I create a support ticket?" default-open="true">
  Sign in to your Calendly account and visit the help section. Choose the option to contact support and fill out the form with details about your issue.

  Include your account email, a clear description of the problem, and any relevant screenshots.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What response time should I expect?">
  Most tickets receive an initial reply within one business day. Complex issues may take longer depending on the details provided.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I attach files to my ticket?">
  Yes. The ticket form accepts images and short documents that help explain the issue.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Browse the community forum

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where can I find the community forum?" default-open="true">
  From the Calendly help center, select the community link. Search existing threads or start a new discussion about your question.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What kinds of questions work best in the forum?">
  Post questions about setup steps, feature ideas, or general workflow tips. Other users often share quick solutions.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Do forum posts receive official answers?">
  Calendly staff monitor the forum and reply to common topics, but peer answers appear first.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Enterprise support options

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do enterprise customers get priority support?" default-open="true">
  Enterprise plan users receive a dedicated support channel with faster response times and account-specific guidance.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can my team request a live training session?">
  Enterprise accounts may schedule onboarding calls or custom training through their account manager.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Who handles security or compliance questions?">
  Enterprise support routes security and compliance inquiries to specialists familiar with your organization's requirements.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
