---

If Fin isn't resolving conversations at the expected rate or is giving incorrect answers, this page helps you find the cause and improve results quickly.

## Low resolution rate

Fin may fail to close conversations when it lacks clear guidance or when your sources are incomplete.

Follow these steps to raise the rate:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review recent unresolved conversations">
    Open the Helpdesk and filter for conversations where Fin handed off to a human.

    Note the topics that appear most often.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add missing answers to your knowledge sources">
    Create or update articles that directly answer those topics.

    Keep each article short and specific.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test Fin on the same questions">
    Use the test chat in the Fin settings panel.

    Confirm that Fin now resolves the issue without handing off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Aim for at least 70 % of common questions to have a matching article before expecting a high resolution rate.
</Callout>

## Incorrect answers

When Fin gives the wrong information, it usually pulls from outdated or conflicting sources.

<Expandable title="Check source freshness">
  Open the knowledge sources list and sort by last updated date.

  Replace or archive any article older than 90 days that Fin has cited in the last week.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Remove conflicting content">
  Search your knowledge base for duplicate answers on the same topic.

  Keep only the most recent version and delete the rest.
</Expandable>

## Knowledge gaps

Fin cannot answer questions that have no matching content in your sources.

Use this table to decide what to add next:

| Symptom                    | Likely gap                        | Quick fix                              |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Fin says "I don't know"    | No article on the topic           | Write a short answer article           |
| Fin gives partial answers  | Article exists but is too broad   | Split the article into specific cases  |
| Fin repeats the same reply | Single article used for many cases| Add follow-up articles for each case   |

## Still stuck?

Contact Intercom support with the conversation IDs where Fin performed poorly.
