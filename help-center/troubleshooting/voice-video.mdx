## Voice Issues

Use the tabs below to switch between voice and video troubleshooting steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Voice" icon="mic">

<Expandable title="Microphone not detected in voice channels" default-open="false">
  Discord shows "No input device found" when you join a voice channel.

  **Cause**  
  Your operating system has not granted Discord microphone access or the device is disabled in system settings.

  **Fix**

  1. Open Discord settings and go to Voice & Video.
  2. Click the Input Device dropdown and select your microphone.
  3. If the device is missing, open your system sound settings and enable the microphone.
  4. Restart Discord and test by speaking in a voice channel.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="RTC Connecting error persists" default-open="false">
  The voice status stays stuck on "RTC Connecting" for more than 30 seconds.

  **Cause**  
  UDP ports required by Discord are blocked by your network or firewall.

  **Fix**

  1. Try switching servers in Voice & Video settings to a different region.
  2. Disable any VPN temporarily.
  3. Add Discord as an allowed app in your firewall settings.
  4. Contact your network admin if you are on a school or work network.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="High latency and robotic audio" default-open="false">
  Other users report choppy or robotic sound from you.

  **Cause**  
  Packet loss or high ping on your current voice server.

  **Fix**

  1. In Voice & Video settings, enable "Enable Quality of Service High Packet Priority".
  2. Change the voice server region to one closer to your location.
  3. Close bandwidth-heavy apps running in the background.
  4. Test again in the same voice channel.

</Expandable>
