Calendly may fail to collect payments for paid events or process refunds. This page shows the fastest fixes for those errors.

## Fix payment collection failures

Most payment errors happen when your Stripe or PayPal connection is outdated or your event type settings conflict with the payment provider.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your payment connection">
    Go to Account Settings and open Integrations. Reconnect Stripe or PayPal if you see a red warning.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the event type">
    Open the paid event type and confirm the price, currency, and payment provider are still correct.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with a new booking">
    Create a test event and complete checkout. If the payment still fails, move to the next step.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
  Do not change your Stripe account ID while active bookings exist. This can cancel pending payments.
</Callout>

## Request a refund

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the booking">
    Go to your Event Types page and open the scheduled event that needs a refund.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue the refund">
    Click the three-dot menu next to the payment and select Issue refund. Choose full or partial amount.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with the invitee">
    Calendly emails the invitee automatically. Check your email to confirm the refund went through.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Generate an invoice

<Expandable title="How do I download an invoice for a paid booking?">
  Open the booking from your dashboard, click the payment line item, then select Download invoice. The PDF appears in your downloads folder.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Why is my invoice missing tax details?">
  Tax information pulls from your Stripe account. Update your tax settings in Stripe, then regenerate the invoice in Calendly.

</Expandable>

## Payment error codes

| Code | Meaning | Quick fix |
| ---- | ------- | --------- |
| 1001 | Card declined | Ask the invitee to try a different card |
| 2003 | Provider disconnected | Reconnect Stripe or PayPal in Account Settings |
| 3002 | Currency mismatch | Edit the event type and match the currency to your payment account |
