## Common Payment Issues

Use the sections below to identify your exact error message and apply the matching fix.

<Expandable title="Card declined with error 'Insufficient funds'" default-open="false">

Your customer sees a decline when trying to pay.

**Cause**  
The card issuer rejected the charge because the account balance is too low.

**Fix**  
1. Ask the customer to try a different card.  
2. If the customer insists the balance is correct, have them contact their bank to authorize the transaction.  
3. In your Shopify admin, go to Orders and mark the order as paid manually only after confirming funds.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Payout delayed beyond expected date" default-open="false">

Your payout from Shopify Payments has not arrived on the scheduled day.

**Cause**  
Bank verification is still pending or a recent policy review flagged the account.

**Fix**  
- Check the Payments section in your Shopify admin for any verification requests.  
- Upload the requested documents directly in the payout settings.  
- Contact your bank to confirm they accept transfers from Shopify Payments.  
- Wait up to two additional business days after verification completes.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Third-party gateway shows 'Invalid API credentials'" default-open="false">

You receive an authentication error when testing a third-party payment gateway.

**Cause**  
The API keys entered in Shopify do not match the ones issued by the gateway provider.

**Fix**  
1. Log in to your gateway dashboard and regenerate both the public and secret keys.  
2. In Shopify admin, go to Settings then Payments.  
3. Paste the new keys exactly as shown, with no extra spaces.  
4. Save and run a test transaction using the gateway's sandbox mode.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Refund stuck at 'Processing' status" default-open="false">

A refund you issued remains in processing and the customer has not received funds.

**Cause**  
The original payment method requires manual review by the bank or the refund amount exceeds daily limits.

**Fix**  
- Go to the order in Shopify admin and cancel the pending refund.  
- Create a new refund for a smaller amount that stays under your account limits.  
- If the card was issued outside your country, advise the customer to allow five to seven business days.  
- For Shopify Payments, open a support ticket with the order number for manual release.

</Expandable>

<Callout kind="tip">
Always test changes in your store's test mode before applying them to live orders.
</Callout>
