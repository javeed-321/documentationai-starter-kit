## Creating a Support Ticket

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I open a ticket?" default-open="true">
    Sign in to your Zendesk account and navigate to the Help Center. Select Submit a request, choose the product area, and describe the issue with relevant details such as account ID and steps to reproduce.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What information should I include?">
    Provide your account email, exact error messages, browser or device details, and any recent changes made before the issue appeared. Attach screenshots when they clarify the problem.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Priority Levels

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What priority options exist?" default-open="true">
    Tickets are classified as Low, Normal, High, or Urgent. Choose based on business impact: Urgent for complete service outages affecting all users.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I change priority after submission?">
    Yes. Reply to the ticket thread and request an adjustment. Support reviews the request and updates the level if the impact description warrants it.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Response SLAs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What are the response times?" default-open="true">
    Urgent tickets receive a first response within one hour. High within four hours, Normal within one business day, and Low within two business days during standard support hours.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Do SLAs differ by plan?">
    Enterprise plans include faster targets and 24/7 coverage for Urgent issues. Check your subscription details in the account settings for exact commitments.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
