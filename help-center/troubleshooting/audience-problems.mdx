## Audience import and contact problems

Mailchimp flags invalid emails, duplicate contacts, and hygiene issues during import. This page shows the fastest fixes for import errors, unsubscribe handling, and GDPR compliance.

## Remove invalid emails before import

Clean your list first to avoid bounces and blocks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export and review your list">
    Open your source file in a spreadsheet and sort by email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete obvious errors">
    Remove rows with missing @ symbols, spaces, or domains like example.com.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a validation check">
    Use a free email checker tool on the remaining addresses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import the cleaned file">
    In Mailchimp, go to Audience, click Import Contacts, and upload the file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warning">
  Never import purchased or rented lists. Mailchimp may suspend accounts that send to them.
</Callout>

## Handle unsubscribes correctly

Unsubscribes must be processed immediately to stay compliant.

<Expandable title="What happens when someone unsubscribes?">
  Mailchimp automatically adds the address to the audience's unsubscribe list and stops all future sends to that contact.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I re-add an unsubscribed contact?">
  No. Re-adding the address violates anti-spam rules. Ask the person to resubscribe through your signup form instead.
</Expandable>

## Meet GDPR requirements

<Callout kind="info">
  GDPR applies if you collect data from people in the EU. Always use double opt-in and keep clear records.
</Callout>

Use this table to choose the right setting:

| Setting | Recommended choice | Reason |
| ------- | ------------------ | ------ |
| Signup form | Double opt-in | Creates proof of consent |
| Data retention | 24 months or less | Limits stored personal data |
| Export audience | Include consent fields | Shows audit trail when requested |
