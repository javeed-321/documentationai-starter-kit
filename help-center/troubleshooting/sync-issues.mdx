## Common Sync Problems

Dropbox keeps your files updated across devices, but occasional issues can interrupt the process. The sections below describe frequent symptoms, their typical causes, and the steps to resolve them.

<Callout kind="info">
Check the Dropbox system tray or menu bar icon first. A red badge or paused status often points to the exact issue.
</Callout>

<Expandable title="Selective sync shows files as not syncing" default-open="false">
Problem: You see the message "This folder is not syncing" or files remain grayed out after you enable selective sync.

Cause: The folder was excluded in selective sync settings or a parent folder is paused.

Fix:

1. Open Dropbox preferences and go to the Sync tab.
2. Click Selective sync and confirm the folder is checked.
3. If the folder still does not appear, unlink and relink the account from the Account tab.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Conflicting copies appear after editing the same file" default-open="false">
Problem: Dropbox creates files named "filename (John's conflicted copy)" or similar.

Cause: Two devices edited the file while offline or during a sync delay.

Fix:

1. Open both the original file and the conflicted copy.
2. Compare changes and merge them into one version.
3. Delete the conflicted copy once the merged file is saved.

Dropbox automatically uploads the merged version to all devices.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Sync stops due to bandwidth limits" default-open="false">
Problem: The sync status shows "Syncing paused" or progress stalls for hours with no error message.

Cause: Upload or download bandwidth is limited in preferences, or your network connection is throttled.

Fix:

1. Open Dropbox preferences and select the Bandwidth tab.
2. Set both upload and download limits to "Don't limit".
3. Click Apply and wait for the sync to resume.

If the issue continues, test your connection speed at speedtest.net and contact your network administrator.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Error: 'Dropbox can't sync because the disk is full'" default-open="false">
Problem: You receive the exact alert "Dropbox can't sync because the disk is full" even though space appears available.

Cause: The local Dropbox folder has reached the operating system's storage limit or a quota is active.

Fix:

1. Move large files out of the Dropbox folder using Finder or File Explorer.
2. Empty the trash or recycle bin.
3. Restart the Dropbox desktop app from the system tray menu.

The sync resumes once free space exceeds 1 GB.

</Expandable>
