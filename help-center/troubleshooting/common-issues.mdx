## AI Resolution Failures

<Expandable title="AI agent returns generic responses instead of resolving tickets">

The AI agent replies with vague messages like "I will look into that" on complex support requests.

This happens when the agent lacks access to your specific knowledge base articles or recent ticket history.

Update the agent's training data in the Resolution Platform settings, then retrain it on your top 50 resolved tickets. Test by creating a sample ticket with the exact phrasing from your customers.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Agent times out during resolution attempts">

You see the error "AI agent failed to resolve ticket: Agent timeout after 30s" in the ticket audit log.

The cause is usually an overloaded integration that the agent relies on for customer data.

Reduce the number of simultaneous actions in the agent's workflow to three or fewer, then restart the agent from the AI agents dashboard.

</Expandable>

## Integration Sync Errors

<Expandable title="CRM data fails to sync into tickets">

Tickets show the message "Sync failed with error: 429 Too Many Requests from CRM" and customer details stay empty.

Rate limits on the connected app are being hit during high-volume periods.

Lower the sync frequency to every 15 minutes in Marketplace settings and add a retry delay of 60 seconds.

</Expandable>

## Channel Connectivity

<Expandable title="Voice channel drops incoming calls">

The Contact Center logs display "Unable to connect to voice channel: SIP registration error" and calls route to voicemail.

Your SIP credentials may have expired or the firewall blocks the required ports.

Re-enter the SIP details under Contact Center configuration and whitelist ports 5060-5061 in your network settings.

</Expandable>
