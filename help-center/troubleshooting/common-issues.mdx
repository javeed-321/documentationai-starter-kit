## Common Issues

Use the sections below to identify symptoms quickly and apply the matching fix.

<Expandable title="AI answers miss key details from your knowledge base" default-open="false">
  **Problem**

  Fin returns incomplete or off-topic replies even though the right articles exist in your Help Center.

  **Cause**

  The articles lack clear headings or the content is not yet indexed after upload.

  **Fix**

  1. Open the article in the Help Center editor.
  2. Add descriptive H2 and H3 headings that match common customer questions.
  3. Save the article, then go to Fin settings and select Reindex knowledge base.
  4. Test the same query again after indexing completes (usually under two minutes).

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Conversations route to the wrong team or queue" default-open="false">
  **Problem**

  Incoming messages land in the general inbox instead of the assigned support queue.

  **Cause**

  Routing rules reference outdated team names or tags that no longer exist.

  **Fix**

  1. Go to Settings → Inbox → Routing.
  2. Review each active rule and replace any missing team or tag references.
  3. Use the Test routing button with a sample conversation to confirm the correct queue is selected.
  4. Save changes and monitor the next few tickets.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Integration sync shows repeated failures" default-open="false">
  **Problem**

  The sync status page displays repeated errors such as "Unable to fetch records from CRM" or stalled updates.

  **Cause**

  Authentication tokens have expired or required scopes were removed during a recent CRM update.

  **Fix**

  1. Navigate to Settings → Integrations and locate the failing connection.
  2. Click Reconnect and follow the OAuth prompts to grant all listed permissions.
  3. After reconnection, select Run manual sync and check the activity log for a successful status.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Helpdesk feels slow when loading conversation lists" default-open="false">
  **Problem**

  The conversation view takes several seconds to load and filters respond sluggishly.

  **Cause**

  Too many active inboxes or saved views are being loaded at once.

  **Fix**

  1. Go to Settings → Workspace → Performance.
  2. Archive or hide any inboxes you no longer use.
  3. Reduce the number of saved views pinned to your sidebar.
  4. Clear your browser cache and reload the workspace.

</Expandable>
