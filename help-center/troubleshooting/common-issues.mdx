## Login Failures

You cannot sign in to Discord. This page shows the quickest fixes for the most common login problems.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify your credentials">
    Enter your email or username and password again. Caps Lock can cause mismatches.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset your password">
    Click the forgot password link on the login screen. Check your email inbox and spam folder for the reset message.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check for account restrictions">
    Log in from the Discord website in a browser. If it works there, the issue is with the app.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Use a strong, unique password and enable two-factor authentication after you regain access.
</Callout>

## Connection Errors

Discord shows you are offline or cannot reach servers. Follow these steps to restore your connection.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Test your internet">
    Open a browser and load a simple webpage. If it fails, restart your router first.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch networks">
    Toggle between Wi-Fi and mobile data. Try a different Wi-Fi network if available.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Disable VPN or proxy">
    Turn off any VPN, proxy, or firewall temporarily. Reconnect to Discord and test.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## App Performance Issues

The Discord app crashes, freezes, or runs slowly. These steps usually resolve the problem.

<Expandable title="Clear the cache">
  Close Discord completely. Delete the cache folder in your app data directory, then reopen the app.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Update the app">
  Go to your app store or Discord website and install the latest version. Outdated builds often crash.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Reinstall Discord">
  Uninstall the app, restart your device, then download and install a fresh copy from the official site.

</Expandable>

<Callout kind="alert">
  Avoid third-party Discord clients. They can cause crashes and put your account at risk.
</Callout>
