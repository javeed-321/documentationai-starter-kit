## Storage quota errors

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Your Dropbox is full">

You see the message "Your Dropbox is full. You have used 100 percent of your storage quota."

This happens when your account reaches its storage limit and new uploads are blocked.

Clear space by deleting unneeded files from your Dropbox folder or upgrading your plan in account settings.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Upload blocked by quota">

Uploads fail with the error "Cannot upload file. Storage quota exceeded."

Dropbox stops new file transfers once the limit is hit.

Remove large files or move them to another location, then retry the upload.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## App connectivity problems

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Unable to connect to Dropbox">

The Dropbox app shows "Unable to connect to Dropbox servers. Check your internet connection."

This occurs when network settings or firewalls block the app.

Verify your internet works in a browser, then restart the Dropbox app or allow it through your firewall settings.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Performance tips

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Dropbox app runs slowly or crashes">

The app freezes or closes unexpectedly during large syncs.

High CPU usage or outdated app versions often cause this.

Update to the latest Dropbox version from the official site and pause syncing for very large folders.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
