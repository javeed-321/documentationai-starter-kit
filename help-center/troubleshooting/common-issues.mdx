If your Zendesk AI agents give inaccurate answers, tickets fail to route, or integrations stop working, this page gives direct fixes for the three most common problems.

## AI agent accuracy

<Expandable title="Agent responses miss key details or suggest wrong solutions">
Check the training data first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review recent interactions">
    Open the agent dashboard and filter conversations from the last seven days.

    Look for patterns in missed details.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add missing examples">
    Go to Agent Settings > Training and upload five new examples that match the gaps you found.

    Save and test with a sample ticket.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Retrain the agent">
    Click Retrain in the same menu.

    Wait for the status to show Complete before testing again.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Run a quick test ticket after each change so you see results immediately.
</Callout>

</Expandable>

## Channel connectivity

<Expandable title="Tickets from chat or voice do not appear">
Confirm the channel is still connected.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check connection status">
    Go to Channels and look for a green checkmark next to the affected channel.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconnect if needed">
    Click the channel name, then select Reconnect.

    Enter your credentials again if prompted.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the channel">
    Send a test message from the live chat or make a test call.

    Confirm the ticket appears in your queue within one minute.

  </Step>
</Steps>

</Expandable>

## Integration errors

<Expandable title="Data does not sync with connected apps">
Most sync failures come from expired tokens or permission changes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the integration list">
    Go to Admin > Integrations and find the failing app.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Reauthorize the connection">
    Click Reconnect and follow the prompts to grant fresh permissions.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the sync">
    Trigger a manual sync from the integration settings.

    Check that new tickets or contacts appear in both systems.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  If the error persists after reauthorization, review the app's required scopes in the Zendesk Marketplace listing.
</Callout>

</Expandable>
