## Email not reaching inboxes

Many users see their Mailchimp campaigns land in spam folders or fail to deliver. This page shows quick fixes for spam delivery, bounce handling, and low open rates.

## Fix spam folder delivery

Follow these steps to improve inbox placement.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your sender reputation">
    Review your domain and IP reputation in Mailchimp's dashboard. Low scores often trigger spam filters.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify authentication">
    Confirm DKIM and SPF records are active for your sending domain.

    - Log in to your domain provider.
    - Add the records Mailchimp provides.
    - Wait up to 48 hours for propagation.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Clean your list">
    Remove inactive or invalid addresses before the next send.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Warm up new domains gradually. Start with small sends and increase volume over days.
</Callout>

## Handle bounces

Bounces reduce deliverability. Mailchimp classifies them automatically.

| Bounce type | What it means | Action |
|-------------|---------------|--------|
| Hard bounce | Permanent failure (invalid address) | Mailchimp removes the address |
| Soft bounce | Temporary issue (full inbox) | Mailchimp retries a few times |

<Expandable title="How do I view bounce reports?">
  Go to Reports, select your campaign, then click the Bounces tab. Export the list to review details.

</Expandable>

## Improve low open rates

Low opens often point to subject lines or timing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Test subject lines">
    Use Mailchimp's subject line helper to compare options and pick the strongest.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Segment your audience">
    Send only to engaged contacts who opened recent campaigns.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check send time">
    Review the time report and reschedule for when your audience is active.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Avoid all-caps subjects or excessive punctuation. These lower engagement.
</Callout>
