## Troubleshooting common issues

If your routing, AI agent replies, integrations, or voice quality are not working as expected, use the sections below to find the most common fixes fast.

## Card declines

Use these steps when a payment fails, a billing update does not go through, or your account stays in a past-due state.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the card details" icon="credit-card">
    Confirm the card number, expiration date, security code, and billing address match the cardholder records.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the card can be charged" icon="check-circle">
    Make sure the card is active, has enough funds or available credit, and allows online or recurring payments.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Try the payment again" icon="arrow-right">
    Re-enter the payment method carefully and retry the charge. If the issue continues, use a different supported card.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact your bank or card issuer" icon="help-circle">
    Ask whether the transaction was blocked for fraud, international use, or subscription billing.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  If you updated the card, wait a few minutes and refresh the billing page before trying again.
</Callout>

## AI agent responses are wrong or off-topic

If an AI agent gives a response that sounds made up, misses your policy, or answers the wrong question, tighten the knowledge and routing inputs first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the source content" icon="book-open">
    Check the help articles, macros, or approved content the agent uses. Remove outdated or conflicting instructions.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the allowed answers" icon="settings">
    Limit the agent to approved topics, required handoff rules, and the exact tone you want.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with real customer questions" icon="play-circle">
    Send a few common prompts and compare the replies to your expected answer.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a handoff rule" icon="users">
    If the question is sensitive, ambiguous, or requires account access, route the conversation to a human agent.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
  AI agents can still produce incorrect answers if the source content is unclear or incomplete. Keep approved answers current and specific.
</Callout>

### Quick checks for hallucination fixes

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Invented policy details | Remove unsupported content from the training source |
| Wrong product or plan details | Update the knowledge article to match the current setup |
| Repeatedly vague answers | Add a more specific prompt or fallback rule |
| Unsafe or sensitive reply | Block the topic and route to a human |

<Expandable title="How do I reduce bad AI answers?" default-open="false">
  Tighten the source content, remove duplicates, and test the agent again with short, specific prompts.

</Expandable>

## Integration sync errors

Sync issues usually come from expired access, changed permissions, or a failed field mapping. Start with the connection, then check the data.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the integration is connected" icon="cloud">
    Open the integration settings and verify the connection is active and authorized.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check permissions and scopes" icon="lock">
    Make sure the connected account still has permission to read and write the data Zendesk needs.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review field mapping" icon="database">
    Confirm required fields still map correctly after any schema changes.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Resync the data" icon="refresh-cw">
    Run a manual sync after reconnecting or updating permissions.

  </Step>
</Steps>

| Sync error | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authorization failed | Token expired or was revoked | Reconnect the integration |
| Missing records | Field mapping changed | Update the mapping and resync |
| Duplicate updates | Two systems are editing the same data | Choose one source of truth |
| Delayed sync | Large queue or temporary outage | Wait and try again later |

<Callout kind="info">
  After you reconnect, give the sync a few minutes to finish before checking the final result.
</Callout>

## Voice quality issues

If calls sound choppy, delayed, or distorted, check network quality and device settings first. Voice quality problems often come from low bandwidth, packet loss, or audio hardware issues.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your network" icon="wifi">
    Use a stable connection and avoid heavy downloads or video streams during calls.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the headset or microphone" icon="mic">
    Try a different device and confirm the microphone is not muted or blocked by system settings.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Close extra apps" icon="x-circle">
    Shut down apps that may use bandwidth, camera, or microphone resources.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Retest on another device" icon="monitor">
    If the problem disappears, the issue is likely local to the original device or browser.

  </Step>
</Steps>

| Voice symptom | Common cause | What to try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Choppy audio | Weak network | Move to a stronger connection |
| Echo | Open speakers or multiple audio devices | Use headphones |
| Delay or lag | High latency | Reduce network load |
| Muffled sound | Bad mic position or hardware issue | Reposition or replace the headset |

<Expandable title="What should I check first for voice problems?" default-open="false">
  Start with the network, then test the headset, then try the call on another device.

</Expandable>

## Still stuck?

If you still cannot resolve the issue, contact Zendesk support with the exact error message, a short description of what you tried, and the time the problem occurred.
