---
title: Common Issues
description: Fix frequent problems like connection errors, audio glitches, and app crashes.
---

<Callout kind="info">
If these steps do not fix the problem, reach out to @discord_support for more help.
</Callout>

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Voice Connections" icon="mic">

<Expandable title='Voice channel shows "Failed to connect"' default-open="false">

Problem: The app displays "Failed to connect to voice channel" and you cannot join.

Cause: Network restrictions or temporary server hiccups block the connection.

Fix:

Check your internet connection first. Restart your router, then try these steps:

- Close and reopen Discord.
- Switch to a different voice region in the channel settings.
- Disable any VPN you have running.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title='No sound or audio glitches during calls' default-open="false">

Problem: Friends can hear you but you hear nothing, or audio cuts in and out.

Cause: The wrong playback device is selected or a driver conflict exists.

Fix:

Open User Settings, go to Voice & Video, and confirm the correct output device appears. Test with the built-in microphone test. If the issue continues, update your sound drivers from the manufacturer site.

</Expandable>

</Tab>
<Tab title="App Crashes" icon="alert-triangle">

<Expandable title='Discord closes immediately on launch' default-open="false">

Problem: The window opens then disappears with no error shown.

Cause: Corrupted cache files or a recent update conflict.

Fix:

Hold Shift while clicking the Discord icon to open the app in safe mode. If it starts, clear the cache from the settings menu. Reinstall the app only if safe mode fails.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title='Crash with message "Discord has stopped working"' default-open="false">

Problem: Windows shows the stopped working dialog during normal use.

Cause: Outdated graphics drivers or overlay interference.

Fix:

Update your graphics drivers. Disable hardware acceleration in Discord settings under Appearance. Restart the computer after changes.

</Expandable>

</Tab>
<Tab title="Login Problems" icon="log-in">

<Expandable title='Login fails with "Invalid token"' default-open="false">

Problem: The error "Invalid token" appears after entering credentials.

Cause: The saved login data is corrupted or your password changed elsewhere.

Fix:

Log out completely, then log back in with your email and current password. If the error stays, reset your password from the login screen.

</Expandable>

</Tab>
</Tabs>
