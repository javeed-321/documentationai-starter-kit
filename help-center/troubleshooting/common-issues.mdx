If your campaigns are not reaching inboxes or you see high bounce rates, this page shows the fastest fixes for delivery, spam filters, and bounce handling in Mailchimp.

## Improve email delivery

Follow these steps to raise your delivery rate.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify your domain">
    Go to Audience and confirm your sending domain has proper DNS records.

    Add the SPF, DKIM, and DMARC entries shown in Mailchimp.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Clean your list">
    Remove addresses that have not engaged in the last six months.

    Use the list health tools to flag inactive contacts before you send.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test before sending">
    Send a test campaign to a few real inboxes.

    Check the preview and spam-score report inside Mailchimp.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Warm up new domains slowly. Start with small sends and increase volume over several days.
</Callout>

## Handle bounces

Mailchimp separates bounces into two types. Use the table below to decide what to do.

| Bounce type | What it means | Action |
|-------------|---------------|--------|
| Hard bounce | Permanent failure such as invalid address | Remove the address from your audience |
| Soft bounce | Temporary issue such as full inbox | Mailchimp retries automatically; monitor after 72 hours |

<Expandable title="How do I remove bounced addresses?">
  Open the audience that received the bounce.

  Filter by bounce status and delete the contacts in bulk.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I resend to soft bounces?">
  Yes. Wait at least three days, then create a segment that excludes hard bounces only.

  Send a short re-engagement campaign to that segment.

</Expandable>

## Avoid spam filters

Spam filters block campaigns that look suspicious. Apply these checks before every send.

- Keep subject lines under 50 characters and avoid all caps or excessive punctuation.
- Include a physical mailing address and one-click unsubscribe link.
- Do not use spammy words such as "free money" or "act now."
- Maintain a spam complaint rate below 0.1 percent.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Repeated spam complaints can suspend your account. Review the abuse reports in your dashboard weekly.
</Callout>
