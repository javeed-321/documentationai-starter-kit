We know how frustrating it is when your Help Center isn't behaving as expected. Use the quick chec ks below to get things working again.

## Articles not publishing

<Expandable title="Articles not publishing" default-open="false">
  Your newly written article stays stuck in Draft and never shows up on the live site.

  **Common cause**\
  The article is saved but the parent collection has not been published, or the article itself is missing the final publish step.

  **Fix**

  1. Open the article in the editor.

  2. Click **Publish** at the top right.

  3. Go to the collection settings and make sure its status is set to **Live**.

  4. Refresh the Help Center URL; the article should now appear within five minutes.
</Expandable>

## Search not returning results

<Expandable title="Search not returning results" default-open="false">
  Visitors type a keyword that matches an article title, yet they see the message "No results found."

  **Common cause**\
  Search indexing has not finished or the article is excluded from search because its visibility is set to "Team only."

  **Fix**

  1. Wait up to 15 minutes after publishing; indexing runs automatically.

  2. Open the article settings and change Visibility to **Everyone**.

  3. In the collection settings, confirm Search is toggled on.

  4. Test again with an incognito window to rule out cached results.
</Expandable>

## Styling issues

<Expandable title="Styling issues" default-open="false">
  Custom CSS you added no longer applies, or the Help Center looks broken after an update.

  **Common cause**\
  The custom CSS file contains a syntax error, or the theme was switched without re-applying the overrides.

  **Fix**

  1. Go to **Settings → Appearance → Custom CSS**.

  2. Paste your rules into a validator and remove any unclosed brackets.

  3. Save, then hard-refresh the Help Center page (Ctrl + Shift + R).

  4. If the issue persists, switch back to the default theme temporarily to confirm the custom file is the source.
</Expandable>

## Access control problems

<Expandable title="Access control problems" default-open="false">
  Logged-in team members see "You do not have permission to view this collection" even though they should.

  **Common cause**\
  The collection's access setting restricts it to specific roles that the user does not have, or an old team permission is cached.

  **Fix**

  1. Open the collection settings and review the **Who can view** list.

  2. Add the missing team member or change the setting to **All team members**.

  3. Ask the affected user to log out and log back in to clear the permission cache.

  4. Test the Help Center link again from their account.
</Expandable>
