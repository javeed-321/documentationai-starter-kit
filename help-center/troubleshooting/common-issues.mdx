Calendar sync errors, double bookings, and missed reminders can disrupt your schedule. This page shows you how to fix them fast.

## Fix calendar sync failures

Reconnect your calendar when events stop appearing in Calendly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check connection status">
    Open your Calendly account and go to the calendar settings page.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconnect the calendar">
    Select the calendar that shows an error, then choose the reconnect option and follow the prompts to sign in again.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify sync">
    Create a test event in your calendar and confirm it appears in Calendly within a few minutes.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Sync works best when you connect no more than six calendars at once.
</Callout>

## Prevent double bookings

Double bookings happen when availability overlaps across calendars or event types.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review connected calendars">
    Make sure every calendar you use for availability is connected and set to check for conflicts.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Update event buffers">
    Add buffer time before and after each event type so meetings cannot overlap.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the booking link">
    Share your link with yourself and try to book two events back to back.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fix reminder delivery issues

Reminders may fail to send if notification settings are turned off or email addresses are incorrect.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check notification settings">
    Go to your event type settings and confirm that email and SMS reminders are enabled.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify contact details">
    Make sure the invitee email address is correct and that your own notification email has not changed.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend reminders">
    Open the scheduled event and use the resend option to trigger the reminder again.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="Why does my calendar show as disconnected?">
  Calendly loses connection when your calendar provider changes permissions or requires re-authentication. Reconnect the calendar using the steps above.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can two people book the same time slot?">
  No, when conflict checking is active across all connected calendars, Calendly blocks overlapping bookings automatically.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Why are reminders not arriving?">
  Check that reminders are enabled for the event type and that the recipient has not marked Calendly emails as spam.

</Expandable>
