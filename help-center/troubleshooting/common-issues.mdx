## Common Issues

We know these hiccups can stop you in your tracks. Each expandable below walks through a specific symptom, the usual cause inside Intercom, and the exact steps to get things working again.

<Expandable title="Messenger widget fails to load or display" default-open="false">
Problem: The Help Center widget does not appear on your site and the browser console shows `Intercom widget failed to initialize: ERR_INTERCOM_WIDGET_TIMEOUT`.

Cause: The Messenger snippet is missing the correct workspace ID or is blocked by a content security policy.

Fix: Open your site HTML and confirm the snippet contains your real workspace ID. Replace the placeholder with your value:

```html
<script>
  window.intercomSettings = { app_id: "YOUR_WORKSPACE_ID" };
</script>
<script async src="https://widget.intercom.io/widget/YOUR_WORKSPACE_ID"></script>
```

Save the file, clear your browser cache, and reload the page. The widget should appear within 30 seconds.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Article links break after migration" default-open="false">
Problem: Old links return a 404 page even though the articles still exist in Intercom.

Cause: Intercom changed the article slug format during the migration.

Fix: In Intercom, go to Help Center > Articles, open each migrated article, and copy the new URL from the share link. Update any external references to use the new slug. For bulk fixes, export the article list from Settings > Data > Export and run a find-and-replace on your site.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Search returns no results" default-open="false">
Problem: Typing a known article title into the Help Center search bar shows "No results found."

Cause: New or updated articles have not finished indexing yet.

Fix: Wait up to 15 minutes after publishing. Then open Help Center > Settings > Search and click "Rebuild index". Test the search again with the exact title. If results still fail to appear, add the article to a collection that is marked as searchable.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Styling conflicts with your theme" default-open="false">
Problem: Help Center text overlaps or colors clash with your site's CSS.

Cause: Your theme's stylesheet overrides Intercom's default classes.

Fix: In Help Center > Appearance > Custom CSS, add the following rule to restore readable contrast:

```css
.intercom-article-content {
  color: #333333 !important;
  font-family: inherit !important;
}
```

Publish the change and refresh the page. If the issue persists, inspect the element in your browser and add more specific selectors.
</Expandable>
