This page helps you fix common issues with tickets, AI agents, integrations, and channels in Zendesk.

## Ticket payment issues

Card declines and refund problems often stem from payment processor settings or ticket status.

<Expandable title="Fix a declined card on a ticket" default-open="true">
  Update the payment method and retry the charge.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open the ticket">
      Go to the ticket and check the payment section for the decline reason.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Update payment details">
      Ask the customer to provide a new card or payment method.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Process the charge again">
      Click Retry payment in the ticket actions menu.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

</Expandable>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Refunds process within 5 business days. Check the transaction log before contacting support.
</Callout>

## AI agent failures

AI agents may stop responding or give incorrect answers when training data is outdated.

<Expandable title="Restart an AI agent that stopped responding" default-open="false">
  Clear the agent cache and reload its configuration.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Pause the agent">
      In Agent settings, toggle the agent to paused.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Clear cache">
      Click Clear training cache under the AI tab.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Resume the agent">
      Toggle the agent back to active and test with a sample query.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

</Expandable>

## Integration errors

Integration errors appear when API keys expire or scopes change.

Check the table below for common error codes.

| Error code | Cause                  | Quick fix                     |
|------------|------------------------|-------------------------------|
| 401        | Invalid API key        | Regenerate the key            |
| 403        | Missing scopes         | Update scopes in the app      |
| 429        | Rate limit reached     | Wait 60 seconds and retry     |

<Expandable title="Reconnect an integration after an error" default-open="false">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Revoke the old connection">
      Go to Admin > Integrations and remove the failed app.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Re-authorize">
      Click Add integration and enter fresh credentials.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Test the connection">
      Send a test ticket through the integration channel.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

</Expandable>

## Channel connectivity

Channels lose connection when webhooks or network settings change.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Never delete a channel while tickets are open. Close or transfer tickets first.
</Callout>

<Expandable title="Restore a disconnected channel" default-open="false">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check webhook status">
      In Channel settings, look for a red status indicator.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Update endpoint URL">
      Replace the webhook URL with the current one from your site.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Verify connectivity">
      Click Test connection and confirm a 200 response.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

</Expandable>

Still stuck? Contact Zendesk support from the Help menu in your account.
