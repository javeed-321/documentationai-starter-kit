---
title: Common scheduling issues
description: Resolve calendar sync failures, double bookings, and link errors.
---

If a scheduling problem blocks your day, start here. Each section shows the exact symptom you see, why it happens in Calendly, and the quickest fix.

<Expandable title="Calendar failed to sync" default-open="false">
  **Problem**  
  After you connect Google Calendar or Outlook, Calendly shows:  
  `Calendar failed to sync. Please reconnect your account.`

  **Cause**  
  The connected calendar revoked access or hit a temporary API limit.

  **Fix**

  1. Go to Account → Calendar Connections.
  2. Click Reconnect next to the failing calendar.
  3. Grant Calendly access again when prompted.
  4. Wait two minutes, then refresh the page.

  If the error returns, remove the calendar completely and add it again.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Events appear in the wrong time zone" default-open="false">
  **Problem**  
  A booking shows at 9 a.m. on your calendar but 2 p.m. for the invitee.

  **Cause**  
  Calendly reads the time zone from your connected calendar, but the event type still uses a different default.

  **Fix**

  1. Open the event type → Show more settings → Time zone.
  2. Choose "Use invitee's time zone" or set it to match your primary calendar.
  3. Save and test with a new booking link.

  The change applies to future bookings only.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Double booking occurred" default-open="false">
  **Problem**  
  Two people booked the same 30-minute slot even though the calendar showed it free.

  **Cause**  
  Calendly checks availability every few minutes. A last-second meeting added outside Calendly was not yet synced.

  **Fix**

  1. Turn on "Check for conflicts" in the event type settings.
  2. Increase the buffer time to at least 15 minutes.
  3. In your connected calendar, create a recurring "busy" block that matches your Calendly hours.

  These steps reduce the chance of overlap while sync catches up.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Booking link shows no times available" default-open="false">
  **Problem**  
  Visitors see "No times available" even though your calendar is clear.

  **Cause**  
  The event type duration or buffer exceeds the remaining free blocks, or the date range is too narrow.

  **Fix**

  1. Edit the event type → Duration and buffers.
  2. Shorten the meeting length or reduce buffer time.
  3. Extend the date range under "When can people book" to at least 30 days.

  Save and share the updated link.

</Expandable>
