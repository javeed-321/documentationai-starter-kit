If Fin gives incorrect answers or fails to escalate conversations, this page shows the quickest fixes.

## Incorrect answers

Check these settings first.

<Expandable title="Fin cites the wrong source">
Update your knowledge base with the latest articles, then retrain Fin on those pages.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Answers feel generic or off-topic">
Add more specific product details and examples to your help content. Test one new article at a time.

</Expandable>

## Escalation failures

Use these steps when Fin does not hand off to a human.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify escalation rules">
    Open Settings > Fin > Escalation and confirm at least one rule matches your current conditions.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check team availability">
    Make sure agents are online and the correct inbox is selected in the rule.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the handoff">
    Start a new test conversation and type a phrase that should trigger escalation.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
Rules run in the order listed. Move the most important rule to the top.

</Callout>

## Performance tuning

Adjust these options to improve speed and accuracy.

| Setting              | Recommended value       | Effect                              |
|----------------------|-------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Response length      | Medium                  | Faster replies, fewer tokens used   |
| Knowledge scope      | Latest 30 days only     | Reduces outdated citations          |
| Confidence threshold | 75%                     | Fewer low-quality answers           |

<Callout kind="warning">
Lowering the threshold too far increases incorrect answers.

</Callout>
