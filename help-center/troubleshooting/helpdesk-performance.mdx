If the Helpdesk loads slowly, messages disappear, or notifications fail, this page gives the quickest fixes.

## Inbox delays

Slow inbox loading usually clears after you refresh your connection.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear your cache">
    Open your browser settings and clear cached images and files for the last 24 hours.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your internet">
    Run a speed test. You need at least 5 Mbps download for smooth Helpdesk use.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch networks">
    Move from Wi-Fi to a wired connection or try mobile data.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notification settings

Missing alerts often trace back to browser or workspace settings.

<Expandable title="How do I turn notifications back on?">
  1. Click your avatar in the bottom left.
  2. Choose Preferences.
  3. Toggle Desktop notifications to On.

</Expandable>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Allow notifications in your browser when prompted. Blocked sites stay silent.

</Callout>

## Browser compatibility

The Helpdesk runs best on current versions of these browsers.

| Browser   | Minimum version | Notes                     |
|-----------|-----------------|---------------------------|
| Chrome    | 120+            | Recommended               |
| Firefox   | 120+            | Works well                |
| Safari    | 17+             | macOS only                |
| Edge      | 120+            | Chromium-based version    |

Update your browser if the version is older. Older browsers drop messages or show blank screens.
