## Voice and video not working as expected

If your voice chat cuts out, video stays blurry, or screen sharing fails, this page walks you through the fastest fixes.

## Improve audio quality

Follow these steps to clear up choppy or low-quality sound.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your connection">
    Move closer to your router or switch to a wired connection.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust input sensitivity">
    Open User Settings, select Voice & Video, and lower the input sensitivity slider until your mic picks up only your voice.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable noise suppression">
    In the same Voice & Video menu, turn on Krisp or Discord's built-in suppression.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Restart Discord after changing these settings so the new values take effect immediately.
</Callout>

## Set up your mic and camera

<Steps>
  <Step title="Grant permissions">
    Make sure Discord has access to your microphone and camera in your device settings.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the right devices">
    Go to User Settings > Voice & Video and choose your preferred mic and camera from the dropdowns.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the hardware">
    Click the microphone or camera test buttons to confirm both are working before joining a call.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change server voice region

Poor voice quality often improves when you switch regions.

1. Open Server Settings.
2. Select Voice Regions.
3. Pick the region closest to most members.

| Region       | Best for                  |
| ------------ | ------------------------- |
| US Central   | North America             |
| Europe       | Western and Central Europe|
| Asia         | East and Southeast Asia   |
| Australia    | Oceania                   |

## Common questions

<Expandable title="Why does my voice sound robotic?">
  High latency or packet loss usually causes this. Switch to a closer voice region or use a wired connection.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Screen share has no audio">
  Make sure you select the correct application window and enable the "Share audio" toggle when starting the share.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Camera stays off for others">
  Confirm your camera is not in use by another app and that video is enabled in the call controls.

</Expandable>
