## Common Delivery Problems

Use the expanders below to match your exact symptom. Each one shows the cause and the quickest fix inside Mailchimp.

<Expandable title="Campaigns land in spam or promotions tabs" default-open="false">
  You see low open rates and complaints that messages appear in spam folders instead of the inbox.

  **Cause**  
  Sender reputation dropped after repeated spam filter triggers or missing authentication records.

  **Fix**  
  Go to Audience → Settings → Sender authentication and confirm DKIM and SPF are both active. Then run a test send from Campaigns → Create campaign and check the Spam filter score. Aim for a score under 2.0 before sending to the full list.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Hard bounces exceed 5 percent" default-open="false">
  Your campaign report shows the error "550 5.1.1 Recipient address rejected: User unknown" for many contacts.

  **Cause**  
  Outdated or invalid email addresses remain in the audience after previous sends.

  **Fix**  
  Open the audience that failed, choose Manage contacts → Clean up, then select Remove hard bounces. After cleanup, re-import only verified addresses and enable the "Remove invalid emails automatically" setting under Audience → Settings.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Soft bounces block the entire send" default-open="false">
  The campaign status shows "Failed" with the message "Temporary delivery failure: mailbox full" repeated across the report.

  **Cause**  
  Too many temporary mailbox issues caused Mailchimp to pause the send to protect your reputation.

  **Fix**  
  From the campaign report, select Retry soft bounces. Then reduce send frequency for that audience and add a one-click unsubscribe link at the top of future emails to lower future soft-bounce volume.

</Expandable>
