## Plan upgrades

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I upgrade my plan?" default-open="true">

You can upgrade from your Shopify admin at any time. Go to Settings, then Plan, and choose the option that fits your current sales volume. Your new plan starts right away, and you only pay the difference for the rest of the billing cycle.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Will I lose my data or settings if I upgrade?">

No. All your products, orders, and customizations stay exactly where they are. Upgrading simply unlocks more features and higher limits.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Transaction fees

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="What transaction fees does Shopify charge?" default-open="true">

Fees depend on your plan. Basic, Shopify, and Advanced plans charge between 0.5% and 2% on every sale processed outside Shopify Payments. Shopify Plus has no additional transaction fees.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Do I pay fees on every order?">

You pay the fee only when a customer pays with a third-party gateway. Orders paid through Shopify Payments skip the extra fee.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Shopify Payments payouts

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="When will I receive my payout?" default-open="true">

Payouts normally arrive in your bank account within 1–3 business days after the order ships. You can check the exact schedule in Settings, then Payments.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Why is my payout delayed?">

Delays usually happen when an order needs extra verification or when your bank is processing a weekend or holiday. Check the Payments section for any alerts or required documents.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Refunds and credits

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I issue a refund?" default-open="true">

Open the order in your admin, click Refund, enter the amount, and choose whether to return it to the original payment method. The refund appears on the customer's statement within a few days.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I get a credit for unused months?">

Shopify does not prorate or refund unused time when you downgrade. You keep access until the end of the paid period, then move to the lower plan automatically.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
