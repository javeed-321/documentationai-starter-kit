## Embedding the Widget

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I add the Help Center widget to my website?" default-open="true">

Copy the embed code from your Intercom settings and paste it into the head section of your site. Refresh the page to see the widget appear in the corner you chose.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="The widget does not show up after I embed the code">

Double-check that the code sits inside the correct site template and that no ad blocker or content security policy is stripping the script. Test in an incognito window to rule out extensions.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Setting Permissions and Visibility

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Who can see my Help Center articles?" default-open="true">

You control visibility in the article settings. Choose public, team only, or specific user segments. Changes take effect immediately after you save.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I hide an article from search results">

Open the article, toggle the visibility switch to private, and save. The article stays in your workspace but no longer appears to visitors.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Importing Existing Articles

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Can I bring in articles from another platform?" default-open="true">

Yes. Export your current articles as HTML or Markdown, then use the import tool in Intercom settings. Map the fields once and the system brings everything over.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What happens to images and formatting during import">

Intercom keeps your images and basic formatting. Review each article after import and re-upload any images that did not transfer.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Localization and Multilingual Support

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I offer the Help Center in more than one language?" default-open="true">

Create a new language version from the localization tab. Translate the articles, then set visitor language detection so the right version loads automatically.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can visitors switch languages themselves">

Yes. Turn on the language switcher in your widget settings. Visitors see a small menu and can pick their preferred language at any time.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
