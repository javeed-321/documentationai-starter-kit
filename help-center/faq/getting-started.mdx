## Initial Setup

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I get started with Intercom?" default-open="true">
    Sign in to your account and follow the guided setup. Add your first help articles, then publish the center so visitors can find answers right away.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What should I prepare before setup?">
    Gather your existing articles, decide on categories, and choose a simple layout. This keeps the first launch quick and useful for your users.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Custom Domain Configuration

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Can I use my own domain for the help center?" default-open="true">
    Yes. Go to settings, enter your domain, and update the DNS records we provide. The change usually takes effect within a few hours.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What if the domain does not show after setup?">
    Double-check the DNS entries and wait a little longer for propagation. If the issue continues, contact support with your domain details.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Article Visibility Settings

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I control who sees each article?" default-open="true">
    Open any article, set its visibility to public, internal, or restricted, then save. Changes apply immediately to new visitors.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I hide draft articles from everyone?">
    Yes. Keep the article in draft status until you are ready. Only team members with edit access can view drafts.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## User Roles and Access

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What roles are available and what can they do?" default-open="true">
    Admins manage settings and users. Editors create and update articles. Viewers can only read published content. Assign roles in team settings.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I give a teammate access without full admin rights?">
    Invite them as an editor. They receive an email link and can start working right after accepting the invitation.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
