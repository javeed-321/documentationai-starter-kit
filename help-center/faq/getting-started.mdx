## Account setup

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I create my Intercom account?" default-open="true">
    Go to the sign-up page and enter your work email. You will receive a confirmation link to verify your address and set a password. The process takes just a few minutes.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What details are required during setup?">
    Provide your company name, team size, and a short description of how you plan to use the help center. These details help tailor your first workspace.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Installing the messenger

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where do I find the install code?" default-open="true">
    After you finish account setup, open the messenger settings page. Copy the snippet shown there and paste it into the head section of your website.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What if the messenger does not appear?">
    Double-check that the code sits in every page of your site. Clear your browser cache and reload. If the issue continues, confirm your site domain is listed in the allowed origins.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Basic configuration

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I set a custom greeting?" default-open="true">
    Navigate to the messenger appearance settings. Enter your welcome message in the greeting field and save the change. Visitors will see it on their next visit.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I change the messenger color?">
    Yes. In the same appearance panel, pick a new color from the palette or enter a hex value. The update shows instantly in the preview.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
