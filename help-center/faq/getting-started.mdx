## Initial Portal Setup

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I create my first Help Center?" default-open="true">

Sign in to your Zendesk account and navigate to the Help Center section. Click the option to create a new portal, then choose a template that matches your brand. Add your company name and logo to personalize the site before publishing.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What settings should I review before launch?">

Check your visibility options to decide if the Help Center is public or restricted. Review the default article layout and enable search features so visitors can find answers quickly.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Custom Domain Mapping

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I connect my own domain to the Help Center?" default-open="true">

Go to the branding settings and enter your custom domain name. Update your DNS records by adding a CNAME entry that points to the Zendesk servers. Once the changes propagate, test the domain to confirm it loads your Help Center.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What happens if the domain does not resolve?">

Double-check that the CNAME record matches exactly and allow up to 48 hours for full DNS propagation. Contact your domain registrar if the records appear correct but the site still fails to load.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## User Roles and Permissions

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Who can edit articles in the Help Center?" default-open="true">

Admins and agents with content permissions can create and update articles. End users only see published content unless you grant them specific access through team settings.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I limit editing rights to certain team members?">

Assign roles in the people section of your Zendesk dashboard. Create custom groups and apply permission rules so only approved users can publish or modify content.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Multilingual Content

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I add support for multiple languages?" default-open="true">

Open the content settings and enable additional languages from the available list. Translate your articles directly in the editor or import translated files. Visitors can then switch languages using the selector on your site.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What is the best way to keep translations up to date?">

Assign native speakers to each language version and set up review workflows. Use the version history to track changes and ensure every language stays current with the original content.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
