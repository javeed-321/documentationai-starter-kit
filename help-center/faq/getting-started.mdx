## Initial setup steps

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I create my first Help Center?" default-open="true">

Go to your Intercom workspace, select Help Center from the menu, and click Create new site. Choose a name and domain, then follow the prompts to add your first collection.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What information do I need before starting?">

Gather your company logo, a short welcome message, and a list of the main topics your customers ask about most often.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Article publishing

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I publish an article?" default-open="true">

Write the article in the editor, add it to a collection, then click Publish. Your changes go live immediately for visitors.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I preview an article before publishing?">

Yes. Use the Preview button at the top of the editor to see exactly how the article will appear to customers.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Search functionality

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Why aren't my articles appearing in search?" default-open="true">

Make sure each article has a clear title and at least one relevant keyword in the first paragraph. Re-index the Help Center after major changes.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How can I improve search results?">

Add synonyms and common customer phrases to article content. Test searches yourself and adjust titles or headings as needed.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Mobile responsiveness

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Does the Help Center work on phones and tablets?" default-open="true">

Yes. The design automatically adjusts to fit any screen size so customers can read articles comfortably on mobile devices.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What should I check on mobile after publishing?">

Open the Help Center on a phone, scroll through a few articles, and confirm that images resize and text remains readable without zooming.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
