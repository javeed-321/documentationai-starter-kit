## Account Creation

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I create my first Intercom account?" default-open="true">

Visit the signup page and enter your work email. You will receive a verification link within minutes. Click it to activate your workspace and complete the initial setup questions.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What if the verification email never arrives?">

Check your spam folder first. If it is not there, request a new link from the signup screen. Contact support only after waiting 30 minutes.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I use a personal email address?">

A work email is required so Intercom can connect your team later. Personal addresses trigger extra verification steps.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Team Permissions

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I invite teammates?" default-open="true">

Go to Settings then Team. Enter their email addresses and choose a role. They receive an invite link that expires in seven days.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What roles are available and what can each do?">

Admins manage billing and settings. Agents reply to conversations. Viewers can only read reports. Start with the Agent role for most teammates.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="A teammate cannot see certain conversations.">

Confirm their role has access to the correct inbox. Admins can adjust inbox permissions under Settings then Inboxes.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Data Import

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I import existing contacts?" default-open="true">

Export your contacts as CSV from your current tool. In Intercom go to Customers then Import. Map the columns and start the upload.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What happens if some rows fail to import?">

Intercom shows a list of failed rows with reasons. Fix the CSV and re-upload only the failed rows.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I import past conversation history?">

Only basic contact data imports at this stage. Full conversation history requires a separate migration request through support.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Messenger Customization

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I change the messenger color and greeting?" default-open="true">

Open Settings then Messenger. Pick a brand color, upload your logo, and write a short welcome message. Save and the changes appear immediately.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="The messenger does not show on my website.">

Check that the install code is on every page. Use your browser console to confirm the script loads without errors.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I hide the messenger on specific pages?">

Add a small code snippet that sets `Intercom('hide')` on those pages. Restore visibility with `Intercom('show')` when needed.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
