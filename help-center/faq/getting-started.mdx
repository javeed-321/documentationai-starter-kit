## Choosing the right plan

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Which plan is best when you are just starting out?" default-open="true">

Start with the Basic plan. It includes everything you need to launch, such as a website, checkout, and basic reports. You can upgrade anytime as your sales grow.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can you switch plans later without losing your work?">

Yes. Upgrading or downgrading keeps your products, orders, and settings intact. Shopify moves your store to the new plan automatically.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Setting up your first store

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How long does it take to create a store?" default-open="true">

You can have a working store in under an hour. Sign up, pick a free theme, add your logo, and publish. No coding is required.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Do you need a custom domain right away?">

No. Shopify gives you a free `.myshopify.com` address to start. Connect a custom domain later through the Domains section in your admin.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Adding products and themes

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="What is the fastest way to add your first products?" default-open="true">

Go to Products in the admin and click Add product. Fill in the title, description, price, and upload photos. Save and the item appears in your store immediately.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can you change themes without affecting your products?">

Yes. Switching themes only changes the look of your store. All products, collections, and settings stay exactly where they are.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Configuring payments and shipping

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do you accept payments on day one?" default-open="true">

Shopify Payments is already built in. Turn it on in Settings > Payments, verify your business details, and start accepting credit cards right away. No extra account needed.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do you set up shipping rates for customers?">

Go to Settings > Shipping and delivery. Create rates based on weight, price, or location. You can offer free shipping, flat rates, or calculated carrier rates.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
