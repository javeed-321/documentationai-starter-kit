## Common questions about getting started

If you are trying to compare plans, join the startup program, move data into Zendesk, or upgrade your plan, this page gives you the shortest path to the answer.

## Pricing tiers

Zendesk offers several pricing tiers so you can choose the level of support, automation, and reporting you need. The exact names and included features can change, so the safest way to confirm current pricing is to check the plan picker in your account or the Zendesk pricing page.

| What you need | Best fit | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic ticketing and shared inbox | Entry-level plan | Ticket channels, workflow limits, reporting |
| More automation and analytics | Mid-tier plan | Triggers, views, dashboards, roles |
| Advanced control and scale | Higher-tier plan | Permissions, governance, advanced reporting |

<Callout kind="tip">
If you are choosing a plan for the first time, start with the smallest tier that covers your required channels and automations. You can upgrade later without rebuilding your workspace.
</Callout>

## Startup program

The startup program is designed to help early-stage teams get started with Zendesk more easily. Eligibility, discounts, and included services depend on the current program rules.

<Expandable title="How do I know if my company qualifies?" default-open="false">
You usually need to meet the program's current startup criteria, such as company stage, size, or funding status. Check the program requirements in your Zendesk account or with Zendesk support.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What should I prepare before applying?" default-open="false">
Have your company details ready, including your business name, website, and a short description of how you plan to use Zendesk. If the program asks for proof of eligibility, gather that before you submit.
</Expandable>

<Callout kind="info">
If you are unsure whether to apply now or after setup, collect your account details first. That makes it easier to move quickly once your eligibility is confirmed.
</Callout>

## Data migration

Moving data into Zendesk usually means bringing over users, tickets, knowledge content, and other support records from your previous system. The best approach depends on how much data you have and which objects you need to preserve.

<Steps>
  <Step title="List the data you need to move" icon="check-circle">
    Decide which records matter most, such as users, ticket history, macros, help center content, and custom fields.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Match your source data to Zendesk fields" icon="settings">
    Compare your old system's fields with the Zendesk fields you plan to use. This helps you avoid missing or duplicate information.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a migration method" icon="upload">
    For small moves, you may be able to import data manually. For larger or more complex migrations, use a partner, integration, or migration tool.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with a small batch" icon="zap">
    Import a limited set of records first. Check formatting, attachments, ticket links, and user ownership before you move everything.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the full migration" icon="rocket">
    After the test looks right, complete the full import and verify that your team can find the records they need.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
Before you migrate, back up your source data. Some imports can overwrite existing records if fields map incorrectly.
</Callout>

## Plan upgrades

You can upgrade when you need more agents, more automation, better reporting, or advanced governance features. Upgrades are usually the fastest way to unlock new functionality without starting over.

<Expandable title="When should I upgrade?" default-open="false">
Upgrade when your team hits plan limits, needs features that are not available on your current tier, or wants better control over workflows and reporting.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Will my existing setup be lost?" default-open="false">
In most cases, your core setup stays in place. Your plan changes, but your tickets, users, and existing configuration remain available unless a feature depends on the old plan.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What should I check before upgrading?" default-open="false">
Review seat counts, billing cycle timing, feature differences, and any add-ons you already use. Make sure the new plan includes the channels and permissions your team needs.
</Expandable>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your current plan" icon="book-open">
    Check which features you use today so you can confirm the new plan covers them.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare available plans" icon="bar-chart">
    Look for changes in automation, reporting, support channels, and admin controls.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm billing impact" icon="settings">
    Check whether the upgrade changes your seat count, renewal date, or payment schedule.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the upgrade" icon="arrow-right">
    Make the change from your account settings or through your account team, depending on how your subscription is managed.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common setup questions

<Expandable title="How long does setup usually take?" default-open="false">
Basic setup can take only a short time, but a full rollout with channels, automations, and migration work usually takes longer. Plan extra time if you are moving historical data.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I start small and expand later?" default-open="false">
Yes. Many teams begin with the essentials and add channels, automation, and advanced reporting as they grow.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Where should I go if I cannot find my billing or plan details?" default-open="false">
Check your account settings first. If the information is not there, contact Zendesk support or your account team for help.
</Expandable>

Still stuck? Contact Zendesk support or your account team for help with billing, migration, or plan changes.
