## Initial platform setup

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I create my first workspace?" default-open="true">
    Sign up for a free trial, then follow the guided setup to name your workspace and connect your first channel. The process takes just a few minutes.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What details do I need before starting?">
    Have your company name, support email, and a list of the channels you want to connect ready. This helps Fin learn your brand voice quickly.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Fin AI Agent training

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How long does it take to train Fin?" default-open="true">
    Fin starts helping immediately using your existing help articles. You can improve accuracy by adding more content over the first few days.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I review Fin's answers before they go live?">
    Yes. Use review mode to see every suggested reply and approve or edit responses until you feel confident.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Team permissions

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Who can access sensitive customer data?" default-open="true">
    Admins control permissions by role. You can limit data access to specific teams or individuals at any time.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I add a teammate?">
    Go to Team settings, invite the person by email, and assign the right role. They receive an email with setup instructions.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Core integrations

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Which tools connect with Intercom out of the box?" default-open="true">
    Intercom connects with Slack, Salesforce, Stripe, and many more. Browse the full list inside your workspace under Integrations.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What if my tool is not listed?">
    Use the open API or Zapier to build a custom connection. Most teams set this up in under an hour.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
