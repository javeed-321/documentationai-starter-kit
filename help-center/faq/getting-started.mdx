## AI Agent Deployment

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I deploy my first AI agent?" default-open="true">

Start in the AI section of your dashboard. Choose an agent template that matches your common ticket types, connect it to your knowledge base, and run the built-in test mode. Once results look accurate, turn the agent live on your chosen channels.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How long does training usually take?">

Most teams see reliable answers after uploading recent resolved tickets and running a few test conversations. Full rollout across channels often completes within one to two weeks.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Connecting Messaging and Voice Channels

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I add messaging channels?" default-open="true">

Go to Channels, select Messaging, and follow the prompts to link web chat, mobile, or social accounts. Each channel appears in the same unified inbox after connection.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I route voice calls into the same tickets?">

Yes. Enable the Voice add-on, then map incoming numbers to queues. Calls create tickets automatically and agents can handle them alongside other channels.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## User Permissions and Roles

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I set up roles for my team?" default-open="true">

Open Admin Center, choose People, then Roles. Create custom roles or use the built-in ones such as Agent and Admin. Assign permissions for tickets, views, and AI settings per role.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I limit who edits AI agent settings?">

Assign the AI Admin role only to users who need to train or publish agents. All other team members keep standard agent access.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Billing and Subscription Plans

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where can I see my current plan and usage?" default-open="true">

Select Billing from the account menu. The overview shows your plan tier, seat count, add-ons, and monthly usage for tickets and AI resolutions.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I change or cancel my subscription?">

Still in Billing, choose Manage Plan. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
