## Account setup and team onboarding

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I create my Intercom account?" default-open="true">
Sign up at the Intercom website with your email. Verify your address, then choose a workspace name that matches your product or company.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I invite my team members?">
Go to Settings, select Team, and enter their email addresses. Assign roles like Admin or Agent based on what they need to do.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What if my team cannot access the workspace?">
Check that invitations were sent to the correct emails and that members accepted them. Confirm their role allows login.
</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Installing Intercom on your website

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where do I find my install code?" default-open="true">
Open Settings, choose Installation, and copy the snippet shown there. Paste it into the head of every page on your site.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="The messenger does not appear after install. What now?">
Clear your browser cache and reload the page. Make sure the snippet sits in the correct spot and no ad blockers interfere.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I install on a site builder like WordPress?">
Yes. Add the snippet through your theme's header file or a custom HTML block. Test on a staging site first.
</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Creating your first help articles

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I start writing help content?" default-open="true">
Go to Help Center in the sidebar. Click New Article, give it a clear title, and write step-by-step instructions in simple language.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Where should I place new articles?">
Create collections that group related topics. Place the most common questions at the top so visitors find answers quickly.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can visitors search my help articles?">
Yes. The search bar works as soon as you publish the first article. Keep titles short and descriptive to improve results.
</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Pricing and plans overview

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Which plan should I choose to get started?" default-open="true">
Begin with the entry plan that includes the messenger and basic help center. Upgrade later when you need advanced reporting or more seats.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Are there limits on the free trial?">
The trial gives full access for 14 days. You can export your data at any time if you decide not to continue.
</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I change plans later?">
Visit Billing in Settings. Select the new plan and confirm. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle.
</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
