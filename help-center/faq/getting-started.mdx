## Initial store setup

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I create my first Shopify store?" default-open="true">

Sign up at the Shopify website with your email address. Choose a plan that fits your business size, then follow the guided setup wizard to name your store and select a free theme.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What information do I need before I start?">

Gather your business name, address, and product details. You will also need a payment method for your Shopify subscription.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Product uploads

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I add my first product?" default-open="true">

Go to the Products section in your Shopify admin. Click Add product, fill in the title, description, price, and upload images. Save the product to make it visible in your store.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I upload multiple products at once?">

Yes. Use the bulk import feature by downloading a CSV template, adding your product data, and uploading the file back into Shopify.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Payment gateways

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Which payment options are available?" default-open="true">

Shopify Payments is built in for many countries. You can also connect third-party gateways such as PayPal or Stripe through the Payments settings page.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I set up Shopify Payments?">

Navigate to Settings then Payments. Click Complete account setup and enter your business and banking details. Shopify reviews the information and activates the gateway.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Domain configuration

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I connect my own domain name?" default-open="true">

Go to Settings then Domains. Click Connect existing domain and enter your domain. Follow the prompts to update the DNS records at your domain registrar.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What if I want Shopify to host my domain?">

Buy a new domain directly inside the Shopify admin under Settings then Domains. Shopify automatically configures everything for you.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
