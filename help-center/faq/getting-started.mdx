## Account Creation

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I create a Shopify account?" default-open="true">

Visit the Shopify website and click the Start free trial button. Enter your email address, store name, and password to begin. Shopify sends a confirmation link to your inbox. Click it to activate your account and access the admin dashboard.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What information do I need to provide during signup?">

You need an email address, a store name, and a password. Shopify also asks for your business location to suggest the right plan and features. No credit card is required to start the trial.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Plan Selection

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Which plan should I choose when starting?" default-open="true">

Start with the Basic plan if you sell fewer than 50 products and ship to one country. Upgrade later as your sales grow. The trial lets you test all features before deciding.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I change plans after I launch?">

Yes. Go to Settings then Plan in your admin dashboard. Select a new plan and confirm the change. Shopify prorates the cost so you only pay the difference.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Domain Setup

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I connect my own domain?" default-open="true">

Go to Settings then Domains. Click Connect existing domain and enter your domain name. Follow the prompts to update your DNS records at your registrar. Changes usually take a few hours to appear.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What if I do not have a domain yet?">

Shopify lets you buy one directly inside the admin under Settings then Domains. Search for an available name, add it to your cart, and complete the purchase. Shopify automatically connects it to your store.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Initial Product Import

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I add my first products?" default-open="true">

From the Products page, click Add product. Fill in the title, description, price, and upload images. Click Save when finished. Repeat for each item or use bulk import for larger catalogs.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I import products from a spreadsheet?">

Yes. Prepare a CSV file with your product details and go to Products then Import. Upload the file and map the columns. Shopify reviews the data before adding the products to your store.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
