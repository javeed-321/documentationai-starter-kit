## Connect your address

Go to **Settings → Channels → Email** and add a forwarding address. Incoming
messages become conversations your team can reply to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a forwarding address">
    Forward your support inbox to the address we generate for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify delivery">
    Send a test email and confirm it appears in your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Replies are sent from your connected address, so customers see your brand.
</Callout>
