---
title: Chat channel
description: Add live chat to your site so visitors can reach you instantly.
---

## Install the widget

Copy the snippet from **Settings → Channels → Chat** and paste it before the
closing `</body>` tag on your site.

## Set availability

Define your team's hours so visitors know when to expect a reply. Outside those
hours, chats are captured as conversations for follow-up.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Pair chat with [Workflows](help-center/product/automation/workflows) to auto-route
  new conversations.
</Callout>
