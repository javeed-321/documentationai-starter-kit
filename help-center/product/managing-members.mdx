---
title: Managing members
description: Add, remove, and manage roles for people in your workspace.
---

## Change a role

Open **Settings → Members**, find the person, and pick a new role from the
dropdown. Changes take effect immediately.

## Remove a member

Removing a member revokes their access right away. Their published articles stay
in the workspace.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  You can't remove the last remaining Admin — promote someone else first.
</Callout>
