## Why macros and automation matter

You spend too much time typing the same answers and routing the same tickets. This page shows you how to set up saved replies, build workflows, and let rules improve themselves so your team handles more conversations with less effort.

## Create a saved reply

Saved replies let you insert common answers in one click.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the reply editor">
    In any conversation, click the reply box then select **Saved replies** from the toolbar.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and save the reply">
    Type your message. Give it a short name like "Refund policy" and click **Save**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in future chats">
    Start typing the name or browse the list and select it. The text appears instantly.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Build a workflow

The Workflow builder turns repeated actions into automatic steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Automation">
    From the Helpdesk sidebar, choose **Workflows** then click **New workflow**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the trigger">
    Pick what starts the workflow, such as "New conversation tagged Billing".

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add actions">
    Choose steps like "Assign to team", "Send saved reply", or "Update priority". Drag to reorder.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Name the workflow and toggle it to **Active**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Test the workflow on a sample conversation before making it live for everyone.

</Callout>

## Let rules improve themselves

Self-improving rules watch how your team edits or overrides automated actions and suggest better versions over time.

<Expandable title="How do self-improving rules start working?">
  Enable the feature in Workflow settings. The system begins learning after 50 manual changes to the same action.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I turn off suggestions for one rule?">
  Yes. Open the rule, click the three-dot menu, and choose **Pause learning**.

</Expandable>

## Quick comparison

| Feature            | What it does                          | Best for                     |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Saved replies      | Inserts pre-written text              | Common questions             |
| Workflows          | Runs a sequence of actions            | Routing and follow-ups       |
| Self-improving rules | Suggests updates based on edits     | Teams that tweak rules often |
