## Find your way around the inbox

The inbox is the central workspace for every conversation. This page shows you how to switch views, read AI summaries, and handle multiple conversations at once.

## Conversation views

Switch between views to focus on what matters right now.

| View          | What it shows                          | When to use it               |
|---------------|----------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| All           | Every open and closed conversation     | Daily overview               |
| Assigned to me| Conversations waiting for your reply   | Your personal queue          |
| Unassigned    | New conversations no one has claimed   | Team triage                  |
| Closed        | Conversations marked as resolved       | Review past work             |

To change views, click the tabs at the top of the inbox.

## AI summaries

Fin creates a short summary for every conversation so you can catch up quickly.

1. Open any conversation from the list.
2. Look for the summary panel on the right.
3. Read the key points and suggested next steps.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Refresh the summary after a long thread to see the latest AI update.
</Callout>

## Bulk actions

Use bulk actions when several conversations need the same update.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select conversations">
    Click the checkboxes next to each conversation you want to change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    Click the bulk action bar that appears at the bottom of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply changes">
    Pick Assign, Close, or Add tag, then confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="Can I create a custom view?">
  Yes. Click the filter icon, set your rules, then save the view with a name.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Why is the AI summary missing?">
  Summaries appear after the first reply. If the conversation is brand new, wait a few seconds for Fin to generate it.
</Expandable>
