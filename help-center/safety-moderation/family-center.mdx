## Overview

Family Center helps parents and guardians stay connected with teens on Discord. You can link accounts, review activity, and adjust safety settings in one place. The tools focus on transparency so families can talk through choices together.

## Link Family Accounts

Start by creating a parent account if you do not already have one. Then send an invite from your account to your teen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Family Center">
    Sign in to Discord and go to User Settings. Select Family Center from the left menu.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click Add Family Member and enter your teen's Discord username. They receive a request in their app.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept on the teen account">
    Your teen opens the request and confirms the link. Both accounts now appear connected.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  The teen must be under 18 and have their own Discord account before you can link.

</Callout>

## Monitor Activity

Once linked, you can view recent server joins, direct messages, and friend requests. Reports update daily and stay available for 30 days.

## Set Safety Controls

Adjust settings that apply to the linked teen account. Changes take effect right away.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Direct messages" icon="message-circle">
    Choose who can message your teen: friends only, server members, or no one.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Friend requests" icon="user-plus">
    Limit requests to mutual friends or turn them off completely.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Explicit content" icon="shield">
    Enable stricter filters that hide mature images and links.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Examples

A parent notices a new server in the activity feed. They open Family Center, review the server name, and ask their teen about it during dinner. Together they decide whether the server stays on the list.

## Best Practices

- Review the activity feed once a week so conversations stay current.
- Talk with your teen before changing any setting so they understand the reason.
- Use the in-app report tool if something looks unsafe.

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What if my teen declines the invite?" default-open="false">
