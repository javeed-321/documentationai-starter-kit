## Direct Message Controls

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I limit who can send me direct messages?" default-open="true">

Go to User Settings, select Privacy & Safety, and adjust the direct message options. Choose who can message you directly, such as friends only or server members.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I block someone?" default-open="false">

Right-click the user's name, choose Block, and confirm. Blocked users cannot message you or see your online status.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Reporting Abuse

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I report harmful content or behavior?" default-open="true">

Click the three dots next to the message or user profile, select Report, and follow the prompts. Provide details so the team can review quickly.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What happens after I submit a report?" default-open="false">

Discord reviews reports in the order received. You receive a confirmation, and the team takes action if the content violates guidelines.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Data Privacy

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How can I control what data Discord collects?" default-open="true">

Visit Privacy & Safety settings and toggle data collection options. Review and update your choices at any time.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I request my data or ask for deletion?" default-open="false">

Contact @discord_support through the help form. Submit your request with your account details, and the team guides you through the process.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
