title: Managing Availability
description: Set working hours, sync multiple calendars, and prevent double-bookings.
---

Calendly shows your availability based on the calendars you connect and the hours you set. This page explains how to adjust those settings so invitees only see times you can actually meet.

## Set your working hours

Define the days and times you accept bookings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your event type">
    Go to your Calendly dashboard and select the event type you want to edit.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit availability">
    Click the Availability section.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your hours">
    Select the days of the week and set the start and end times for each day.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click Save & close. Your new hours apply immediately.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sync multiple calendars

Calendly checks up to six connected calendars to block busy times and prevent double-bookings.

| Calendar limit | What it covers                  |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 1              | Your main work calendar         |
| 2–6            | Additional personal or project calendars |

<Callout kind="tip">
  Connect every calendar where meetings might already exist. This keeps your availability accurate.

</Callout>

## Add date-specific overrides

Temporarily change availability for a single day or week without editing your regular hours.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Availability">
    Open the event type and click Availability.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add override">
    Click Add date override and pick the date.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set new hours">
    Choose the hours you want to offer or block that day.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click Save. The override takes effect right away.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="What happens if two calendars show the same meeting?">
  Calendly treats the time as busy and removes it from your availability.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I sync more than six calendars?">
  No. Calendly supports a maximum of six calendars per user.

</Expandable>
