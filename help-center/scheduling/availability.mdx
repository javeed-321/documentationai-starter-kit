## Overview

Calendly removes the back-and-forth of scheduling by letting you publish clear availability windows. You connect up to six calendars, define when you are open for meetings, and the system automatically blocks busy times. This page shows how to set recurring hours, handle one-off changes, and cap daily bookings so your calendar stays manageable.

## Define recurring availability

Set your standard weekly hours once and reuse them across every event type.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Weekly hours" icon="calendar">
    Choose the days and times you normally accept meetings. Calendly repeats this pattern every week.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Buffer time" icon="clock">
    Add padding before or after events so you never feel rushed between calls.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Apply date overrides

Life happens. Date overrides let you open or close specific days without touching your recurring schedule.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Use overrides for holidays, offsites, or personal days. They take precedence over your normal hours.
</Callout>

## Limit maximum bookings per day

Prevent overload by capping how many meetings you accept on any single day.

<Expandable title="How daily limits work" default-open="false">
  Enter the maximum number of events allowed. Once the limit is reached, that day disappears from your booking page.

</Expandable>

## Examples

- A consultant blocks Fridays and sets a limit of three 30-minute calls per day.
- A sales rep opens evenings only during a product launch week using a date override.

## Best practices

- Review connected calendars weekly so Calendly always sees the full picture.
- Start with generous buffers and tighten them after you see real usage patterns.
- Test your booking link in an incognito window before sharing it with others.
