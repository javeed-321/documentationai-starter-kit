## Submitting a Support Request

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I open a support ticket?" default-open="true">
    Visit the Discord Help Center and select the option to submit a request. Provide your account email and a clear description of the issue.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What details should I include?">
    Share your username, the exact problem, any error messages, and steps you have already tried. Screenshots help the team understand the situation quickly.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Response Times

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How long will it take to receive a reply?" default-open="true">
    Most tickets receive an initial response within 24 to 48 hours. Complex issues may require additional time for investigation.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I check the status of my ticket?">
    Log in to the Help Center with the same email used to submit the request. You will see updates as the team works on your case.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Escalating an Issue

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What if my first reply does not solve the problem?" default-open="true">
    Reply directly to the ticket thread with any new details. The support agent will review the additional information and continue assisting you.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I request further review?">
    If the issue remains unresolved after several exchanges, ask the agent to escalate the ticket. They will route it to the appropriate specialist team.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
