## Overview

You want customers to finish their purchase without friction. Shopify helps you set up reliable payments, recover lost sales, and keep taxes accurate so checkout feels smooth.

Start with the basics below. Each section gives clear steps you can follow right now.

## Set up Shopify Payments

Connect your account so you can accept cards and other methods directly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your account">
    Go to your Shopify admin and open Settings then Payments. Click Complete account setup and enter your business details.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test a payment">
    Use the test mode to place a small order. Confirm the funds appear in your balance before you switch to live mode.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Keep your bank details up to date so payouts reach you on schedule.
</Callout>

## Recover abandoned carts

Many shoppers leave before paying. Shopify can send gentle reminders that bring them back.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email reminders" icon="mail">
    Turn on abandoned cart emails in Settings then Checkout. Choose timing that matches your customers.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Discount offers" icon="tag">
    Add a small incentive in the recovery message. Test different amounts to see what works best.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Configure taxes

Accurate taxes prevent checkout surprises.

<Expandable title="How to set tax rates" default-open="false">
  Open Settings then Taxes and duties. Select your region and let Shopify calculate rates automatically. Review the list once a quarter.

</Expandable>

## Best practices

- Keep checkout pages short. Remove any fields you do not need.
- Show clear shipping costs before the final step.
- Send recovery emails within an hour for best results.
- Review tax settings after any change in your product catalog.

If something still feels off, check the status page first. Most issues clear up after you refresh your payment settings.
