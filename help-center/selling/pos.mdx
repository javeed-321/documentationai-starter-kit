## Overview

Shopify POS helps you sell face-to-face while keeping your online store in sync. You can ring up customers at markets, pop-ups, or your physical shop without losing track of stock.

This guide walks you through the main steps so you can get selling quickly.

## Hardware Setup

Choose the right hardware for your needs.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Card reader" icon="credit-card" href="#">
    Accept contactless payments on the go.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Receipt printer" icon="printer" href="#">

Connect devices in the Shopify POS app under Settings. Pair via Bluetooth and test a transaction before your first sale.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Keep a spare battery pack for busy market days.
</Callout>

## Inventory Sync

Your online and in-person stock stay aligned automatically.

<Expandable title="How sync works" default-open="false">
  When you sell an item in store, the quantity drops everywhere. Restocks update both channels at once.
</Expandable>

Check stock levels from the app before you head out. This prevents overselling popular products.

## Offline Mode

You can keep selling even without internet.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Start offline" icon="wifi-off">
    Toggle offline mode in the app menu. Sales are stored safely on the device.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Reconnect later" icon="wifi">
    When you regain signal, tap Sync. All transactions upload and inventory updates.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="info">
  Test offline mode once before relying on it at an event.
</Callout>

## Receipt Customization

Make receipts match your brand.

Add your logo, return policy, and thank-you message in Settings. You can also include order numbers for easy lookups later.

## Examples

A market vendor sells handmade candles. She uses the card reader and prints receipts with a short care-tip note. Stock counts update her website overnight.

A small clothing store uses POS at the counter. Offline mode handles busy periods, and sync runs automatically each morning.

## Best Practices

- Charge devices the night before events.
- Print a test receipt after any setting change.
- Review daily sales reports to spot trends early.
- Train staff on the simple offline toggle so everyone feels confident.
