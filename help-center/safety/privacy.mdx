If unwanted contacts or data exposure worry you on Discord, this page shows how to set privacy controls, block users, and request your data.

## Adjust who can contact you

Update these controls to limit direct messages and friend requests.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open User Settings">
    Click your profile picture in the bottom left, then select Privacy & Safety.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set direct message rules">
    Choose who can send you direct messages: everyone, friends only, or no one.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Control friend requests">
    Toggle options for server members, mutual friends, or everyone.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Changes apply immediately across all your servers.
</Callout>

## Block and report users

Stop interactions from specific accounts right away.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a user's profile">
    Right-click their name in chat or the member list.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Block the user">
    Select Block and confirm. They can no longer message or see your online status.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Report if needed">
    Choose Report and follow the prompts with details about the issue.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage your data

Request a copy of your information or ask for deletion.

| Option              | What it does                          | How to request                  |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Data download       | Get a file of your messages and settings | Go to Privacy & Safety then Request Data |
| Account deletion    | Permanently remove your profile       | Submit a request through support |

<Expandable title="How long does a data request take?">
  Downloads usually arrive within 24 hours. Deletion requests process in up to 30 days.

</Expandable>
