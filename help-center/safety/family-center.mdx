## Family Center overview

Family Center gives you direct tools to monitor your teen's Discord activity and apply safety controls. This page shows you how to set it up and use the main features.

## Enable Family Center

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open settings">
    Tap your profile picture, then select Family Center.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Link accounts">
    Enter your teen's Discord username and send the invite. They must accept on their device.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review defaults">
    Check the pre-set limits on direct messages and friend requests.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Monitor activity

You see a weekly summary of your teen's servers, friends added, and time spent in voice channels. No message content is shown.

| Activity type       | What you see                  | Update frequency |
|---------------------|-------------------------------|------------------|
| Servers joined      | List of server names          | Daily            |
| Friends added       | New usernames                 | Daily            |
| Voice time          | Hours in voice channels       | Weekly           |
| Direct messages     | Count only, no content        | Weekly           |

## Set controls

Use the toggles in Family Center to limit who can message your teen or add them as a friend. Changes apply immediately after you save.

<Callout kind="info">
  These controls work only while your teen's account stays linked to Family Center.
</Callout>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="Can I read my teen's messages?">
  No. Family Center shows activity counts and server lists only. Message content stays private.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What happens if my teen removes the link?">
  You receive an alert and lose access to their activity data until they re-link.

</Expandable>
