You want to reach customers through email, SMS, and social ads without juggling separate tools. This page shows how to build and track a single multichannel campaign in Mailchimp.

## Add SMS to an email campaign

Follow these steps to include SMS messages in your workflow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the campaign">
    Go to Campaigns and select Create campaign. Choose Email as the starting channel.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable SMS">
    In the campaign builder, open the Channels menu and toggle on SMS. Enter your SMS content in the new section that appears.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set send rules">
    Choose whether SMS sends at the same time as email or after a delay. Save the settings.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Only send SMS to contacts who have opted in. Mailchimp blocks campaigns that violate consent rules.
</Callout>

## Run social ads from the same campaign

You can push the same offer to social platforms without leaving the workflow.

- Select the Social tab inside your campaign.
- Connect your Facebook or Instagram ad account.
- Choose the audience segment and ad creative.
- Set a budget and schedule that matches your email and SMS timing.

## Track results across channels

Use this table to see which metrics update automatically.

| Channel | Tracked metrics                  | Where to view          |
| ------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Email   | Opens, clicks, bounces           | Campaign reports       |
| SMS     | Delivery rate, replies, opt-outs | SMS performance tab    |
| Social  | Impressions, clicks, conversions | Ads manager dashboard  |

All data appears in one combined report under Analytics.

## Common questions

<Expandable title="Can I send SMS without an email?">
  Yes. Start a new campaign and select SMS as the only channel.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I see which channel performed best?">
  Open the campaign report and switch to the Cross-channel view. It shows side-by-side results.

</Expandable>
