## Start a new campaign

You need a clear way to build emails that match your audience. This page shows you how to pick a template, generate content with AI, and add personalization so your message reaches the right people.

## Choose a template

Mailchimp offers built-in templates that match common campaign types.

| Template type | Best for |
|---------------|----------|
| Welcome series | New subscribers |
| Promotional | Sales and offers |
| Newsletter | Regular updates |
| Abandoned cart | Recover lost sales |

## Build your email in steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a template">
    Go to Campaigns and click Create Campaign. Choose Email, then pick a template from the gallery.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate content with AI">
    Click the AI content button inside the editor. Enter a short prompt that describes your offer or message. Review the generated text and edit as needed.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add personalization">
    Insert merge tags such as first name or purchase history. These tags pull real data from your audience list so each recipient sees relevant details.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Test your email on both desktop and mobile before you send. Small layout changes can improve readability.

</Callout>

## Personalization options

Use these common merge tags to make each email feel personal:

- `*|FNAME|*` shows the recipient's first name.
- `*|LNAME|*` shows the recipient's last name.
- `*|PRODUCT|*` shows items the recipient viewed or bought.

## Quick answers

<Expandable title="Can I edit the AI text after it is generated?">
  Yes. Click any generated block and type your own changes. The AI text serves as a starting point only.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What happens if a merge tag has no data?">
  Mailchimp leaves the tag blank or shows a fallback value you set in the audience settings.

</Expandable>

Still stuck? Contact Mailchimp support through the Help menu in your account.
