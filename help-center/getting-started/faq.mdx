## Onboarding steps

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I create my first Zendesk account?" default-open="true">

Sign up at the Zendesk website with your work email. Verify your address, then follow the guided setup to name your help center and add your first team member.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What should I configure right after signup?">

Add your support channels such as email, chat, and voice. Set up basic routing rules so tickets reach the right agents from day one.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How long does initial setup usually take?">

Most teams complete core setup in under two hours when they prepare their team list and channel details in advance.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Plan selection

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Which plan fits a small support team?" default-open="true">

Start with the Team plan if you have fewer than five agents and need essential ticketing plus AI suggestions. Upgrade later as volume grows.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I compare plans before committing?">

Use the pricing page to view side-by-side features. Focus on channel limits, AI agent capacity, and analytics depth to match your current needs.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I change plans later without losing data?">

Yes. Upgrades or downgrades keep all your tickets, articles, and settings intact. Contact support if you need help during the switch.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Data migration

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="What data can I import into Zendesk?" default-open="true">

You can bring in tickets, contacts, knowledge base articles, and macros from most legacy systems using the built-in importer or CSV files.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I start a data migration?">

Go to Admin Center, select Data Import, and follow the prompts. Test with a small sample first to confirm field mapping.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Who can help if my data needs custom mapping?">

Zendesk Professional Services or certified partners can handle complex migrations. Reach out through your account dashboard for a quote.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
