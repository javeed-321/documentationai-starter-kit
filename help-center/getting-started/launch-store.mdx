## Overview

Creating your first store on Shopify takes only a few minutes. The platform guides you through each step so you can focus on your products instead of technical setup. Follow the process below to sign up, configure your basics, and publish a live site.

## Create Your Account

Visit the Shopify homepage and select the option to start a free trial. Enter your email address, store name, and password. Shopify sends a confirmation link to verify your account.

After verification, the setup wizard opens automatically. Provide basic business details such as your location and product type. These answers help tailor the recommendations that appear next.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Use a business email you check often. You will receive important notifications about orders and payments.
</Callout>

## Run the Store Setup Wizard

The wizard asks a short series of questions about your sales channels and product categories. Answer each one, then review the suggested apps and settings.

- Choose the sales channels that match your plans (online store, social, or point of sale).
- Add a product photo and price to test the catalog.

The wizard creates a starter store with placeholder content you can replace later.

## Connect a Domain

You can connect a custom domain or use the free `.myshopify.com` address that Shopify provides.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Custom domain" icon="globe">
    In the admin panel, go to Settings then Domains. Enter your domain name and follow the DNS instructions shown on screen. Changes usually propagate within an hour.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Free subdomain" icon="link">
    Keep the default address until you are ready to purchase a custom domain. You can switch later without losing any content.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Choose Your First Theme

Shopify includes a selection of free and paid themes. From the admin, open Online Store then Themes. Preview a few options that fit your brand.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Start simple" icon="layout" href="#">
    Pick a clean theme with clear product images. You can customize colors and fonts without writing code.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Add later" icon="palette" href="#">
    Upgrade to a premium theme once your store has traffic and you know which layout converts best.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Best Practices

- Publish your store as soon as the wizard finishes so you can test the checkout flow.
- Replace placeholder text and images before sharing the link with customers.
- Enable two-factor authentication in your account settings right away.

<Expandable title="What if the domain does not connect?" default-open="false">
  Double-check that the DNS records point to Shopify exactly as shown in the admin. Wait 30 minutes and refresh the status page. If the issue continues, contact Shopify support from the help menu.
</Expandable>
