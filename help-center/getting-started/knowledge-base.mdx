You need a central set of help articles so agents and customers get the same answers on every channel. This guide shows how to create, connect, and publish those articles in Zendesk.

## Create your first article

Follow these steps to write and publish a new article.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Knowledge Base editor">
    Go to the admin interface and select Knowledge Base, then click New Article.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the content">
    Add a clear title and body text. Use headings and bullet lists to break up long sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add labels and permissions">
    Attach labels so the article appears in the right searches. Set visibility to internal, agents only, or public.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click Publish. The article now appears in search results and can link to other articles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Get AI suggestions while writing

Zendesk Copilot suggests content as you type.

- Start a new article or edit an existing one.
- Type a few sentences about the topic.
- Review the suggested sections that appear on the right.
- Accept, edit, or reject each suggestion.

<Callout kind="tip">
  AI suggestions improve when you add labels and link related articles first.
</Callout>

## Connect articles for self-service

Link articles so customers find answers without contacting support.

1. Open an article in edit mode.
2. Highlight a term or phrase.
3. Choose Link to article and select the target page.
4. Save and publish.

Customers who reach your Help Center now see these connections as recommended reading.

## Common questions

<Expandable title="Can I reuse the same article across multiple brands?">
  Yes. Publish the article once and assign it to every brand that needs it.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I update an article without breaking links?">
  Edit the article directly. Existing links stay intact and show the new content immediately.
</Expandable>
