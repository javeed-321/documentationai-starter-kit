If you want faster campaign creation and sharper audience targeting, Mailchimp's AI tools handle content generation, subject line testing, and segmentation for you. This guide walks through the three main features and shows exactly how to use them.

## Using the Content Assistant

The Content Assistant writes email copy, headlines, and calls to action based on your campaign goals.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assistant">
    In the email builder, click the sparkle icon in the toolbar.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your goal">
    Type a short prompt such as "promote our new summer sale to existing customers."

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and insert">
    Read the generated options, edit any section you need, then click Insert.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Keep prompts specific. Include your product name, offer, and target audience for better results.
</Callout>

## Optimizing subject lines

AI subject line optimization tests multiple options and predicts which ones will perform best.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your subject ideas">
    In the campaign settings, add up to five subject lines in the optimization field.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the analysis">
    Click Optimize. Mailchimp scores each line and shows expected open rates.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose and send">
    Pick the highest-scoring line or let Mailchimp rotate them automatically.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Predictive segmentation

Predictive segmentation groups contacts by likelihood to open, click, or buy.

| Segment type       | What it predicts          | Best used for                  |
|--------------------|---------------------------|--------------------------------|
| High engagement    | Opens and clicks          | Re-engagement and upsells      |
| Likely to purchase | Conversion probability    | Product launch campaigns       |
| At risk            | Drop in activity          | Win-back offers                |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the segment">
    Go to Audience > Segments and select Create with AI.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the goal">
    Choose the outcome you want to predict, such as "will purchase within 30 days."

  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply to your campaign">
    Save the segment and select it when you build your next send.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="How accurate are the AI predictions?">
  Predictions improve as Mailchimp learns from your past campaigns. Results are estimates, not guarantees.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I edit AI-generated content?">
  Yes. Every suggestion is fully editable before you insert it into your email.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Is there a cost to use these tools?">
  The features are included in all paid plans. Free accounts have limited access.
</Expandable>
