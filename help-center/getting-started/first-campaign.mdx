## Overview

Mailchimp helps you launch your first campaign with clear tools that reduce guesswork. You select an audience, build content, and send with confidence. The process works for email or multichannel sends and supports every skill level.

## Campaign Builder

The campaign builder guides you from start to finish. Follow these steps to create your first send.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose campaign type">
    Pick email, SMS, or a combined send from the main dashboard. Each option shows a short description so you know what reaches your contacts.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your campaign">
    Give the campaign a clear internal name. This label stays private and helps you find the send later in reports.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review settings">
    Confirm the from name, reply-to address, and subject line. Small details here affect delivery and open rates.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Audience Selection

Choose the right contacts before you write content. Accurate selection prevents sends to the wrong people and improves results.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Start with a small test group if this is your first campaign. You can expand the audience after you review early performance.
</Callout>

Use the audience selector to filter by signup date, engagement level, or tags. The tool shows a live count so you always know how many contacts will receive the message.

## AI Content Suggestions

Mailchimp includes AI tools that generate subject lines, preview text, and body copy. Open the AI panel inside the content editor and type a short prompt about your goal. The suggestions appear in seconds and you can edit any part before you save.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email" icon="mail">
    AI suggests subject lines and full email layouts. Review three options and pick the tone that matches your brand.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="SMS" icon="message-circle">
    AI creates short message text that stays under character limits. It also suggests follow-up timing.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Example Walkthrough

Imagine you run a local restaurant. You select your recent diners list, let AI draft a lunch special announcement, and send a quick test to yourself first. The preview shows exactly how the message looks on mobile and desktop.

## Best Practices

Keep these habits in mind for every future campaign.

<Expandable title="Test before you send" default-open="false">
  Always send a test message to yourself or a colleague. Check links, images, and mobile layout before the final send.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Track results right away" default-open="false">
  Open the reports page within the first hour. Look at open rate and clicks to spot any issues early.

</Expandable>
