## Overview

Calendly booking links let you share your availability without email ping-pong. You can send a personal link, embed it on a site, or route prospects to the right team member. Each link respects your calendar rules and automatically confirms meetings.

## Personal links

Create a link that shows only your free times. After you set your availability, Calendly generates a unique URL you can copy, shorten, or add to email signatures.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Test the link in an incognito window to confirm it shows the correct times.
</Callout>

## Team scheduling

When multiple people need to meet a prospect, use a team link. Calendly checks every team member's calendar and offers the first available slot that works for everyone.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Round robin" icon="users">
    Distributes meetings evenly across the team.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Collective" icon="calendar">
    Requires all selected members to be free.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Link branding

Replace the default calendly.com path with your own domain so links feel like part of your brand.

<Expandable title="How to add a custom domain" default-open="false">

1. Verify ownership of your domain in account settings.
2. Add the CNAME record provided by Calendly.
3. Choose which event types use the branded domain.

</Expandable>

## Best practices

- Keep event names short and benefit-focused.
- Turn on buffer times so back-to-back meetings do not overlap.
- Review analytics monthly to see which links convert best.
