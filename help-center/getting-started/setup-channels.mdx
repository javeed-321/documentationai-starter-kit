## Connecting Your Channels

You need incoming messages to reach the right teams fast. This page shows you how to connect messaging, voice, email, and social channels in Zendesk so every request lands in one workspace.

## Messaging and Live Chat

Set up web chat and messaging apps so customers reach you in real time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the widget">
    Go to Admin > Channels > Messaging.

    Paste the widget code on your website pages.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect apps">
    In the same menu, select the apps you want.

    Enter the API keys for WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, or Instagram.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the flow">
    Send a test message from each channel.

    Confirm the ticket appears in your Zendesk inbox.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Turn on AI routing so new chats go straight to the best agent or AI agent.
</Callout>

## Voice Integration

Add phone support so calls create tickets automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable voice">
    Go to Admin > Channels > Voice.

    Choose your phone provider and enter the SIP credentials.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up numbers">
    Assign numbers to departments.

    Record a short greeting that plays when customers call.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ticketing System

All channels feed into the same ticketing system. Tickets carry the full conversation history and customer profile.

| Channel       | Ticket Created | AI Assist          |
|---------------|----------------|--------------------|
| Live chat     | Instant        | Suggested replies  |
| Voice         | On call end    | Call summary       |
| Email         | On receipt     | Auto-categorize    |
| Social        | On mention     | Sentiment check    |

## Knowledge Base Connection

Link your help center so agents and AI agents can suggest articles during chats.

<Expandable title="How do I connect the knowledge base?">
  Go to Admin > Channels > Knowledge.

  Select the help center you want to use.

  Turn on article suggestions for both agents and AI agents.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I limit which articles appear?">
  Yes. Add tags to articles and choose which tags the AI agent can use.

</Expandable>
