If your support team needs access to Intercom, this page shows how to invite teammates, assign roles, and set permissions so everyone can work effectively.

## User roles

Intercom offers three roles. Choose the one that matches each person's responsibilities.

| Role          | What they can do                                      |
|---------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| Admin         | Full access, including billing and role changes       |
| Agent         | Handle conversations and use the Helpdesk             |
| Viewer        | Read reports and view conversations only              |

## Invite teammates

Follow these steps to add new team members.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open team settings">
    Go to Settings and select Team.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an invite">
    Click Invite teammate and enter their email address.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a role">
    Choose Admin, Agent, or Viewer from the role menu.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure permissions

Permissions control what each role can see and change. Admins can edit these settings at any time.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Start with the default permissions and adjust only when a specific workflow requires it.

</Callout>

## Team workflows

Here are answers to common questions about day-to-day team setup.

<Expandable title="Can I change a teammate's role later?" default-open="false">
  Yes. Open Team settings, select the person, and update their role.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I remove someone from the team?" default-open="false">
  Go to Team settings, find the teammate, and choose Remove.

</Expandable>
