## Send invites

Go to **Settings → Members** and click **Invite**. Enter one or more email
addresses and choose a role for each person.

## Roles

<CollectionList node="tabs:Help Center/groups:Getting Started" layout="list" />

- **Admin** — full access to settings and billing.
- **Editor** — can create and publish articles.
- **Viewer** — read-only access.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Invites expire after 7 days — resend them anytime from the Members page.
</Callout>
