## Sign up

Head to the sign-up page, enter your work email, and choose a password. We'll
send a verification link to confirm your address.

## Name your workspace

Pick a name your team will recognize. You can rename it later from
**Settings → General**.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  A workspace is the home for all your articles, members, and settings.
</Callout>
