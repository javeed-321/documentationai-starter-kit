---
title: Setting up Fin
description: Configure Fin's knowledge sources and escalation rules.
---

If Fin gives incomplete answers or fails to hand off complex issues, this guide shows you how to connect your knowledge base and set clear escalation rules.

## Import your knowledge base

Connect the sources Fin uses to answer customer questions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fin settings">
    Go to your Intercom workspace and select Fin from the sidebar.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add knowledge sources">
    Click Knowledge sources, then choose the type you want to import.

    - Help Center articles
    - Past conversation transcripts
    - Uploaded PDFs or docs

  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the import">
    Select the sources and click Import. Fin processes the content and becomes ready to answer questions.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Import your most up-to-date articles first. Fin learns faster from clear, concise content.

</Callout>

## Adjust AI behavior settings

Control how Fin responds before it contacts a human.

| Setting | Options | Effect |
| ------- | ------- | ------ |
| Response tone | Friendly, Professional, Concise | Changes how Fin writes replies |
| Confidence threshold | Low, Medium, High | Sets how sure Fin must be before answering |
| Max follow-ups | 1–5 | Limits how many questions Fin asks before escalating |

Change these values in the AI behavior section and save. Test with a few sample questions to confirm the new settings.

## Set up human handoff

Decide when Fin should pass a conversation to your team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create escalation rules">
    In the Handoff rules panel, add conditions such as "customer asks for a refund" or "Fin confidence below 70%."

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the destination">
    Select the inbox or team that receives the escalated conversation.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a handoff message">
    Write a short note Fin shows the customer before transferring the chat.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="How long does the import take?">
  Most knowledge bases finish importing within 15 minutes. Larger sets can take up to an hour.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I edit what Fin learned?">
  Yes. Remove or update any source at any time. Fin refreshes its answers automatically.

</Expandable>
