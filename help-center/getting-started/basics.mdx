## Getting started with Discord

This page shows you how to create an account, download the app, and join your first server so you can start chatting right away.

## Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the signup page" title-type="p">
    Go to the Discord website and click the sign up button.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your details" title-type="p">
    Type in your email address, username, and a strong password.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email" title-type="p">
    Check your inbox for the confirmation link and click it to activate your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Download the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your platform" title-type="p">
    Visit the Discord website and select the version for your device.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install the app" title-type="p">
    Run the downloaded file and follow the on-screen prompts to complete installation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in" title-type="p">
    Open the app and sign in with the email and password you created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  The desktop app gives you the best experience for voice chats and screen sharing.
</Callout>

## Join your first server

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find an invite link" title-type="p">
    Ask a friend for an invite or browse public servers on the Discord website.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the invite" title-type="p">
    Click the link or paste it into the app's join server field.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start chatting" title-type="p">
    Accept the rules if prompted, then introduce yourself in the welcome channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="What if I forget my password?" default-open="false">
  Click the forgot password link on the login screen and follow the reset steps sent to your email.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I use Discord on my phone?" default-open="false">
  Yes. Download the mobile app from your device's app store and log in with the same account.
</Expandable>

Still stuck? Reach out to @discord\_support.
