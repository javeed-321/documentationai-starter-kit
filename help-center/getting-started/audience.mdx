To build an engaged audience in Mailchimp, import your contacts, organize them into segments, and set clear preferences while staying GDPR compliant. This page shows the fastest way to complete each step.

## Import your contacts

Use the import tool to add contacts from a CSV file or another source.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your file">
    Export your contacts as a CSV. Include columns for email address, first name, last name, and any custom fields you want to use later.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the import">
    In Mailchimp, go to Audience, click the current audience name, then choose Import contacts. Select CSV or copy-paste.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your fields">
    Match each column in your file to an existing Mailchimp field or create a new one. Review the preview to confirm the mapping.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and import">
    Check the summary for any issues, then click Import. Mailchimp adds the new contacts and skips duplicates.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Tag contacts during import so you can find them easily later.
</Callout>

## Create segments

Segments let you send targeted campaigns to specific groups.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the segment builder">
    Go to Audience, then click Segments and choose Create segment.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add conditions">
    Choose conditions such as signup date, engagement level, or custom field values. Combine multiple conditions with AND or OR logic.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and save">
    Review the contact count, then name and save the segment. You can use it right away in any campaign.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Meet GDPR requirements

GDPR rules apply when you collect or store data from EU contacts.

| Option | What it does | Recommended setting |
| ------ | ------------ | ------------------- |
| Double opt-in | Sends a confirmation email before adding contacts | Enabled |
| Preference center | Lets contacts choose topics and frequency | Enabled |
| Data retention | Automatically removes inactive contacts after a set time | 24 months or less |

<Expandable title="How do I add a GDPR consent field?">
  During import or signup form setup, add a required checkbox labeled “I agree to receive emails.” Store the date and IP address of consent in a custom field.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What if a contact asks to be deleted?">
  Use the Contact search tool, open the profile, and select Delete contact. This removes all data permanently and cannot be undone.

</Expandable>
