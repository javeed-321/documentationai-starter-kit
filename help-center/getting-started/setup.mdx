## Overview

Calendly removes the back-and-forth of scheduling by connecting to your existing calendars. Once linked, you define when you are free and share a booking link that respects those rules. The first setup takes just a few minutes and prevents double-bookings across up to six calendars.

## Connect your calendar

Choose the calendar service you use most. Calendly supports the major providers and stays in sync automatically.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Google" icon="calendar">
    Sign in with your Google account and grant Calendly read-and-write access to your primary calendar.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Microsoft 365" icon="calendar">
    Enter your work email and approve the permissions Calendly needs to read availability.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Outlook" icon="calendar">
    Connect your personal Outlook.com account in the same way.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
  You can add more calendars later from Account Settings. Calendly checks all connected calendars before offering a time.

</Callout>

## Set availability windows

After the calendar connects, define when people can book you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an event type">
    Give the event a name, duration, and location (video link, phone, or in-person).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose days and hours">
    Select the days of the week and the time ranges you want to offer.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add buffers">
    Set buffer time before or after each meeting so you have breaks between calls.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Handle time zones

Calendly detects the time zone of each invitee and shows times in their local zone. You can lock your own time zone or let it adjust automatically when you travel.

<Expandable title="What if my calendar is in a different zone?" default-open="false">
  Calendly always converts between zones. The time you see in your calendar will match the time the invitee selected.

</Expandable>

## Examples

A sales representative might offer 30-minute discovery calls on weekdays between 9 a.m. and 5 p.m., with a 15-minute buffer after each call. A recruiter could create a 45-minute interview slot available only on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

## Best practices

- Start with one simple event type and add more later.
- Review the "Date-specific hours" option when you have days with unusual availability.
- Turn on automatic reminders so invitees receive a confirmation and a 24-hour reminder.
- Test your booking link in an incognito window before sharing it.
