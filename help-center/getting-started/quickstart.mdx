Follow these steps to set up your workspace and ship your first article.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up and verify your email, then name your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Add teammates so everyone can collaborate from day one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your first article">
    Write, preview, and publish — your changes go live instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>
