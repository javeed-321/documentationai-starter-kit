If you need to stop emailing back and forth to book meetings, this guide shows you how to create a Calendly account, connect your calendar, and share your first booking link.

## Create your account

Sign up at calendly.com with your email or Google account. Verify your email if prompted, then choose a username that appears in your booking links.

## Connect your calendar

Calendly checks your availability in real time so you never double-book.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open calendar settings">
    After sign-up, click your avatar and select Calendar Connections.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your calendar">
    Select Google Calendar, Outlook, or iCloud. Follow the on-screen prompts to grant access.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm sync">
    Calendly shows a success message once your calendar appears. Toggle on the calendars you want to sync.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  You can connect up to six calendars on paid plans.

</Callout>

## Create your first event type

An event type defines the meeting length, availability, and location.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new event type">
    From the Calendly home screen, click New Event Type and choose One-on-One.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the basics">
    Name the event, add a description, and choose the duration. Set the location to Zoom, Google Meet, or a phone call.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose availability">
    Pick the hours you want to offer. Calendly automatically blocks times already booked in your connected calendars.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and share">
    Click Save & Publish. Copy the link and send it to anyone who needs to book time with you.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="Can I change the event type later?">
  Yes. Go to Event Types, select the event, and edit any setting. Changes apply immediately.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What if my calendar does not appear?">
  Make sure you granted full access during the connection step. Try disconnecting and reconnecting the calendar.

</Expandable>
