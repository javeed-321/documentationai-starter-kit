Need help launching your first campaign fast? This guide shows you how to create an account, import your audience, and send your first email in Mailchimp.

## Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to the Mailchimp website and click the free sign-up button. Enter your email address, then choose a username and password.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for the confirmation message and click the link inside.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Add your business name and website when prompted. This information helps personalize your campaigns.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Import your audience

<Callout kind="tip">
  Prepare a CSV file with at least email addresses before you start.

</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Audience">
    From the left menu, select Audience, then click Import contacts.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your method">
    Select the import option that matches your file.

  </Step>
</Steps>

| Import method | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| CSV or tab-delimited file | Uploading a spreadsheet |
| Copy and paste | Small lists under 100 contacts |
| Connect an app | Pulling contacts from another tool |

## Send your first campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the email">
    Click Campaigns, then Create campaign. Choose Email as the type and give it a name.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your audience">
    Select the imported audience list. Review the subscriber count before continuing.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Design and send">
    Use the editor to add your subject line and content. Click Send when you are ready.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="What happens if my import fails?">
  Check that your CSV uses the correct column headers and that email addresses are valid. Try importing a smaller test file first.

</Expandable>

Still stuck? Contact Mailchimp support through the Help menu in your account.
