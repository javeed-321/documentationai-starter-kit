This page shows you how to create your workspace, activate Fin, and run your first conversation so you can start responding to customers right away.

## Create your workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to the Intercom sign-up page and create an account with your work email.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your workspace">
    Enter your company name and select the industry that matches your business.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite teammates">
    Add the email addresses of anyone who will help manage conversations.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Activate Fin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the AI settings">
    In the left sidebar, click **Fin** then **Activate**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose data sources">
    Select the help articles or past conversations Fin should learn from.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Fin">
    Toggle the switch to **On** and save your changes.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Fin starts learning immediately but improves after it sees 20–30 real conversations.
</Callout>

## Run your first conversation

1. Open the Messenger on your website or use the test widget in the dashboard.
2. Send a sample question such as "How do I reset my password?"
3. Watch Fin reply in the Inbox.
4. If the answer needs adjustment, click **Edit** on the reply and refine it.

## Common questions

<Expandable title="How long does Fin take to activate?" default-open="false">
  Activation usually finishes in under two minutes once you save the settings.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I turn Fin off later?" default-open="false">
  Yes. Return to the Fin settings page and toggle the switch to **Off** at any time.
</Expandable>

## Still stuck?

Contact Intercom support from the Help menu inside your workspace.
