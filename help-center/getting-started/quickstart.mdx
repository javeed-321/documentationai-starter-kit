This page shows you how to create your Zendesk account, configure an AI agent, and activate your first support channel so you can start resolving customer issues faster.

## Create your account

<Steps>
<Step title="Sign up">
  Go to the Zendesk sign-up page and enter your business email. Choose a strong password and complete the verification step sent to your inbox.

</Step>
<Step title="Set your workspace">
  Name your account and select the primary region closest to your customers. This choice affects data residency and response times.

</Step>
<Step title="Add team members">
  Invite colleagues by email. Assign them roles such as Admin or Agent so they can access the right tools immediately.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Use a company email domain during sign-up to unlock enterprise security features from the start.
</Callout>

## Configure your AI agent

<Steps>
<Step title="Enable AI agents">
  In the admin panel, navigate to AI settings and turn on AI agents. These agents learn from every interaction to improve answers over time.

</Step>
<Step title="Add knowledge sources">
  Upload your help articles, past tickets, and policy documents. The AI uses this content to generate accurate responses.

</Step>
<Step title="Review governance rules">
  Set approval workflows so every AI-generated answer meets your quality standards before it reaches customers.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Activate your first channel

Choose one channel to start. You can add more later.

| Channel       | Best for                     | Setup time |
|---------------|------------------------------|------------|
| Live chat     | Website visitors             | 5 minutes  |
| Ticketing     | Email and form requests      | 10 minutes |
| Voice         | Phone support                | 15 minutes |

<Steps>
<Step title="Connect the channel">
  Select the channel in the Channels menu and follow the on-screen prompts to embed the widget or link your email inbox.

</Step>
<Step title="Test the flow">
  Send a sample request through the new channel. Confirm the AI agent responds and routes the ticket correctly.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Still stuck?

Contact Zendesk support directly from the Help menu inside your account.
