## Overview

You want fast resolutions for your customers. Zendesk lets you create an account, connect a channel, and train your first AI agent in just a few steps. Follow the sections below to get live support running today.

## Account Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to the Zendesk sign-up page and enter your business email. Choose a subdomain that matches your brand.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your workspace">
    Check the confirmation email and set a secure password. Enable two-factor authentication right away for extra safety.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team">
    Invite agents from the admin panel. Assign basic roles so they can start handling tickets immediately.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Channel Configuration

Choose the support channel that matches where your customers already reach you.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Messaging">
    Enable the web widget on your site. Paste the provided snippet into your footer and publish.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Email">
    Connect your support mailbox. Forward incoming messages to the address Zendesk supplies.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Voice">
    Add a phone number from the Contact Center settings. Route calls to AI agents or live agents.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Initial AI Agent Training

Train the AI so it understands your common questions from day one.

<Expandable title="Upload sample tickets" default-open="true">
  Import 50 recent tickets. The AI reviews them and suggests answers you can approve or edit.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Define intents">
  List the top five customer goals, such as password reset or order status. Add sample phrases for each.

</Expandable>

## Quick Configuration Example

Use the admin API to finish setup from the command line.

````bash
curl -X POST https://example.com/api/v2/channels \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -d '{"type":"messaging","enabled":true}'
````

## Best Practices

<Callout kind="tip">
  Start with one channel. Add more only after the first AI agent reaches 80 percent resolution accuracy.

</Callout>

Review resolved tickets weekly. Mark helpful answers so the AI keeps improving on its own.
