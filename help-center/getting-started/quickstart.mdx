This page shows you how to create your Mailchimp account, add contacts, and send your first campaign.

## Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to the Mailchimp website and click the sign-up button. Enter your email address, a password, and your business name.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for the confirmation message and click the link inside.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Add your website URL and industry details when prompted.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Use a business email address you check often so you receive important account updates.

</Callout>

## Import your audience

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an audience">
    From the dashboard, select Audience then click Create Audience. Give it a name that matches your contact list.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add contacts">
    Choose Import contacts and upload a CSV file or paste email addresses directly.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your fields">
    Match your file columns to Mailchimp fields such as email, first name, and last name.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send your first campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new campaign">
    Click Campaigns then Create Campaign. Select Email as the channel.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your audience">
    Pick the audience you imported earlier.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Design your email">
    Select a template, add your subject line, and insert your content.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    Preview the email on both desktop and mobile, then click Send.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Campaign options

| Option       | Best for                     |
| ------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Regular      | Standard promotional emails  |
| Automated    | Welcome series or abandoned cart |
| RSS          | Blog post notifications      |

<Expandable title="What if my contacts do not import correctly?">
  Double-check that your CSV file uses the correct column headers and that email addresses are valid.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I test my campaign first?">
  Yes. Use the Send a test email button before you send to your full audience.

</Expandable>

Still stuck? Contact Mailchimp support through the Help menu in your account.
