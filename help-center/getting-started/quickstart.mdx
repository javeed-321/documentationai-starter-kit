## Overview

Dropbox helps you store, share, and access files from any device. This quickstart walks you through account creation, app installation, and your first file sync so you can start collaborating right away.

Follow the steps below in order. Each section takes only a few minutes.

## Set up your account

Create a free Dropbox account with your email address. You receive a confirmation link that activates your space.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Use a work email if you plan to share files with teammates later.
</Callout>

## Install the apps

Choose the platforms you use most. The desktop app keeps files in sync automatically, while the mobile app lets you view and edit on the go.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop" icon="monitor">
    Download the installer from dropbox.com and run it. Sign in with the same email you used to create your account.

    The Dropbox folder appears in your file explorer.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mobile" icon="smartphone">
    Get the Dropbox app from your app store. Open it and sign in.

    Enable camera upload if you want photos saved automatically.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Sync your first files

Drag any document into the Dropbox folder on your computer. The file uploads in the background and becomes available on all your devices within seconds.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a file">
    Place a test document in the Dropbox folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check status">
    Look for the green checkmark icon that shows the file finished syncing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open elsewhere">
    Open the same file on your phone to confirm it appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

Keep these habits to avoid common issues:

- Name files clearly so teammates know the content at a glance.
- Share folders instead of individual files when you expect ongoing edits.
- Enable two-step verification in your account settings for extra security.

<Expandable title="What if a file will not sync?">
  Check your internet connection first. If the problem continues, right-click the Dropbox icon in your system tray and choose Pause syncing, then resume after 30 seconds.
</Expandable>
