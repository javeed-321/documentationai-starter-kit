This page shows you how to set up your Zendesk account and activate AI-enhanced support so you can resolve tickets faster across channels.

## Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to the Zendesk sign-up page and enter your business email.

    Choose a strong password and verify your account through the confirmation email.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Add your company name and select the primary support region.

    Upload a logo so customers recognize your brand in every channel.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Use a dedicated admin email during setup so you keep full control of the account later.
</Callout>

## Configure channels

Choose the channels your customers already use. You can turn on more later.

| Channel       | Best for                     | Setup time |
|---------------|------------------------------|------------|
| Email         | Traditional ticket flow      | 5 minutes  |
| Live chat     | Real-time website questions  | 10 minutes |
| Voice         | Phone support with AI routing| 15 minutes |
| Messaging     | Social and mobile apps       | 10 minutes |

## Activate AI agents

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable AI features">
    In Admin Center, go to AI and turn on the Resolution Platform toggle.

    Accept the AI governance terms to unlock agent creation.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first AI agent">
    Name the agent and select the channels it will monitor.

    Add sample questions so the agent learns your common resolutions.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Handle your first ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send a test message">
    Use live chat or email to create a sample ticket in your account.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review AI suggestions">
    Open the ticket and check the AI-generated reply options.

    Edit the reply if needed, then send it to close the ticket.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="How long does AI training take?">
  The agent starts helping immediately with basic answers. It improves after every resolved ticket, usually within the first day of normal use.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I turn AI off for certain tickets?">
  Yes. Add a tag such as `no-ai` to any ticket and the AI agent will skip it.
</Expandable>

Still stuck? Contact Zendesk support directly from the Help menu inside your account.
