## Submitting a Support Ticket

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I submit a support ticket?" default-open="true">

Go to the Help section in your Intercom workspace. Click "Contact support" and choose your issue type. Fill out the form with details about what you need.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What information should I include?">

Describe the problem clearly, note any error messages, and mention the steps you tried. Attach screenshots when they help show the issue.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Accessing Community Forums

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where can I find the community forums?" default-open="true">

Visit the Intercom Community from the footer of any help page. Search existing threads or start a new post to connect with other users.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I get answers from Intercom staff there?">

Yes, Intercom team members monitor the forums and reply to common questions. Peer answers often appear first for quick tips.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Scheduling Demos or Consultations

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I book a demo?" default-open="true">

From the Intercom dashboard, open the chat widget and request a demo. A team member will follow up within one business day to schedule a time.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What happens during a consultation?">

You share your current setup and goals. The consultant walks through relevant features and answers setup questions in real time.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Reporting Bugs and Feature Requests

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I report a bug?" default-open="true">

Use the support ticket form and select "Bug report." Include the exact steps that led to the problem and your browser details.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Where do I suggest a new feature?">

Choose "Feature request" in the same form. Describe the idea and how it would help your workflow so the product team can review it.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
