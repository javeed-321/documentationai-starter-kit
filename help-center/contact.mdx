## Creating support tickets

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I submit a support ticket?" default-open="true">
  Sign in to your Zendesk account and navigate to the Help Center. Click Submit a request, choose your product area, and describe your issue. Attach screenshots if they help clarify the problem.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What details should I include in my ticket?">
  Provide your account email, the exact steps that led to the issue, any error messages, and your Zendesk plan level. Clear details help our team respond faster.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Priority support options

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Who qualifies for priority support?" default-open="true">
  Priority support is available to customers on Enterprise and Enterprise Plus plans. It includes faster response times and dedicated account contacts during business hours.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I request priority handling for an urgent issue?">
  When submitting your ticket, select the Priority or High urgency option. Enterprise customers can also reach their assigned support engineer directly through the account portal.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Community forums and knowledge base

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where can I find self-service answers?" default-open="true">
  Visit the Zendesk Knowledge Base inside your Help Center. Search for articles on setup, troubleshooting, and best practices written by Zendesk experts and community contributors.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I ask the community for help?">
  Post your question in the Zendesk Community forums. Tag your post with the relevant product so other users and Zendesk staff can reply quickly with proven solutions.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Enterprise account management

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I contact my dedicated account team?" default-open="true">
  Enterprise customers can message their Customer Success Manager directly from the Account Settings page. They will coordinate with support specialists on complex requests.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What support is included for large-scale rollouts?">
  Enterprise plans include implementation reviews, custom SLA options, and quarterly business reviews to ensure your Zendesk instance scales smoothly with your team.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
