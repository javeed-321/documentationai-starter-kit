## Contacting support

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I reach support quickly?" default-open="true">

Click the chat bubble in your Help Center. A support agent responds within minutes during business hours.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What details should I include in my message?">

Share your workspace ID, the exact page or article, and what you tried. This helps us solve the issue faster.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Community forums

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where can I ask other users for help?" default-open="true">

Visit the Intercom Community forums linked from the Help Center footer. Search first—many common questions already have answers.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I post feature ideas there too?">

Yes. Use the Ideas category so the community can upvote and discuss before it reaches our team.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Documentation resources

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where do I find step-by-step guides?" default-open="true">

Browse the full collection of Help Center articles. Start with the Getting Started collection for the most common tasks.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I search the docs effectively?">

Use the search bar at the top and filter by collection. Keywords like "article editor" or "permissions" return the most relevant results.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Feature requests

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I suggest a new feature?" default-open="true">

Open a support chat and select "Feature request." We review every idea and update you when it moves forward.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I track the status of my request?">

Yes. Check your request in the Community Ideas section. We post updates there as the feature progresses.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
