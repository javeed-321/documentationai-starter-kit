## In-App Chat Support

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I start a chat with support?" default-open="true">

Open the Intercom Messenger from any page in your workspace. Type your question in the chat box and a support agent will respond within minutes during business hours.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What if chat is unavailable?">

If no agents are online, the chat window offers self-service articles first. You can also leave a message that converts into a ticket automatically.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Creating a Support Ticket

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I open a ticket?" default-open="true">

From the Messenger, choose the option to leave a message when chat is closed. Provide a clear subject and details so the team can assist faster.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I attach files to my ticket?">

Yes. Use the paperclip icon in the message composer to add screenshots or logs up to 25 MB each.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Checking System Status

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where do I find the status page?" default-open="true">

Visit status.intercom.com to see real-time uptime for all Intercom services and any ongoing incidents.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Will I receive alerts for outages?">

Subscribe on the status page with your email to get notifications when incidents are opened or resolved.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Joining Community Discussions

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I access the community forum?" default-open="true">

Go to community.intercom.com and sign in with your Intercom account. Browse existing threads or post your question in the appropriate category.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What topics are covered in the forum?">

The forum includes product tips, troubleshooting threads, feature requests, and peer-to-peer advice from other Intercom users.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
