## Creating a Support Ticket

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I open a support ticket?" default-open="true">

  Sign in to your Intercom workspace. Click the Help icon, then select Contact support. Describe your issue clearly and add any relevant screenshots. Submit the form. You receive a confirmation email with your ticket number.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What details help resolve my ticket faster?">

  Include your workspace ID, the exact steps that led to the problem, and any error messages. Attach logs or screen recordings when possible. This helps the team investigate quickly.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Accessing the Community Forum

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where can I find the community forum?" default-open="true">

  Go to the Intercom Help Center and click Community in the top navigation. Browse existing threads or start a new one. Search first to see if your question already has answers.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I post in the community?">

  Sign in with your Intercom account. Click New Post, choose the right category, and write a clear title and description. Other users and Intercom staff reply to help.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Monitoring System Status

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I check Intercom status?" default-open="true">

  Visit the status page at status.example.com. Green indicators mean all systems work normally. Yellow or red shows ongoing incidents with details and estimated fix times.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I get status updates by email?">

  Subscribe on the status page with your email address. You receive notifications only when incidents affect your workspace.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Submitting Feature Requests

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I request a new feature?" default-open="true">

  Open the Help Center and select Product feedback. Describe the feature and why it would help your team. Vote on similar ideas already posted to show support.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What happens after I submit feedback?">

  The product team reviews all requests. Popular ideas move into planning. You receive updates when the feature reaches testing or release.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
