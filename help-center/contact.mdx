## Support Channels

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I reach Mailchimp support?" default-open="true">
  Open the Help Center from your account dashboard and use the chat icon for live assistance. Support is available during business hours and responds quickly to account or campaign questions.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What if chat is unavailable?">
  Submit a ticket through the Help Center form. Include your account email and a brief description of the issue so the team can assist without delay.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Onboarding Services

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I schedule an onboarding session?" default-open="true">
  After signing up for the 14-day trial, visit the onboarding section in your dashboard. Choose a time that fits your schedule and an expert will walk you through campaign setup.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Are onboarding sessions included with every plan?">
  Yes. Every new account receives access to guided onboarding calls at no extra cost during the trial period.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Hiring Certified Experts

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I hire a Mailchimp certified expert?" default-open="true">
  Browse the Expert Directory linked from the Help Center. Filter by industry or skill, then contact the expert directly to discuss your project needs.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What services can certified experts provide?">
  Experts help with list building, automation setup, and multichannel campaign strategy. They work with you to match Mailchimp tools to your specific business goals.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
