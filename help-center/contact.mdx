## Submitting Support Tickets

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I submit a support ticket?" default-open="true">

Go to your Shopify admin and open the Help section. Choose the topic that matches your issue, then fill out the form with details. You will receive a confirmation email with your ticket number.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What details should I include in my ticket?">

Add your store URL, a clear description of the problem, and any error messages or screenshots. This helps the team respond faster.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Using Live Chat and Phone Support

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I start a live chat?" default-open="true">

From the Help panel in your admin, select Chat. Available agents will reply during business hours for your plan level.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="When can I call Shopify support?">

Phone support is offered on select plans. Check your plan details in the Billing section or request a callback through the Help panel.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Shopify Community Forums

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do the community forums work?" default-open="true">

Visit the Shopify Community site and search existing threads. Post your question in the right category if you do not find an answer.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Are forum answers reliable?">

Answers come from other merchants and Shopify staff. Look for replies marked as solutions by the community.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Partner and Agency Assistance

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I find a Shopify partner?" default-open="true">

Use the Partner Directory in your admin or on the Shopify website. Filter by location, services, and expertise.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What can partners help with?">

Partners can set up your store, customize themes, migrate data, or run marketing campaigns. Many offer ongoing support packages.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
