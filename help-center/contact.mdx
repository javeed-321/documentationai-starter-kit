## Creating a Support Ticket

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I open a support ticket?" default-open="true">
    Sign in to your Help Center account. Click the Contact Support link at the bottom of any page. Fill out the form with a clear description of your issue and submit. You will receive a confirmation email with your ticket number.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What details should I include in my ticket?">
    Describe the exact steps you took before the problem appeared. Include any error messages you see and note which browser or device you use. The more specific you are, the faster we can help.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Participating in the Community Forum

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where can I ask questions from other users?" default-open="true">
    Visit the Community tab in your Help Center. Search existing threads first. If you do not find an answer, start a new discussion and tag it with the relevant product area.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I get a quick reply in the forum?">
    Write a short title that states your problem. Add the steps you already tried. Other users and Zendesk staff often respond within a few hours during business days.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Exploring Premium Support Tiers

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What extra help comes with paid support plans?" default-open="true">
    Paid plans give you faster response times, a dedicated account manager, and priority access during high-volume periods. You also receive quarterly reviews of your usage and recommendations.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I upgrade to a premium tier?">
    Open a ticket from your account settings and request a support plan comparison. Our team will reply with pricing and next steps within one business day.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Submitting Feature Requests

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How can I suggest a new feature?" default-open="true">
    Go to the Ideas section inside the Community forum. Search to see if the idea already exists. If not, post your suggestion and explain the benefit it would bring to your workflow.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Do feature requests receive updates?">
    Yes. Staff review every idea. When an idea moves forward, you receive a notification and the status changes to In Review or Planned.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
