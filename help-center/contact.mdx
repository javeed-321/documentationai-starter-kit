## Submitting a Support Ticket

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I submit a support ticket?" default-open="true">

Visit the Help Center and click the contact option in the footer. Fill out the form with a clear description of your issue. Attach any relevant screenshots or error messages to speed up the response.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What details should I include?">

Provide your workspace ID, the exact steps that led to the problem, and any browser or device information. This helps the team diagnose the issue quickly without extra back-and-forth.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Live Chat with the Intercom Team

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I start a live chat?" default-open="true">

Open the Help Center and look for the chat bubble in the bottom corner. Type your question and a support agent will respond in real time during business hours.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="When is live chat available?">

Live chat runs Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. in your local time zone. Outside those hours, leave a message and the team will reply by email within one business day.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Community Forum and Webinars

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where can I join the community forum?" default-open="true">

Go to the resources section and select the forum link. Search existing threads first, then post your question if you do not find an answer. Other users and Intercom staff often reply within a few hours.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I find upcoming webinars?">

Check the events calendar in the same resources area. Register for sessions on Help Center best practices. Recordings are posted afterward for anyone who misses the live event.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Status Page and Incident Reports

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I check the current status?" default-open="true">

Visit the status page at https://status.example.com. Green indicators mean all systems are operating normally. Yellow or red shows active incidents with details and estimated resolution times.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What if I notice an issue not listed?">

Use the live chat or ticket form to report it. Include the time you observed the problem and any error codes. The team will investigate and update the status page if needed.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
