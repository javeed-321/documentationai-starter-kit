## Support Ticket Creation

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I create a support ticket?" default-open="true">

Open the Helpdesk from your workspace sidebar and select New ticket. Choose the relevant category, add a clear subject, and include steps to reproduce the issue.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What details should I include?">

Provide your workspace ID, the exact steps that led to the problem, any error messages, and screenshots. This helps our team respond faster.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Priority Assistance Options

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Who qualifies for priority support?" default-open="true">

Priority assistance is available to customers on paid plans. It routes your ticket to a dedicated queue with faster response targets.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I request priority handling?">

When creating a ticket, select the Priority option if your plan includes it. You can also reach out via the in-app chat for urgent cases involving Fin or live conversations.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Feedback Channels

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="Where can I share product feedback?" default-open="true">

Use the Feedback form inside the Helpdesk under Settings. Your input directly influences improvements to Fin and Helpdesk workflows.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I join a customer advisory session?">

Yes. Sign up through the Resources section of your account. Sessions focus on upcoming features for the AI Agent and support automation.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
