## Opening a Support Ticket

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I submit a support ticket?" default-open="true">
    Sign in to your Shopify admin. Go to Help and then Contact support. Choose the topic that matches your issue, add details, and submit. You receive an email with your ticket number.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What details should I include in my ticket?">
    Describe the exact steps that led to the problem. Include any error messages, affected order numbers, and browser details. Screenshots help support staff see the issue quickly.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How long until I receive a reply?">
    Most tickets receive a first response within one business day. Complex issues may take longer while the team investigates.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Live Chat Availability

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="When is live chat available?" default-open="true">
    Live chat appears in your Shopify admin when agents are online. Hours vary by region and plan level. Check the Help section for the current status.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I request a callback instead?">
    Yes. During chat hours you can choose the callback option and enter your phone number. An agent calls you within the stated wait time.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Community Forums

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where can I ask other merchants for advice?" default-open="true">
    Visit the Shopify Community forums. Search existing threads first, then post your question in the most relevant category. Include your store URL if you want targeted replies.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Are the forums monitored by Shopify staff?">
    Shopify staff and partners read and answer many threads. You can mark helpful replies and close your thread once the issue is resolved.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Partner Directory

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I find a Shopify expert?" default-open="true">
    Go to the Shopify Partner Directory. Filter by location, service type, and language. Contact partners directly to discuss your needs.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What should I ask a potential partner?">
    Ask about experience with stores of your size, typical project timelines, and ongoing support options. Request references from similar merchants.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
