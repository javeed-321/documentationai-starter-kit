## Find the right way to get help

If you need help with Zendesk, this page shows the fastest path to support, community help, or account-specific assistance.

## Contact options

| Need help with | Best option | Who can use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A product issue or incident | Support ticket | Customers with an active support plan |
| How-to questions or shared best practices | Community forums | Anyone with community access |
| Account-specific guidance or planning | Customer success manager | Enterprise accounts with a CSM |
| Product feedback or ideas | Feedback channel | Anyone |

## Submit a support ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the support portal" icon="arrow-right">
    Sign in with your Zendesk account and go to the support request form.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the right category" icon="settings">
    Select the product area, describe the issue, and include the urgency if the form asks for it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add helpful details" icon="zap">
    Include clear steps to reproduce the issue, error messages, affected users, and any recent changes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the request" icon="check-circle">
    Submit the ticket and watch for the confirmation message or email with the case number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  If the issue affects many users or blocks support work, say that clearly in the ticket so the right team can prioritize it.
</Callout>

## Support plans

Your support plan decides how you can contact Zendesk support and how quickly you can expect a response.

| Plan type | What it usually includes |
| --- | --- |
| Standard support | Ticket-based help during published support hours |
| Premium support | Faster response options and broader coverage |
| Enterprise support | Dedicated assistance for larger deployments and complex needs |

If you do not know your plan, check your account details or ask your administrator.

## Community forums

Community forums are the best place to ask practical questions, compare notes with other users, and find answers from shared experience.

Use the forums when you want to:

- Learn how other teams handle a setup
- Ask about product behavior that is not an outage
- Find workarounds or configuration tips
- Browse existing answers before opening a ticket

<Callout kind="info">
  Community replies are helpful for advice and examples, but they do not replace a support ticket for urgent incidents or account-specific issues.
</Callout>

## Enterprise customer success manager

If your account includes a customer success manager, use that contact for planning, adoption, and broader account guidance.

Common reasons to reach out:

- Rollout planning
- Feature adoption
- Best practices for your team
- Quarterly goals and success reviews

## Send feedback or ideas

If you want to share a product idea, report a gap, or suggest an improvement, use the feedback channel available in your account or through your support contact.

Before you send feedback, include:

- What you are trying to do
- What is blocking you
- Why the change would help
- Any examples or screenshots that make the request clear

## Frequently asked questions

<Expandable title="When should I open a support ticket?" default-open="false">
  Open a ticket when you have a bug, outage, access problem, billing issue, or anything that needs account-specific investigation.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="When should I use the community forums?" default-open="false">
  Use the forums when you want advice, examples, or peer feedback and the issue is not time-sensitive.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What if I have an enterprise account?" default-open="false">
  If you have an enterprise account with a customer success manager, start there for planning and strategic guidance, then use support for technical issues.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I send product feedback?" default-open="false">
  Yes. Use the feedback channel in your account or share it through your support contact so the request reaches the right team.
</Expandable>

## Still stuck?

If you still need help, submit a support ticket or contact your account team for the fastest next step.
