## Submitting support tickets

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I open a support ticket?" default-open="true">
    Sign in to your Help Center, then click the Contact support link in the footer. Choose your issue type and fill out the form with as much detail as possible. You will receive a confirmation email with your ticket number.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What details should I include in my ticket?">
    Describe the exact steps you took, any error messages you saw, and the browser or device you used. Attach screenshots or a short video when it helps clarify the problem.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How long until someone replies?">
    Most tickets receive a first response within a few hours during business days. You can check the ticket status anytime from your account.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Priority support options

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Who qualifies for priority support?" default-open="true">
    Priority support is available on paid plans. It gives you faster response times and direct access to senior support engineers.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I request priority handling?">
    When you create a ticket, select the Priority support option if it appears. You can also mention your plan level in the ticket description.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I upgrade for priority support later?">
    Yes. Open a ticket or contact your account manager to discuss plan options that include priority support.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Community forums and user groups

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where can I ask other Zendesk users for help?" default-open="true">
    Visit the Zendesk Community at community.zendesk.com. Search existing threads or start a new one in the Help Center section.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Are there local user groups?">
    Check the Community events calendar for virtual and in-person meetups. Many regions host regular sessions where users share tips and best practices.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I suggest new features?">
    Use the Product feedback area inside the Community. Other users can vote on ideas, and the product team reviews popular requests.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Status page and incident reporting

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where do I check Zendesk service status?" default-open="true">
    Go to status.zendesk.com. The page shows current uptime for all products and lists any ongoing incidents.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How will I know about planned maintenance?">
    Subscribe to updates on the status page. You will receive email notifications before scheduled maintenance that might affect your Help Center.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What should I do during an outage?">
    Check the status page first for official updates. If your ticket relates to the incident, reference the incident ID so support can prioritize it correctly.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
