## Support channels

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I contact support?" default-open="true">

You can reach the Intercom team directly through the messenger inside your workspace. Open the help menu and select the chat option to start a conversation.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I use email instead?">

Yes. Send a message to the address listed in your account settings. Include your workspace ID for faster routing.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Response times

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How long until I hear back?" default-open="true">

Most messages receive a reply within a few hours during business days. Complex issues may take longer, but you will get an acknowledgment right away.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What if my question is urgent?">

Mark the message as urgent in the messenger. The team prioritizes these requests and will respond as soon as possible.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Feature requests

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I suggest a new feature?" default-open="true">

Use the feedback option inside the messenger. Describe the idea clearly so the product team can review it.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Will I get updates on my request?">

You receive a confirmation when the request is logged. Major updates appear in the product changelog when the feature ships.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
