## Chat and Phone Support

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I start a chat with support?" default-open="true">
    Open your Shopify admin and click the chat icon in the bottom right. Available during business hours for most plans.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I call Shopify support directly?" default-open="false">
    Phone support is included on Shopify Plus and some higher plans. Check your plan details in the admin under Billing.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Priority Support Tiers

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What support level comes with my plan?" default-open="true">
    Basic and Shopify plans get email and chat. Advanced and Plus add phone and priority routing.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I upgrade for faster help?" default-open="false">
    Go to Settings then Plans and pricing. Upgrades usually take effect immediately.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Submitting Tickets

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="When should I submit a support ticket?" default-open="true">
    Use tickets for complex issues that need investigation, such as payment or app errors.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What details help resolve my ticket faster?" default-open="false">
    Include your store URL, a clear description of the problem, steps you already tried, and any error messages or screenshots.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Community Forums

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where can I ask other merchants for help?" default-open="true">
    Visit the Shopify Community forums at community.shopify.com. Search first to see if your question was answered.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Are forum answers official?" default-open="false">
    Forum replies come from merchants and Shopify staff. For official help, contact support directly.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
