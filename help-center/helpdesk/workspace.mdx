The Helpdesk Workspace feels crowded when tickets pile up. This page shows you exactly how to manage your inbox, work with teammates, and set automation rules so you stay in control.

## Manage your inbox

Your inbox lists every conversation in one place. Fin, the AI agent, already sorts and answers many messages before they reach you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the inbox">
    Click Inbox in the left sidebar. New conversations appear at the top.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter quickly">
    Use the search bar or click any filter button to show only unread, assigned, or priority items.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply or hand off">
    Click a conversation. Type your reply or click Assign to pass it to a teammate.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Keep the inbox sorted by "Last activity" so nothing important slips down the list.
</Callout>

## Collaborate with your team

Work together without leaving the workspace.

- Mention a teammate with @name to pull them into a thread.
- Add internal notes that only your team sees.
- Watch a conversation to get notified on every update.

## Set automation rules

Automation rules handle repetitive work so you can focus on customers who need a human.

| Rule type       | What it does                     | Example trigger             |
|-----------------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Auto-assign     | Routes new tickets to the right person | Message contains "billing" |
| Auto-close      | Closes solved conversations      | Customer says "thanks"      |
| Auto-tag        | Adds labels for reporting        | Conversation lasts over 30 minutes |

<Expandable title="How do I create a new rule?">
  1. Go to Settings > Automation.
  2. Click Create rule.
  3. Choose the trigger and action.
  4. Save and test with a real message.

</Expandable>
