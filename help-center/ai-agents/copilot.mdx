## Overview

Zendesk Copilot gives agents real-time help inside the workspace. It watches the conversation, suggests next steps, and pulls relevant articles so you spend less time searching and more time resolving.

Copilot works across every channel. It learns from past tickets and your knowledge base to stay accurate.

## Proactive suggestions

Copilot reads the ticket and offers reply drafts before you ask. It highlights key details from the customer message and proposes tone adjustments.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Accept a suggestion with one click, then edit as needed. Copilot updates the draft if the customer adds more context.
</Callout>

## Knowledge retrieval

When a ticket mentions a product or policy, Copilot surfaces the right articles from your help center. It shows the most relevant snippet first and links to the full page.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Customer view" icon="users">
    Copilot shows the customer-facing article and any internal notes attached to it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Agent view" icon="terminal">
    Internal procedures and troubleshooting steps appear only to agents.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Quality scoring

After each reply, Copilot scores the message for clarity, empathy, and completeness. Scores appear in the ticket sidebar with short improvement tips.

<Expandable title="How scores are calculated" default-open="false">
  Zendesk uses your past high-rated tickets as the benchmark. The model compares sentence structure, sentiment, and coverage of the customer's question.
</Expandable>

## Best practices

- Keep your knowledge base up to date so retrieval stays accurate.
- Review Copilot drafts before sending to maintain your team's voice.
- Use the quality score feedback to train newer agents faster.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Copilot">
    Go to Admin Center and turn on Copilot for the desired groups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your content">
    Select the help center sections Copilot should search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review first week">
    Check the quality dashboard and adjust suggestions as needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
