Copilot gives you real-time AI help while you handle tickets. This guide shows how to turn on its main features and use them to resolve issues faster.

## Enable Copilot in your workspace

Follow these steps to activate Copilot for your team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open admin settings">
    Go to Admin and select AI agents.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Copilot">
    Toggle Copilot to active and choose the channels where it should appear.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to agents">
    Select the agent groups that need access and save your changes.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Start with a small pilot group before rolling out to everyone.
</Callout>

## Real-time suggestions

Copilot watches the conversation and offers replies or next steps as you type.

- Accept a suggestion with one click.
- Edit the text before sending.
- Dismiss suggestions that do not fit.

## Knowledge retrieval

Copilot searches your help center and past tickets for the right answer.

| Source          | What it returns                  | Best used for          |
|-----------------|----------------------------------|------------------------|
| Help center     | Published articles               | Common customer issues |
| Past tickets    | Similar resolved cases           | Edge cases             |
| Internal notes  | Team-only procedures             | Policy questions       |

## Quality assurance scoring

After each chat, Copilot scores the interaction on speed, tone, and completeness.

- Scores appear in the ticket sidebar.
- Low scores trigger a short coaching note.
- You can review trends in the QA dashboard.

## Agent coaching

Copilot gives private tips to improve your replies over time.

<Expandable title="How do I see my coaching notes?">
  Open any ticket and look for the Coaching tab on the right.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I turn coaching off for a shift?">
  Yes. Go to your personal settings and pause coaching for a set time.

</Expandable>
