title: Using Copilot for Agents
description: Empower human agents with proactive AI assistance and real-time suggestions.
---

Copilot helps agents resolve tickets faster by delivering real-time suggestions, summaries, and knowledge. This page shows you how to use its core features in Zendesk.

## Get proactive recommendations

Copilot scans the ticket and surfaces next-best actions while you work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a ticket">
    Select any open ticket in the agent workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View suggestions">
    Look at the Copilot panel on the right. It shows recommended replies, macros, and follow-up steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply a suggestion">
    Click a suggestion to insert it into the reply box or trigger the related action.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Accept or dismiss suggestions quickly so Copilot learns your preferences over time.
</Callout>

## Summarize long tickets

Use Copilot to create a concise summary of any ticket thread.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Summarize">
    In the Copilot panel, select the Summarize button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the summary">
    Copilot generates a short overview of the issue, customer history, and previous replies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy or edit">
    Copy the summary into an internal note or edit it before you send it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Retrieve knowledge articles

Copilot searches your knowledge base and suggests relevant articles in real time.

| Option              | What it does                              | When to use                  |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Suggested articles  | Shows top matches based on ticket content | While drafting a reply       |
| Search bar          | Lets you type a custom query              | When you need a specific fix |
| Insert link         | Adds the article link to your response    | To share self-service steps  |

<Expandable title="How do I turn off knowledge suggestions?">
  Go to Admin > Copilot settings and toggle Knowledge retrieval off for your team.
</Expandable>
