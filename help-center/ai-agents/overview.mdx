## Overview

Zendesk AI agents handle customer issues on their own across chat, voice, and ticketing. They learn from each conversation and improve over time. You set the goals, and the agents manage the rest while staying inside your security rules.

## Agent Training

Training starts with your existing tickets and chat logs. The agents review past resolutions to understand your tone and common fixes. You add sample conversations to guide how they reply in new situations.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Upload at least 50 recent resolved tickets before the first training run. This gives the model a clear picture of successful outcomes.
</Callout>

## Resolution Workflows

Agents follow clear steps for each channel. Choose the flow that matches your support style.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Chat" icon="message-circle">
    The agent greets the visitor, asks clarifying questions, and offers fixes. It escalates only when the issue needs a human.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Voice" icon="phone">
    The agent answers calls, confirms details with the caller, and logs the outcome. It transfers complex cases without dropping context.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Ticketing" icon="inbox">
    The agent reads new tickets, applies the right tags, and suggests replies. It closes simple requests and routes the rest.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Self-Improving Models

After each resolved interaction the model reviews the result. Successful paths receive higher weight. Failed paths trigger a review flag so you can adjust training data quickly.
