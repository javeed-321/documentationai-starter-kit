## Overview

Zendesk keeps your customer and employee data safe while you deliver fast resolutions. The Trust Center gives you clear controls, transparent policies, and tools that scale with your team. You stay in charge of access, encryption, and how AI handles every interaction.

## Data Protection

Zendesk encrypts data at rest and in transit. Role-based access limits who sees tickets, chats, or voice recordings. You set retention rules and export data on demand.

## AI Governance

AI agents learn from resolved tickets, but you decide what data they use. Review training settings, turn off learning for sensitive queues, and audit every suggestion before it reaches customers.

<Callout kind="info">
  Always test AI responses in a sandbox first. This prevents surprises when the agent goes live.
</Callout>

## Examples

A support lead at a retail company needed to restrict AI training to non-PII tickets only. They used queue-level settings and confirmed the change in the activity log within minutes.

## Best Practices

- Review access logs weekly.
- Rotate encryption keys every 90 days.
- Keep AI learning off for queues that hold payment details.
- Export compliance reports before audits.
