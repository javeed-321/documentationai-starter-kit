## Overview

Calendly keeps your scheduling data safe with strong protections that meet global standards. You can book meetings confidently knowing your information stays private and secure.

Calendly encrypts data at rest and in transit. Access controls limit who can view details, and regular audits confirm the safeguards work as intended.

## Data retention

Calendly keeps data only as long as needed for the service.

<Expandable title="How long does Calendly store data?" default-open="false">
  Event details stay available while your account is active. You can delete records at any time through account settings.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What happens when I delete an event?" default-open="false">
  Deleted events are removed from active systems within thirty days.
</Expandable>
