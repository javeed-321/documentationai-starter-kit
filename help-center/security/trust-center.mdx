You need to meet enterprise security and responsible AI requirements in Zendesk. This page shows you how to set data residency, access controls, and AI audit logs.

## Choose your data residency region

Zendesk stores customer data in the region you select. Pick the region that matches your compliance needs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open admin settings">
    Sign in to Zendesk as an administrator. Go to Admin > Security > Data Residency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select region">
    Choose one of the available regions from the list. Save your choice.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm migration">
    Zendesk moves existing data to the new region. This process runs in the background and may take up to 48 hours.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Test a small account first if you manage multiple brands.
</Callout>

## Set role-based access controls

Limit who can view or change sensitive data and AI features.

| Role | Can view tickets | Can edit AI settings | Can export logs |
|------|------------------|----------------------|-----------------|
| Agent | Yes | No | No |
| Admin | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Auditor | Yes | No | Yes |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create custom roles">
    Go to Admin > People > Roles. Click Add Role and set permissions for each area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign roles to users">
    Open each user profile and select the new role from the dropdown.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Turn on AI audit logs

AI audit logs record every prompt sent to AI agents and every response generated.

<Expandable title="Why enable audit logs?">
  Audit logs help you review AI decisions, meet regulatory requests, and debug unexpected agent behavior.
</Expandable>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable logging">
    Go to Admin > AI > Governance. Toggle AI Audit Logs to On.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set retention period">
    Choose how long to keep logs (30, 90, or 365 days). Click Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download logs">
    Return to the same page and click Export Logs when you need a copy.
  </Step>
</Steps>
