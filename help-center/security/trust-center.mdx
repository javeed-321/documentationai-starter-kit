## Security and trust settings

If you need to confirm how Zendesk handles data residency, AI governance, compliance, or access controls, this page shows you what to check and where to start.

## Check your security settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your data residency needs" icon="database">
    Confirm where your customer data must be stored and processed.

    - Check your account or plan details for available data residency options.
    - Compare your required region with the regions enabled for your workspace.
    - Make sure any connected workflows do not move data outside your approved region.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify AI governance controls" icon="shield">
    Review how AI features are allowed to use your data.

    - Confirm which AI features are turned on for your account.
    - Check whether human review is required for sensitive workflows.
    - Limit AI access to the minimum set of data needed for support tasks.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm compliance coverage" icon="check-circle">
    Make sure your compliance expectations match your Zendesk setup.

    - Review SOC 2 materials in your trust resources.
    - Confirm GDPR handling for personal data and retention settings.
    - Verify any contract or policy requirements with your security team.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Audit access controls" icon="lock">
    Check who can view, edit, or export data.

    - Review admin roles and permissions.
    - Remove unused accounts and stale access.
    - Turn on the strongest sign-in controls available for your organization.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Security options at a glance

| Area | What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data residency | Region settings, storage location, and any data transfer paths | Helps you keep data in approved locations |
| AI governance | Allowed AI features, data use rules, and review requirements | Helps you control how AI uses support data |
| SOC 2 | Current audit status and available trust documentation | Supports vendor security review |
| GDPR | Personal data handling, retention, and access requests | Helps you meet privacy obligations |
| Access controls | Roles, permissions, and sign-in policies | Reduces the risk of unauthorized access |

## Common questions

<Expandable title="What is data residency?" default-open="false">
Data residency means where your data is stored and processed. If you have location requirements, confirm that your Zendesk setup matches the region you need.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do AI governance controls help?" default-open="false">
AI governance helps you decide where AI features can be used, what data they can access, and when human review is needed. Use it to keep automation aligned with your internal policies.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Does Zendesk support SOC 2 and GDPR needs?" default-open="false">
Zendesk provides enterprise-grade security and compliance resources so you can review SOC 2 and GDPR coverage as part of your own security process. Check your trust materials and legal terms for the latest details.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I reduce access risk?" default-open="false">
Use the smallest set of permissions needed, review admin access regularly, and remove inactive accounts. Strong sign-in controls also help protect your workspace.
</Expandable>

## Before you change anything

<Callout kind="warning">
  Changing residency, access, or AI settings can affect how support workflows run. Review the impact with your security and admin teams before you apply changes.
</Callout>

If you need help confirming your security setup, contact your Zendesk support team or your account admin for the latest trust and compliance details.
