You need clear details on how Zendesk protects your data and AI systems. This page shows the controls, certifications, and steps you can take right now.

## Data Protection

Zendesk encrypts data at rest and in transit. You control where your data is stored through region settings.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Enable data residency in your account settings to keep information in your chosen region.
</Callout>

## AI Governance

Zendesk AI agents learn only from your approved interactions. You decide what data the models can access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review AI data sources">
    Go to Admin Center and open the AI section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set governance rules">
    Choose which ticket fields and customer data the agents may use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor usage">
    Check the AI activity log weekly to confirm only allowed data is processed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compliance Certifications

Zendesk holds the following certifications. You can download the latest reports from the Trust Center.

| Certification | Scope | Renewal |
| ------------- | ----- | ------- |
| ISO 27001 | All products | Annual |
| SOC 2 Type II | Customer Service, AI Copilot | Annual |
| GDPR | Global data processing | Continuous |
| HIPAA | Health data in Employee Service | Annual |

## Access Controls

You manage who can reach your Zendesk instance with these options.

<Expandable title="How do I enforce single sign-on?">
  Turn on SSO in Admin Center under Security. Choose SAML or OpenID Connect and map your identity provider groups to Zendesk roles.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I limit IP addresses?">
  Yes. Add allowed IP ranges in the Network Access settings. Requests from other addresses are blocked automatically.
</Expandable>
