If you manage a Calendly team, set up single sign-on, assign roles, and automate user provisioning to keep access secure and consistent.

## Enable single sign-on

Connect Calendly to your identity provider so team members sign in with existing credentials.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open admin settings">
    Sign in to Calendly and go to Admin settings.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your provider">
    Select SSO and pick your provider from the list.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter provider details">
    Paste the SSO URL and certificate from your identity provider.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Sign out, then sign back in with your provider credentials to confirm it works.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  SSO is available on Teams and Enterprise plans.
</Callout>

## Assign roles and permissions

Control what each person can do by assigning one of these roles.

| Role    | Can create events | Can manage team settings | Can view all reports |
|---------|-------------------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Owner   | Yes               | Yes                      | Yes                  |
| Admin   | Yes               | Yes                      | Yes                  |
| User    | Yes               | No                       | No                   |
| Viewer  | No                | No                       | Yes                  |

To change a role, go to Team members, find the person, and select a new role from the dropdown.

## Set up SCIM provisioning

Automate adding and removing users through your identity provider.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate the SCIM token">
    In Admin settings, open Provisioning and create a new token.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the token to your provider">
    Copy the token and paste it into your identity provider's SCIM settings.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Map user attributes">
    Match email and name fields between Calendly and your provider.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Expandable title="What happens when I remove a user in my identity provider?">
  The user loses access to Calendly within minutes and all scheduled events stay intact.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I require SSO for every team member?">
  Yes. Turn on the "Enforce SSO" toggle in Admin settings. Members without SSO credentials will be prompted to set it up on next sign-in.

</Expandable>
