You need clear answers on how Calendly protects scheduling data and meets regulatory requirements. This page shows the controls that keep your information secure.

## Data Encryption

Calendly encrypts data both in transit and at rest.

- All traffic uses TLS 1.2 or higher.
- Stored data uses AES-256 encryption.

These measures apply automatically. No setup is required on your side.

## Compliance Certifications

Calendly holds the following certifications and attestations.

| Certification | Scope | Renewal |
|---------------|-------|---------|
| SOC 2 Type II | Security, availability, confidentiality | Annual |
| GDPR | Data processing for EU users | Ongoing |
| CCPA | California consumer privacy rights | Ongoing |

You can request the latest SOC 2 report from the support team.

## Data Residency

Calendly stores data in the United States by default. Enterprise customers can request data residency in the European Union or other supported regions.

<Expandable title="How do I request EU data residency?">
  Contact your account manager or support with your workspace ID. The team reviews the request and applies the change within 30 days.

</Expandable>

<Callout kind="info">
  Data residency changes apply only to new data after the update. Existing data remains in its original location.

</Callout>
