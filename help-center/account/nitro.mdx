## Discord Nitro gives you premium chat features and server boosts

This page shows the available perks, explains the subscription tiers, and walks you through managing your plan or boosting a server.

## Nitro perks at a glance

| Perk                  | Nitro Basic | Nitro          |
|-----------------------|-------------|----------------|
| Larger file uploads   | 50 MB       | 100 MB         |
| Custom emojis         | Yes         | Yes            |
| Animated avatar       | No          | Yes            |
| Server boosts         | 1           | 2              |
| HD streaming          | No          | Yes            |

## Subscribe to Nitro

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Discord settings">
    Click your profile picture in the bottom left, then select **Nitro**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your tier">
    Pick Nitro Basic or Nitro and review the monthly or yearly price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm payment">
    Enter your payment details and complete the purchase.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Yearly plans save you money compared with paying month to month.
</Callout>

## Manage your subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to subscription settings">
    Open **User Settings** and choose **Subscriptions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update or cancel">
    Select **Manage** next to your Nitro plan to change payment method, switch tiers, or cancel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Boost a server

Server boosting unlocks perks for everyone in that server. You receive two boosts with a Nitro subscription.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the server menu">
    Right-click the server icon and choose **Server Boost**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply your boosts">
    Select how many boosts to add and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<Expandable title="Can I cancel anytime?">
  Yes. You keep Nitro benefits until the end of the paid period.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Do boosts stay if I cancel?">
  No. Boosts end when your subscription ends, and the server loses those levels.
</Expandable>
