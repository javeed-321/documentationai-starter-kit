## Support Channels

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I reach support by chat?" default-open="true">
    Open your Shopify admin and click the chat icon in the bottom right. A support advisor responds quickly during business hours.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I get help by email?">
    Submit a request from the Help section in your admin. You receive a reply within one business day.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Priority Support

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Who qualifies for priority support?" default-open="true">
    Merchants on Shopify Plus receive dedicated priority support with faster response times and a named contact.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I upgrade for priority help?">
    Visit your plan settings in the admin and choose Shopify Plus. You gain immediate access to priority channels.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Community Forums

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where can I ask other merchants for advice?" default-open="true">
    Post your question in the Shopify Community forums. Experienced users and Shopify staff often reply within hours.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Are forum answers reliable?">
    Forum responses come from verified merchants and staff. Check the accepted answer for the most accurate fix.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Partner Directory

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I find a Shopify expert?" default-open="true">
    Search the Partner Directory on the Shopify website. Filter by expertise, location, and reviews to match your needs.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What can partners help with?">
    Partners assist with store setup, custom themes, marketing, and advanced integrations when you need hands-on help.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
