Need help with your Zendesk account or billing? This page shows how to contact support and submit tickets that reach the right team quickly.

## Support plans

Your plan determines response times and available channels. Choose the one that matches your needs.

<Expandable title="Essential">
Standard support with responses within one business day.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Professional">
Priority handling and access to phone support.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Enterprise">
Fastest response times plus dedicated account management.
</Expandable>

## Submit a ticket

<Steps>
<Step title="Sign in to your account">
Open Zendesk and sign in with your admin credentials.
</Step>

<Step title="Go to the Help menu">
Select Help, then choose Contact Support.
</Step>

<Step title="Fill out the form">
Describe your account or billing question and attach any relevant screenshots or invoices.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Include your account ID and plan type so the team can route your ticket correctly.
</Callout>

## Priority handling

Support reviews every ticket and assigns a priority level.

| Priority | When to use                  | Typical first response |
|----------|------------------------------|------------------------|
| High     | Billing issues blocking work | 1 hour                 |
| Normal   | General account questions    | 4 hours                |
| Low      | Feature requests or feedback | 1 business day         |

## Still stuck?

Submit a ticket directly from your Zendesk account using the steps above.
