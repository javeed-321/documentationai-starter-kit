If you need to review your current subscription, track usage, or change plans, this page shows you exactly how to handle billing in Intercom.

## Pricing Tiers

Intercom offers four tiers. Choose the one that matches your team size and support volume.

| Tier    | Monthly price | Key limits                     | Best for                  |
| ------- | ------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Starter | $0            | 10k conversations              | Small teams testing Fin   |
| Growth  | $65           | 50k conversations              | Growing support teams     |
| Scale   | $130          | 150k conversations             | High-volume helpdesks     |
| Custom  | Contact sales | Unlimited conversations        | Enterprise needs          |

## Usage Metrics

Intercom measures usage by resolved conversations and active seats. You can view your current numbers in the billing dashboard at any time.

- Go to Settings > Billing.
- The overview shows conversations used this month and remaining allowance.

## Upgrading Your Plan

Follow these steps to move to a higher tier.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing settings">
    Sign in and navigate to Settings > Billing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select new tier">
    Click Upgrade and choose the plan that fits your volume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm payment">
    Update your card details if needed, then click Confirm upgrade.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your new limits apply immediately after confirmation.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Upgrades are prorated. You pay only for the remaining days in the current cycle.
</Callout>

## Common Questions

<Expandable title="Where can I download past invoices?">
  Open Settings > Billing > Invoices. Click any invoice to download the PDF.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What happens if I exceed my conversation limit?">
  Intercom sends an alert at 80 percent usage. You can upgrade or wait until the next cycle.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I downgrade later?">
  Yes. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.
</Expandable>
