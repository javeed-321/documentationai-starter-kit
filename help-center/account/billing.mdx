Having trouble with payments, Nitro renewals, or refunds on Discord? This page shows you how to fix the most common billing issues.

## Accepted Payment Methods

Discord accepts these options for Nitro and server boosts:

| Method          | Notes                              |
|-----------------|------------------------------------|
| Credit or debit card | Visa, Mastercard, American Express |
| PayPal          | Linked account required            |
| Google Pay      | Android and web only               |
| Apple Pay       | iOS and web only                   |

## Cancel a Subscription

Follow these steps to stop future charges.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open User Settings">
    Click your profile picture in the bottom left, then select Billing.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your plan">
    Locate the Nitro or boost subscription you want to cancel.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel">
    Click Cancel Subscription and confirm. Your access continues until the end of the paid period.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Canceling does not issue an immediate refund. See the refunds section below.
</Callout>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Expandable title="How do I update my payment method?">
  Go to User Settings > Billing, select the subscription, then choose Edit Payment Method. Enter your new details and save.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="When does Nitro renew?">
  Nitro renews automatically on the date shown in your Billing settings. You receive an email reminder three days before renewal.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I get a refund?">
  Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase if you have not used the subscription benefits. Contact support with your order number to request one.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Why was I charged twice?">
  This usually happens when a renewal processes before a previous payment clears. Check your Billing history and reach out if the duplicate charge remains after 48 hours.

</Expandable>
