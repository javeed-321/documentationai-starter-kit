## Plan Comparison

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="What plans does Shopify offer?" default-open="true">

Shopify provides four main plans to match different business stages. Starter works for simple online sales. Basic adds core store tools. Shopify unlocks more sales channels. Advanced includes advanced reporting and lower fees.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="How do I pick the right plan?">

Start with your monthly sales volume and needed features. Review the comparison table on the pricing page. Upgrade later takes only a few clicks.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Transaction Fees

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="What are Shopify transaction fees?" default-open="true">

Fees apply when you use Shopify Payments. They range from 2.9 percent plus 30 cents on the Starter plan down to 2.4 percent plus 30 cents on Advanced. External gateways add extra fees.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Can I avoid transaction fees?">

Yes. Switch to Shopify Payments on any paid plan. Fees drop further with annual billing.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Billing Cycle

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How does billing work?" default-open="true">

You choose monthly or annual payment at signup. Annual plans save about 25 percent. Your card is charged on the same date each period.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="Where can I see my invoice?">

Log in to your account and visit Settings then Billing. Download past invoices or update your payment method there.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Downgrade Process

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="How do I downgrade my plan?" default-open="true">

Go to Settings then Plan. Select the lower plan and confirm. Changes take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

</Expandable>
<Expandable title="What happens to my data after downgrade?">

Your store stays live. Some advanced features turn off, but all your products and orders remain safe.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
