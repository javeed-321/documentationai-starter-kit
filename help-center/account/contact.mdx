If you need help with your account or run into a technical issue, this page shows the fastest ways to reach the Intercom team.

## Support channels

Use the table below to choose the best option.

| Channel       | Best for                        | How to reach it                          |
|---------------|---------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| In-app chat   | Quick questions and urgent help | Open the Messenger in your workspace     |
| Email         | Detailed account issues         | Send a message to support@intercom.com   |
| Help Center   | Self-service articles           | Search directly from the dashboard       |

## How to submit a request

Follow these steps to get a response.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Messenger">
    Click the chat icon in the bottom right of your Intercom dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your issue">
    Type a clear summary and include any error messages or screenshots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and track">
    Send the message. Check your email for updates and replies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Response times

<Expandable title="What are the typical response SLAs?">
  Most messages receive a first reply within a few hours during business days. Complex issues may take longer.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I request a new feature?">
  Use the in-app chat and mention it is a feature request. The team reviews all suggestions.
</Expandable>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Include your workspace ID when you email support to speed up help.
</Callout>

Still stuck? Reply to your open support thread or start a new chat in the app.
