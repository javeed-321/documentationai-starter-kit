## Creating a Support Ticket

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I open a support ticket?" default-open="true">
    Sign in to your Dropbox account, go to the Help Center, and select Contact support. Choose your issue type, add details, and submit. You will receive a confirmation email with your ticket number.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What details should I include in my ticket?">
    Describe the problem clearly, note your device and app version, and attach any error messages or screenshots. This helps our team respond faster.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How long does it take to get a reply?">
    Most tickets receive a first response within one business day. Complex issues may take longer depending on volume.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Community Forums

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Where can I ask other Dropbox users for help?" default-open="true">
    Visit the Dropbox Community forums at community.dropbox.com. Search existing threads or post your question in the relevant category.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Are the forums moderated?">
    Yes. Dropbox staff and experienced users monitor posts and provide official guidance when needed.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Priority Support Plans

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What priority support options exist?" default-open="true">
    Dropbox Business and Enterprise plans include priority email support with faster response times and dedicated account assistance.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I upgrade for priority support?">
    Open the Admin console, select Billing, and choose a plan that includes priority support. Changes take effect immediately.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
