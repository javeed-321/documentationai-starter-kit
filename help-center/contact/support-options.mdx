You need fast answers for your Zendesk account. This page shows your support options and how to reach the right team.

## Support Plans

Zendesk offers three paid support plans. Choose the one that matches your needs.

| Plan       | Response Time | Channels          | Best For                  |
|------------|---------------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Essential  | 24 hours      | Email             | Basic account questions   |
| Professional | 4 hours     | Email, chat       | Growing teams             |
| Enterprise | 1 hour        | Email, chat, phone| Mission-critical setups   |

## How to Submit a Ticket

Follow these steps to open a support request.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to your account">
    Go to the Zendesk Admin Center and log in with your admin credentials.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Help menu">
    Click your profile icon, then select Get Support.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill out the form">
    Choose your plan level, describe the issue, and attach any relevant screenshots or logs.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and track">
    Click Submit. Check your email for the ticket number and updates.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Questions

<Expandable title="How do I change my support plan?">
  Sign in to Admin Center, go to Billing, and select Change Plan. Your new plan starts at the next billing cycle.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What counts as an escalation?">
  An escalation is a request that needs faster handling because it affects live operations. Select the Escalation checkbox when you submit the ticket and explain the business impact.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Where can I find community help?">
  Visit the Zendesk Community forums to search existing threads or post your question. Many agents and customers share solutions there.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I manage account access for my team?">
  In Admin Center, go to People and Roles. Add users, assign roles, and set permissions in one place.

</Expandable>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Keep your ticket number handy when you follow up. It speeds up every conversation with support.

</Callout>
