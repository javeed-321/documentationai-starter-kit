If you need help with Intercom, this page shows the fastest ways to reach support for priority issues, feature requests, or account assistance.

## Quick contact options

| Method       | Best for                  | Response time     |
|--------------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| In-app chat  | Most questions            | Usually minutes   |
| Email        | Detailed account issues   | Within one business day |
| Scheduled call | Complex setup or training | Booked in advance |

## Start in-app chat

Open Intercom in your workspace and click the chat icon in the lower right. Type your question to connect with a support agent right away.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Include your workspace ID and a short description of what you see on screen for faster help.
</Callout>

## Priority support

<Expandable title="How do I request priority support?">
  Priority support is available for urgent production issues.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open chat">
      Start an in-app chat and select "Urgent issue".
    </Step>
    <Step title="Provide details">
      Describe the impact on your team and include error messages or screenshots.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Receive callback">
      An agent will reply in chat or schedule a call within the hour.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

</Expandable>

## Feature requests

<Expandable title="How do I submit a feature request?">
  Use the in-app chat and choose the "Feature request" option. Describe the use case and any current workaround. The product team reviews all requests and may follow up for more details.
</Expandable>

## Account assistance

<Expandable title="How do I get help with billing or users?">
  For account changes, email support@intercom.com with your workspace ID and the specific request. An agent will confirm the change and any next steps.
</Expandable>

Still stuck? Reply in the in-app chat or email support@intercom.com.
