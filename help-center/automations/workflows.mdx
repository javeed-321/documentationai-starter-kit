## Set up automated journeys in Mailchimp

Mailchimp automations let you send the right message at the right time. This page shows you how to create welcome series, abandoned cart, and re-engagement flows.

## Welcome series

A welcome series greets new subscribers and introduces your brand.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create the automation">
  Go to Automations and select Create automation.

  Choose the Welcome new subscribers template.

</Step>
<Step title="Set the trigger">
  Use the "Subscribes to your audience" trigger.

  Add a delay of one day before the first email.

</Step>
<Step title="Add conditions and actions">
  Insert a condition to check if the subscriber opened the first email.

  Send a follow-up email only to those who opened it.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Keep the first email short and focused on one benefit.
</Callout>

## Abandoned cart

Recover sales from visitors who add items but do not complete checkout.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create the automation">
  Start a new automation and pick the Abandoned cart template.

</Step>
<Step title="Set the trigger">
  Choose the "Abandons cart" trigger.

  Set it to run 30 minutes after abandonment.

</Step>
<Step title="Add conditions and actions">
  Add a condition for cart value over $50.

  Send a discount code only to carts that meet this rule.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Re-engagement flows

Bring back subscribers who have not opened emails recently.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create the automation">
  Select the Re-engage subscribers template.

</Step>
<Step title="Set the trigger">
  Use the "Has not opened email in 90 days" trigger.

</Step>
<Step title="Add conditions and actions">
  Send a "We miss you" email first.

  Remove subscribers who do not engage after the second email.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Common trigger options

| Trigger                  | Best used for             |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Subscribes to audience   | Welcome series            |
| Abandons cart            | Abandoned cart recovery   |
| Has not opened email     | Re-engagement             |
| Makes a purchase         | Post-purchase follow-up   |

<Expandable title="Can I edit an automation after it starts?">
  Yes. Pause the automation, make your changes, then resume it.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How many emails should a series contain?">
  Start with three emails and test open rates before adding more.
</Expandable>
