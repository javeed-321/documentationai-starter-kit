## Recent updates

<Update label="2024-10-15" description="2024-10-15" tags={["feature", "api"]}>

- Added support for instant bank verification in the Payment Intents API.
- Introduced new Radar rules for crypto on-ramp transactions.
- Deprecated legacy tokenization endpoints scheduled for removal in 2025.

</Update>
<Update label="2024-09-20" description="2024-09-20" tags={["feature"]}>

- Launched beta for embedded checkout with custom branding options.
- Expanded billing support for usage-based pricing in 12 additional currencies.

</Update>
<Update label="2024-08-12" description="2024-08-12" tags={["bugfix", "api"]}>

- Fixed an issue with webhook signature verification for Connect accounts.
- Resolved timeout errors during high-volume subscription updates.

</Update>
