## Overview

ShopifyMax helps you launch and scale your store with reliable tools that cover every stage of commerce. You build your site, manage orders, track inventory, automate shipping, and review performance data in one place. Sidekick AI assists with tasks while point-of-sale and B2B features extend your reach.

## Website Builder and Themes

Start with the website builder to create a store that matches your brand. Choose from ready themes or customize layouts directly. Sidekick suggests product descriptions and page sections as you work.
