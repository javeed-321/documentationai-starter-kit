## Overview

Linear organizes work around fast, keyboard-driven workflows. You move issues through structured planning tools that keep teams aligned without heavy process overhead. The core features—cycles, roadmaps, triage, and search—form the foundation for sprint planning, milestone tracking, and issue discovery.

## Cycles

Cycles replace traditional sprints with flexible timeboxes. You assign issues to a cycle, set a duration, and track progress in real time. Linear automatically rolls unfinished work to the next cycle when you choose.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Planning" icon="calendar" href="#cycles">
    Break work into two- or four-week cycles and see capacity at a glance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automation" icon="zap" href="#cycles">
    Configure rules that move issues between cycles based on status changes.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Roadmaps and milestones

Roadmap views turn issues and projects into visual timelines. You group work by quarter or release and connect milestones to specific dates or cycle boundaries.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Timeline" icon="bar-chart">
    Drag milestones across quarters to adjust delivery dates instantly.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="List" icon="list">
    Switch to a prioritized list view when you need to reorder initiatives quickly.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Triage and labels

The triage inbox collects new issues from integrations and teammates. You review, label, and route them without leaving the keyboard.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Use the `T` key to open triage from anywhere in the app.
</Callout>

Labels combine with custom properties so you can filter by priority, team, or customer impact in one click.

## Filters, views, and commands

Linear's command bar (`Cmd/Ctrl+K`) gives you instant access to saved views and complex filters. You build queries such as `assignee:me status:in-progress cycle:current` and save them for daily use.

<CodeGroup tabs="Search,Filter">
```text
assignee:me AND label:bug cycle:current
```
```text
project:Roadmap status:backlog
```
</CodeGroup>

## Best practices

<Expandable title="Keyboard-first workflow" default-open="false">

  Learn the most common commands first: `C` to create an issue, `K` and `J` to navigate the list, and `/` to open the command bar. Muscle memory reduces context switching.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Cycle hygiene" default-open="false">

  Keep cycles under two weeks when possible. Review carry-over issues at the end of each cycle and decide whether to move, split, or close them.

</Expandable>
