## Overview

Canva provides a complete set of design tools that help you create professional visuals without specialized software. The editor forms the core workspace where every project begins. From there you access layers, photo tools, video features, and multiple publishing paths. These capabilities work together so you can move from idea to finished design in one place.

## Editor Interface and Layers

The editor displays a clean canvas with a left sidebar for elements and a right panel for properties. Layers appear in a stack that you reorder by dragging. You can group items, lock them, or adjust opacity directly on the layer list.

## Photo Editing and Background Remover

Upload any image and open the Edit photo menu. Adjust brightness, contrast, and saturation with sliders. The background remover isolates subjects automatically and places them on a transparent layer.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web" icon="monitor">
    Click Edit photo then select Background remover. The tool processes in seconds.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mobile" icon="smartphone">
    Tap the photo, choose Edit, then tap Remove background.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Video and Animation Tools

Add video clips directly to the timeline. Trim, split, and reorder clips without leaving the editor. Apply animations to text and images by selecting an element and choosing from the Animate menu.

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Upload clip] --> B[Trim & split]
    B --> C[Add animation]

```

## Print Products and Presentations

Design once and publish to multiple formats. Choose print products such as posters or business cards from the Publish menu. For presentations, switch to the Slides view and reorder pages instantly.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Always check the bleed and margin settings before sending a design to print.
</Callout>

## Examples

A marketing team created a product launch deck in Canva by combining layered graphics, a short product video, and print-ready flyers. They used the background remover on lifestyle photos and applied consistent animations across all slides.

## Best Practices

- Keep the layers panel tidy by naming groups as you build.
- Preview animations at different speeds before final export.
- Use the print preview to confirm colors and crop marks.

These tools give you full control while staying simple enough for quick iterations.
