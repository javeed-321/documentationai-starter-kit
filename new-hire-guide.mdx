## Overview

This guide walks you through setting up your Notion workspace on day one. Follow the checklist in order. Complete each item before moving to the next.

## First Week Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your workspace and pages">
    Create a personal workspace. Duplicate the team template for notes and projects. Organize pages into folders for quick access.

    Ask your manager for the shared workspace link.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure AI Writing Assistant and agents">
    Enable AI features in workspace settings. Test the Writing Assistant on a sample page. Create one custom agent for task routing.

    Review the available agent templates before building your own.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect calendars and mail">
    Link your calendar from the Connections menu. Add Notion Mail to route messages into dedicated pages.

    Verify sync by creating a test event and email thread.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review security and permissions">
    Check page permissions with your team lead. Enable two-factor authentication in account settings.

    Confirm you have view access only on sensitive project spaces.

  </Step>
</Steps>
