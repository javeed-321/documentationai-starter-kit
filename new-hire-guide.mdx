## Overview

The company knowledge base lives in Notion. Every new hire receives access on their first day. You use the workspace to locate policies, record project notes, and update shared pages.

## First Week Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account" icon="user-plus">
    Accept the invitation email sent by your manager. Sign in with your work email and set a strong password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the onboarding page" icon="search">
    Open the sidebar and search for "New Hire Onboarding". Pin this page to your workspace for quick reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review core pages" icon="book-open">
    Read the company handbook and the engineering handbook. Add any missing information you notice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first edit" icon="edit-3">
    Update the team directory page with your photo and role details. Follow the template exactly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Documentation Standards

<Callout kind="info">
  Always use the approved page template. Add a last-updated date at the top of every page you edit. Request review from your manager before publishing changes that affect other teams.
</Callout>

## Who to Contact

Reach out to your onboarding buddy for questions about page structure. Contact the workspace admin for permission issues. Use the `#notion-help` channel in Slack for general questions.
