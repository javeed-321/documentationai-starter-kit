## Overview

This guide walks you through the basic steps to get set up in Notion on your first day. Follow the sections in order to create your account, join the right workspaces, and save the pages you will use daily.

## First Week Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account" icon="user-plus">
    Your manager sends an email invitation to the address listed in your offer letter. Open the link and complete the sign-up form. After you log in, go to Settings and update your profile with your full name, job title, and a photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join team workspaces" icon="users">
    Open the workspace switcher in the top-left corner. Accept the invites for the workspaces that match your team assignments. If a workspace does not appear, ask your manager for the correct link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bookmark key pages" icon="bookmark">
    Navigate to the Company Home page. Use the three-dot menu on the page title and select Add to bookmarks. Repeat this step for the Team Templates page and the IT Support page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review templates" icon="copy">
    Open the Templates gallery inside your personal workspace. Duplicate the Onboarding Checklist template and the Meeting Notes template into your private area so you can edit them later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Contacts

Reach out to these people if you run into setup problems:

- Your direct manager for workspace invites and team-specific pages.
- The IT administrator listed on the IT Support page for account or permission errors.
- The People Ops coordinator if your profile information needs correction after the first day.
