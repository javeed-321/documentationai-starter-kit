## Purpose

This template provides a standard structure for documenting recurring business processes in Notion. Use it to ensure every process includes clear steps, assigned owners, and a defined review schedule. The goal is to make processes repeatable and auditable across teams.

## Steps

Follow these steps when creating or updating a process page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the process scope" icon="target">
    Write a short description of what the process covers and its intended outcome. List any inputs required and outputs produced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Break the work into ordered steps" icon="list">
    Number each action in sequence. Include decision points and any conditional branches. Keep each step actionable and specific.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign owners and due dates" icon="user">
    Attach a Notion person property to every step. Add a date property for expected completion or next occurrence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link related resources" icon="link">
    Embed or link to supporting Notion pages, databases, templates, or external files. Use inline @mentions for quick navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add version information" icon="git-branch">
    Record the current version number and last updated date in a dedicated property. Note major changes in the page comments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Owners

Assign a primary owner who maintains the process documentation and ensures steps remain current. List secondary owners for each major step when multiple teams participate. Update owner fields whenever personnel change.

## Review Cadence

Schedule a review at least once per quarter. The primary owner initiates the review and records the outcome in the page history. Mark the review date and any resulting updates in the version property.
