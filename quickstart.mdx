## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">

Create a free Notion account before you begin. You will need an email address to sign up and enable AI features.

</Callout>

## Set up your workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a workspace" icon="users">
    Go to the Notion homepage and sign up or log in. Choose a workspace name and select a team or personal plan.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Notion AI" icon="zap">
    Open workspace settings, navigate to the AI section, and toggle Notion AI on. Accept the terms to activate Meeting Notes, Enterprise Search, and the Writing Assistant.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your first page" icon="book-open">
    Click New page in the sidebar. Add a title, then start typing or use the AI command menu with the slash key to generate content.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Minimal working example

<CodeGroup tabs="Page Content,Agent Command">
```markdown
# Project Kickoff

## Goals
- Launch new feature
- Align team priorities

Use Notion AI to expand this outline.
```

```text
Type /ask in any page and enter:
"Summarize last week's meeting notes and create action items"
```
</CodeGroup>
