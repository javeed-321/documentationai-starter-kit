## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">
  You need a valid email address to create a Linear account. No credit card is required to start.
</Callout>

## Create your workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up" icon="user-plus">
    Go to the Linear sign-up page and enter your email. Verify the code sent to your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your workspace" icon="edit">
    Choose a workspace name such as `Acme Engineering`. This becomes your team URL at `https://linear.app/acme-engineering`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your team" icon="users">
    Add your first team name, for example `Platform`. You can create additional teams later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite your team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send invites" icon="mail">
    From workspace settings, enter teammate emails and choose their role (Admin, Member, or Guest).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm access" icon="check-circle">
    Each person receives an email with a direct link to join. They can sign in with Google, GitHub, or email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create your first issues

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the composer" icon="plus">
    Press `C` anywhere in the app or click the `New issue` button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill details" icon="edit-3">
    Add a title, description, assignee, and labels. Use markdown for formatting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and track" icon="save">
    Press `Enter` to create the issue. It appears in your team's inbox and backlog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keyboard navigation basics

Linear is designed for speed. Try these core shortcuts after signing in:

- `G` then `I` — Go to Inbox
- `G` then `B` — Go to Backlog
- `C` — Create new issue
- `K` and `J` — Move between issues
- `/` — Focus search

<Callout kind="tip">
  Press `?` at any time to view the full shortcut list.
</Callout>
