## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">
  You need a valid email address and basic details about your products and business. No credit card is required to start.
</Callout>

## Create your account and store

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up" icon="user">
    Visit the signup page and enter your email address. Choose a store name that reflects your brand.

    After verification, you land on the dashboard at `https://dashboard.example.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your store" icon="settings">
    Select a built-in theme and set your currency and shipping zones. This takes under two minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add products and payments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your first product" icon="package">
    Enter a product title, description, price, and upload an image. Save the product to make it live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect payments" icon="credit-card">
    Enable the built-in payment provider and enter test credentials. Real transactions activate after verification.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure checkout and domain

<CodeGroup tabs="Configuration,Preview">
```json
{
  "checkout": {
    "theme": "default",
    "fields": ["email", "address", "payment"]
  },
  "domain": "yourstore.example.com"
}
```

```bash
# Preview your store
curl https://yourstore.example.com
```
</CodeGroup>
