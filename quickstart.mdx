<Callout kind="info">
Create a free Canva account before you begin. Sign up at `https://www.canva.com` with your email or a Google account.
</Callout>

## Account setup and teams

Sign in to Canva and select your account type. Choose the free plan to start immediately. Invite teammates by entering their email addresses in the Teams section of your account settings. This lets you collaborate on designs without sharing login details.

## Create your first design

Follow these steps to finish a basic design in under five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a template" icon="layout">
    Click Create a design on the home screen. Browse templates by category or search for a specific size such as social media post or presentation. Select one to open the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add and edit text" icon="type">
    Click any text placeholder. Replace the sample words with your own content. Use the top toolbar to change font, size, and color. Drag the text box to reposition it on the canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert images and elements" icon="image">
    Click the Uploads tab on the left sidebar to add your own photos. Or open the Elements tab and drag shapes, icons, or illustrations onto the page. Resize and rotate items with the corner handles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download or share" icon="share">
    Click the Share button in the top right. Choose Download to export a PNG, PDF, or JPG file. Or select a share link to send the design to teammates or post directly to social platforms.
  </Step>
</Steps>
