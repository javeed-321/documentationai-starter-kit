MetricsFlow continues to expand its real-time analytics capabilities with a focus on performance and integrations.

<Board>
  <BoardColumn title="Planned">
    <BoardCard title="Advanced Time-Series Functions" description="Add support for moving averages, exponential smoothing, and percentile calculations over streaming windows." />
    <BoardCard title="New Data Source Connectors" description="Introduce native connectors for Apache Kafka and Amazon Kinesis to ingest high-volume event streams." />
    <BoardCard title="Enterprise Security Features" description="Implement role-based access control, audit logging, and encryption for data in transit and at rest." />
  </BoardColumn>
  <BoardColumn title="In Progress">
    <BoardCard title="Enhanced Windowing Capabilities" description="Extend tumbling and sliding window support with custom trigger conditions and late-event handling." />
    <BoardCard title="Expanded Sink Integrations" description="Build direct connectors to Snowflake and Google BigQuery for seamless result delivery." />
  </BoardColumn>
  <BoardColumn title="Completed">
    <BoardCard title="Core Query Engine" description="Delivered support for complex aggregations across more than one billion records with sub-50ms latency." />
    <BoardCard title="Initial Data Connectors" description="Released stable integrations for PostgreSQL, Amazon S3, and HTTP endpoints processing over 50000 events per second." />
  </BoardColumn>
</Board>
