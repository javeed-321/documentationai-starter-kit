## Welcome

Notion is an AI-powered workspace that brings notes, docs, projects, and apps into one place. You use built-in agents, enterprise search, and meeting notes to keep work moving without switching tools.

It is designed to reduce tool sprawl for teams of any size. You can centralize knowledge, automate repetitive tasks, and connect the systems you already use.
